Getting Your Bearings

Where Is Double Oak, Texas?

Double Oak is a small incorporated town in southern Denton County, tucked between Flower Mound to the north and east, Highland Village to the west, and Lantana and Bartonville to the north. It sits just off FM 407, between I-35E to the west and US-377 to the east, roughly 30 miles northwest of Downtown Dallas and about 25 miles northeast of Downtown Fort Worth.

What sets Double Oak apart is its scale and feel. With around 3,000 residents on larger, often acre-plus lots, it is a quiet, almost rural-feeling residential community rather than a commercial hub. There is no town center full of shops here. Day-to-day errands, dining, and big-box shopping happen a few minutes away in Flower Mound, Highland Village, or Lantana. The tradeoff for that quiet is real driving, so this is a place that rewards families who value space and calm over walk-out-the-door convenience.

Plan on driving for nearly everything, including groceries and dining in neighboring towns.
Education

Lewisville ISD: Schools Deep Dive

Schools are a big part of why families choose Double Oak. Most of the town is served by Lewisville ISD, which earns an A overall on Niche, but there is an important wrinkle here: Double Oak is a split-district town. The majority of addresses fall in Lewisville ISD, while a western portion of town is zoned to Denton ISD (Adkins Elementary, Harpool Middle School, and Guyer High School). Which district your home is in depends on the exact address, so this is one to verify carefully before you buy.

Family Life

What Life Actually Looks Like in Double Oak

Youth Sports Nearby

Double Oak does not field its own large league system, but families plug into the deep Flower Mound and Highland Village youth sports scene minutes away: soccer, baseball, softball, basketball, and more through area associations and Lewisville ISD athletics.

Space & the Outdoors

The big draw here is room to breathe: large lots, mature trees, and quiet streets. Many homes back to greenbelt or open space, and Lewisville Lake and the trails of Flower Mound and Highland Village are a short drive for paddling, fishing, and biking.

Shopping & Dining (Nearby)

Double Oak itself has essentially no retail or restaurants, this is a residential town. The upside is how close the options are: Flower Mound's River Walk and Highland Village's Shops at Highland Village put grocery, dining, and shopping within five to ten minutes.

Small-Town Calendar

As a town of about 3,000, Double Oak runs at a small-town pace. Civic life centers on Town Hall, neighborhood gatherings, and the broader Flower Mound area events calendar, where larger seasonal festivals and farmers markets are held.

Acreage Close to Amenities

What makes Double Oak distinctive is the combination most DFW suburbs cannot offer: genuine acreage and privacy that still sits minutes from top schools, healthcare, and shopping. You get the quiet of the country without being truly far from anything.

Tight-Knit & Established

With near-total owner occupancy and long-tenured residents, Double Oak feels settled rather than transient. Neighbors know each other, and the pace is calm, a good fit for families who want roots and quiet over constant activity.

The honest tradeoff: Double Oak has almost no in-town commercial life, no walkable downtown, no restaurants, and no public transit. If you want to walk to coffee or have nightlife around the corner, this is not that town. What you trade convenience for is space, quiet, and privacy, with everyday amenities a short drive away in Flower Mound, Highland Village, and Lantana.
Getting Outside

Recreation, Walkability & City Amenities

Double Oak is a highly car-dependent town, that's the honest starting point. There is no DART or DCTA rail, no real public transit, and very little within walking distance. What the town offers instead is space at home and quick access to the strong parks, trails, and lake recreation in neighboring Flower Mound, Highland Village, and Lantana.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Within its own borders Double Oak keeps things simple, with a small town park and a great deal of private green space on residential lots. The real recreation map for most families extends just past the town line, where Flower Mound, Highland Village, and Lewisville Lake offer trails, sports complexes, and water access. The cards below mix the in-town option with the nearby parks Double Oak families use most.

Double Oak Town Park

The town's own neighborhood park, a quiet, low-key green space for local families. Modest by design, in keeping with Double Oak's small-town character. Larger park needs are met in the neighboring communities below.

Lewisville Lake (Highland Village)

Just minutes west, Lewisville Lake offers boating, fishing, paddling, and lakeside parks. Highland Village's shoreline parks and Pilot Knoll Park are popular with Double Oak families for weekends on the water.

Flower Mound Trail System

Flower Mound maintains an extensive multi-use trail network connecting parks, neighborhoods, and the lake. It is the go-to for Double Oak families who want to walk, jog, or bike beyond their own quiet streets.

The Grand Park / Lantana Amenities

The nearby Lantana master-planned community and Flower Mound's larger parks add splash pads, sports fields, playgrounds, and community pools, all a short drive from Double Oak.

Lake Park (Lewisville)

A large lakeside park on Lewisville Lake with campsites, trails, a marina, and open shoreline. A favorite for bigger family outings within about 15 minutes of Double Oak.

Private Green Space at Home

Worth naming honestly: in Double Oak, much of your outdoor life happens on your own property. Acre-plus lots mean room for play sets, gardens, and pets, the backyard does a lot of the work other suburbs hand to public parks.

Honest Assessment

Pros & Cons of Living in Double Oak

I won't sell you on Double Oak. I'll give you the real picture so you can decide if it's right for your family.

What Double Oak Does Well

  • Space and privacy, large, often acre-plus lots and quiet streets give you room most DFW suburbs cannot, while still sitting minutes from amenities.
  • Strong schools, the town is served by Lewisville ISD (Niche A overall, A+ college prep), with the western portion in Denton ISD's Guyer feeder.
  • Quiet, established feel, near-total owner occupancy and long-tenured neighbors make Double Oak feel settled and calm rather than transient.
  • Close to everything you need, Flower Mound, Highland Village, and Lantana put grocery, dining, healthcare, and shopping within a short drive.
  • Lake and trail access nearby, Lewisville Lake and the Flower Mound and Highland Village trail systems are minutes away for weekend recreation.
  • Low town tax rate, the Town of Double Oak's own tax rate is modest; ISD remains the largest line on the bill.
  • No state income tax, meaningful savings, especially for high earners from CA, NY, or IL.

What to Weigh Carefully

  • Up-market pricing, the market skews high, with a median near $970K and most sales above $700K. True entry-level homes are rare here.
  • Very thin inventory, only about 39 homes sold in the past year, so the right home may take patience and timing.
  • Split school districts, most of town is Lewisville ISD, but a western portion is Denton ISD. You must verify the exact address before buying.
  • Almost no in-town amenities, no restaurants, no walkable shopping, and a single small park. You drive for nearly everything.
  • Car-dependent, no DART or DCTA rail and no meaningful public transit. A personal vehicle is essential, and most households need two.
  • Higher property tax bill in dollars, the rate is typical for Denton County, but on up-market home values the annual dollar total runs high. Always file your homestead exemption.
