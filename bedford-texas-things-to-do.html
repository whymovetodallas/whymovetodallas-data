What Bedford Families Actually Do Here

Bedford sits in the heart of Hurst-Euless-Bedford, one of the most established family communities in the Mid-Cities. The City's investment in Generations Park and The Center YMCA gives residents access to resort-quality recreation without the north Frisco price tag. If you're relocating to the H.E.B. area, Bedford offers a compact, connected community where parks, youth sports, and neighborhood events are genuinely part of everyday life. See the full Bedford relocation guide →

The Center YMCA

Opened in 2023 inside Generations Park, The Center delivers indoor pools, a gym, courts, fitness classes, and kids' programming in one spot. Nothing else in the H.E.B. area matches it under a single roof.

Deep Sports Roots

BESA soccer and Tri-Cities baseball have served H.E.B. families for decades. Meadow Park Athletic Complex puts dedicated diamonds and soccer fields within reach of nearly every Bedford neighborhood.

Year-Round Community Events

From spring concerts at Old Bedford School to the Labor Day Beats and Eats Fest drawing 15,000 neighbors, Bedford's free event calendar gives families a reason to be outside together all year.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Bedford maintains 12 parks and green spaces across the city, ranging from the flagship Generations Park complex to neighborhood pocket parks within walking distance of most homes. The Linear Trails corridor adds 32 acres of paved, traffic-free path through northeast Bedford.

Generations Park at Boys Ranch

Bedford's flagship park at 2801 Forest Ridge Dr. Home to The Center YMCA, an outdoor water park with lazy river, two slides, and splash pad (seasonal), ballfields, a lake-loop walking path, 9-hole disc golf, playground, and event lawn. Shaded pavilions available for rent. Best for families with kids of any age.

Water Park Disc Golf Trails Playground Picnic Areas

Meadow Park Athletic Complex

The sports hub of Bedford at 3200 Meadow Park Dr. Baseball and softball diamonds host Tri-Cities league games, while dedicated soccer fields handle BESA matches. The Bark Park sits on the same grounds, with separate fenced areas for small and large dogs. Restrooms on site.

Baseball Soccer Dog Park Picnic Areas

Central Park

Anchored by Roy Savage Pool and a full basketball court, this centrally located park at 1201 Central Dr. is the go-to for summer swimming and pickup hoops. Shaded playground, swings, grill stations, and a rentable pavilion. Restrooms on site. Good for after-school hangouts.

Pool Basketball Playground Pavilion

Stormie Jones Park

A versatile neighborhood park at 2700 Brasher Lane with a playground, soccer fields, volleyball court, walking path, and pavilion. Water fountains and restrooms make it practical for longer visits. Good mix for families with kids at different activity levels.

Playground Soccer Volleyball Trails

Harris Ryals Park

Two playgrounds plus a walking path make this 2700 Cummings Dr. spot a strong option for families with young kids. Covered pavilion for birthday parties and picnics. Both regular and infant swings. Water fountains throughout. Stroller-friendly surfaces.

Playground (x2) Walking Path Pavilion

Old Bedford School

Home to the Sparkling Ice Musical Pocket Playground, an accessible and inclusive play space at 2400 School Lane that serves all abilities. Walking path circles the grounds. This is also where Bedford hosts its free spring concerts and community celebrations.

Inclusive Play Walking Path Event Lawn

Brook Hollow Park

A compact neighborhood park at 752 Rankin Dr. with a playground, basketball goal, picnic areas, and water fountains. Good for quick after-school stops. Low crowds on weekday mornings, shaded playground areas work well for younger kids.

Playground Basketball Picnic Areas

Bark Park (at Meadow Park)

Bedford's off-leash dog park at 3200 Meadow Park Dr. features separate fenced areas for small and large dogs, covered picnic tables, water fountains, and agility equipment. Closed Wednesday mornings for maintenance. Dog agility courses keep dogs busy while kids watch.

Off-Leash Agility Equipment Small/Large Dog Areas

Trail System Highlight

Linear Trails, Bedford's 32-acre paved trail corridor, follows the Oncor power easement through northeast Bedford from Woodpark Lane to Forest Ridge Drive. The flat concrete surface is stroller-friendly and safe for young bikers. Fitness stations with instructions appear at intervals, and a playspace for children with physical challenges sits along the route. Solar lighting extends usable hours into the evening.

Relocation Tip

Start your Bedford exploration at Generations Park. The lake-loop walking path gives you a real feel for the scale of the complex, and you can see The Center's outdoor water park from the trail before committing to a YMCA membership. If the water park is busy on a Saturday, Roy Savage Pool at Central Park is a quieter option for laps and open swim.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Bedford's Parks and Recreation department partners with four established H.E.B.-area youth sports associations, each with fields and facilities embedded in the city's parks. Games are played locally, and registration happens through the individual associations.

Soccer

League: Bedford Euless Soccer Association (BESA)
Ages: 5 to 18
Season: Fall (Aug–Nov) and Spring (Feb–May)
Fields: Meadow Park Athletic Complex, Bedford
BESA runs two seasons per year with both competitive and recreational tracks. Registration for fall opens each summer.

Baseball & Softball

League: Tri-Cities Baseball & Softball
Ages: 3 to 14 (boys and girls)
Season: Spring and Fall
Fields: Generations Park Baseball Complex
Tri-Cities offers T-ball through select-level play, with recreation leagues open to all skill levels. Call 817-952-3393 for registration.

Football & Cheerleading

League: Mid-Cities Peewee Football & Cheerleading
Ages: Youth division, H.E.B. area residents
Season: Fall (Aug–Nov)
Dedicated to providing fun, safe, character-building athletics for H.E.B.-area kids. Contact 817-282-2390 for current division info.

Basketball

League: Mid-Cities Basketball Association (MCBBA)
Ages: Grades 1 through 8
Season: Winter
Courts: H.E.B. area gyms
Non-profit, volunteer-run organization serving the Hurst-Euless-Bedford corridor. Contact 817-354-6208 for registration.

Disc Golf

League: City of Bedford (free, open access)
Ages: All ages
Season: Year-round
Course: Generations Park at Boys Ranch
9-hole course designed for beginners, with wooded areas, water hazards, and holes from 150 to 320 feet. Alternate tees for the water-crossing hole on #9.

Featured Sports Facility

Generations Park Baseball Complex anchors the south end of Generations Park at Boys Ranch. Lighted diamonds, concessions, and restrooms support Tri-Cities league games and area tournament play. The same complex hosts the mid-week practice schedule for most H.E.B. baseball families.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Bedford's Mid-Cities location gives families solid local options and access to major regional draws within 20 to 30 minutes, without needing to fight Frisco traffic.

Movie Tavern at Central Park

Dine-in cinema at 2204 Airport Fwy operated by Marcus Theatres. Watch first-run films while ordering food and drinks to your seat. Reclining chairs and a full menu make this a step above a standard movie night, and it's a reliable rainy-day option for kids of all ages.

Generations Park Disc Golf Course

Free 9-hole disc golf course at Generations Park, beginner-friendly with wooded shots and five holes that play over water. Hole #9 has an alternate tee across the bridge for those not ready for the pond carry. Casual and accessible for all skill levels including teens.

The Center YMCA Outdoor Water Park

Seasonal outdoor water park at Generations Park featuring a lazy river, two waterslides, splash pad, play features, concessions, and reservable cabanas. Day passes available for non-members ($8 for residents, $12 for non-residents). Check ymcafw.org for the current season's opening dates.

North East Mall (Hurst, 5 min)

At 1.54 million square feet with 140+ stores, North East Mall in neighboring Hurst is the primary regional retail and entertainment hub for H.E.B. families. Dillard's, Macy's, and JCPenney anchor the center, with dining and entertainment options inside. 5 minutes from most Bedford addresses.

Day Trip from Bedford

Six Flags Over Texas (Arlington, about 20 minutes), the classic DFW thrill destination, is close enough for an afternoon trip without making it an all-day ordeal. Hurricane Harbor next door adds a water park option in summer. Families in Bedford also regularly make the run to the National Videogame Museum in Frisco (about 30 minutes north) for a rainy-day reset.

Family-Friendly Dining

Bedford's dining scene runs from casual Texas BBQ to international flavors, with most family-friendly spots concentrated along Airport Freeway and the Central Drive corridor. Post-practice dinner runs are easy here.

Pappadeaux Seafood Kitchen
Best for a special family dinner
Cajun-style seafood and Gulf classics at 2121 Airport Fwy. Full of flavor and atmosphere, best for kids who are past the chicken-nugget phase.
Texas Roadhouse
Best picky-eater friendly
Hand-cut steaks, free bucket of peanuts on arrival, and a kids' menu that actually works. Loud enough that a toddler meltdown goes unnoticed.
Saltgrass Steakhouse
Best classic Texas steak night
Texas institution at 1813 Airport Fwy. Scratch-made sides, bread baked daily, and the kind of steakhouse vibe that feels right after a long moving week.
Spring Creek Barbeque
Best quick dinner after practice
Cafeteria-style Texas BBQ at 1505 Airport Fwy. Order, pay, eat. Zero waiting. Kids point to what they want and dinner is done in 10 minutes.
Fazenda Gaucha Brazilian Steakhouse
Best with adventurous older kids
All-you-can-eat Brazilian rodizio at 405 E Pipeline Rd. Servers circulate with skewers of 15+ meats. Great for meat-lovers of all ages, but save it for when the kids are old enough to appreciate it.
Macarena Taco Shop and Margarita Bar
Best casual taco night
Creative tacos and a vibrant atmosphere on Harwood Rd. Busy on weekend evenings. Kid-friendly menu alongside solid margaritas for the adults.
On The Border Mexican Grill
Best Tex-Mex with kids' menu
Reliable Tex-Mex at 2500 Airport Fwy with a full kids' menu and big portions. Senior discount available. Good for extended-family dinners with mixed age groups.
Chili's Grill & Bar
Best weeknight standby
Bedford's classic weeknight fallback at 2400 Airport Fwy. Kids' menu staples, free drink refills, and no surprises. Senior discount available.
Lovely Day Cafe
Best weekend brunch
Local cafe at 1424 Airport Fwy with a warm atmosphere and scratch-made breakfast and lunch. A quieter alternative to the chain corridor when you want something more personal.
Buttermilk Cafe and Bakery
Best Saturday morning treat
Local bakery on Pipeline Rd with fresh pastries, baked goods, and cafe fare. The kind of place that makes Saturday morning feel like a real neighborhood moment.

Most family-friendly options cluster along Airport Freeway and the Central Drive corridor, both within a 5-minute drive of nearly any Bedford address. The Pipeline Road stretch adds more local flavor heading toward Euless.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Bedford's position in the Mid-Cities gives families access to one of DFW's largest regional malls within 5 minutes, plus a practical local retail corridor for everyday errands.

North East Mall

Regional Mall

At 1.54 million square feet in neighboring Hurst, North East Mall is the Mid-Cities' primary enclosed regional mall with 140+ stores: Dillard's, Macy's, JCPenney, Buckle, White House Black Market, and dozens more. Five minutes from most Bedford homes.

Best for: a full Saturday outing with older kids who want to browse, shop, and grab a meal without a two-hour drive to NorthPark.

The Shops at North East Mall

Lifestyle Center

The outdoor companion to North East Mall sits at the northeast corner of Loop 820 and Pipeline Rd. Specialty retailers, restaurants, and services with easier parking and an open-air feel.

Best for: combining a quick errand with lunch outside, or shopping with a stroller when the enclosed mall feels like too much.

Airport Freeway Corridor

Everyday Retail

Bedford's main retail strip along Airport Freeway covers the everyday bases: multiple grocery options, Target, Home Depot, Walgreens, and a dense cluster of restaurants. Everything within a 2-mile stretch.

Best for: weekly groceries, pharmacy runs, and quick weeknight errands when you don't want to leave the neighborhood.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Bedford runs a consistent calendar of free community events from spring through Labor Day, with Generations Park and Old Bedford School serving as the main venues. Live entertainment lineups are announced close to each event; check the city's events page for current year details.

Spring

Bedford in Bloom Concert Series

Free outdoor concert series held at Old Bedford School. The city books cover bands and tribute acts (check the official events page for this year's lineup). Bring lawn chairs and arrive early for a spot on the grass. Families fill the grounds for each performance.

June

Juneteenth Celebration

Bedford's annual Juneteenth celebration at Old Bedford School featuring performances, music, and storytelling. A free community event held each June that draws families from across the H.E.B. area for an evening of culture and community.

May–July

HEB Reads! Summer Reading Challenge

A collaboration between Bedford, Hurst, and Euless public libraries running May through July. Kids log reading minutes through the Beanstack app to earn prizes. STEAM workshops, games, and events run at Bedford Public Library throughout the summer to keep learning active between school years.

July 4

Bedford 4thFest

The city's Fourth of July celebration at Generations Park at Boys Ranch. Free live music (check city events page for this year's lineup), a kids' zone, food trucks, and a fireworks show at sundown. One of the most attended events on Bedford's calendar.

September

Bedford Beats and Eats Fest

Labor Day weekend free community festival at Generations Park. Live entertainment (check city events page for lineup), food trucks, a kids' zone, vendor market, and activities for all ages. Draws more than 15,000 attendees each year. One of the largest free events in the Mid-Cities.

Indoor Activities for Kids

When summer temps push past 105 degrees, Bedford's indoor options keep kids active and learning. The Center YMCA anchors the list, and the library's summer programming is better than most families expect.

The Center YMCA

Type: Recreation Center · Ages: All ages
Indoor pool with two waterslides, lap lanes, and current lane. Second-floor fitness mezzanine with indoor track. Double basketball court with pickleball and volleyball striping. YPlayhouse short-term child care. Fitness classes for teens and adults. Open Mon–Thu 5 AM–10 PM, Fri–Sun abbreviated hours. Memberships start at $62/month for families.

Bedford Public Library

Type: Library · Ages: All ages
Over 100,000 items including books, DVDs, audiobooks, and magazines. Open 7 days a week, 59 total hours. STEAM workshops run during the school year and summer. Summer Reading Challenge (May–July) lets kids earn prizes through the Beanstack app. Call 817-283-1521 or visit bedfordlibrary.org for current program calendar.

Movie Tavern at Central Park

Type: Dine-In Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
Marcus Theatres dine-in cinema at 2204 Airport Fwy. Reclining seats, full food and drink service to your seat, first-run films. A reliable rainy-day choice and a step up from a standard theater. Book in advance for weekend showings.

Urban Air Adventure Park (Euless)

Type: Trampoline and Adventure Park · Ages: 3+
About 5 minutes from Bedford in neighboring Euless. Indoor trampolines, climbing walls, obstacle courses, a dedicated toddler area, and more. Good for birthday parties and long summer afternoons when outdoor play is not an option.

GameTime (HEB area)

Type: Entertainment Center · Ages: All ages
Games, bowling, laser tag, and dining in the greater H.E.B. area. All-you-can-eat pizza available. A strong option for family group outings or birthday parties that need something beyond a park and a pizza place.

Roy Savage Pool at Central Park

Type: Public Pool · Ages: All ages
Seasonal outdoor pool at 1201 Central Dr. available to Bedford residents for lap swimming and open swim. A lower-key alternative to The Center's water park when you want something quieter. Check bedfordtx.gov/pool for current season schedule.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

When July hits 105 degrees, local families head to The Center YMCA first. The indoor pool runs until 10 PM on weeknights, so you can skip the worst heat and still get a full swim in after dinner. The YPlayhouse child care option means parents can work out while younger kids play. For a longer day out, the Movie Tavern at Central Park handles afternoon screenings with food, and the library's STEAM programs keep older kids engaged without turning it into screen time.
