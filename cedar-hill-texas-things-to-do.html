Cedar Hill stands apart from most DFW suburbs because of what's literally in the backyard: 1,826 acres of Cedar Hill State Park on Joe Pool Lake, 60-plus miles of city trails, and nature preserves that give outdoor families genuine room to explore without driving far.

What Cedar Hill Families Actually Do Here

Cedar Hill is the DFW suburb where the outdoors genuinely define daily life. With 52 parks and preserves, 60-plus miles of trails, and Cedar Hill State Park right at the city's edge, families here measure quality of life in trail miles and lake access rather than mall proximity. The city's five youth sports associations and a rich community events calendar round out the picture. See the full Cedar Hill relocation guide →

Cedar Hill State Park on Your Doorstep

Direct access to 1,826 acres on Joe Pool Lake with 15-plus miles of mountain bike trails, a swimming beach, two fishing jetties, and one of the largest campgrounds in the Texas state park system.

60-Plus Miles of City Trails

Cedar Hill maintains more than 60 miles of trails connecting parks, greenbelts, and neighborhoods, with named corridors like the Red Oak Creek Trail, Pleasant Run Trail, and the Mansfield Road Trail offering lake views.

Traditions Since 1937

Country Day on the Hill, held annually since 1937 in Historic Downtown Cedar Hill, is one of the oldest community festivals in DFW. Nine annual city events give families a packed community calendar year-round.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Cedar Hill maintains 52 parks and nature preserves within approximately 3,770 acres, with over 60 miles of trails connecting them. That figure includes Cedar Hill State Park (1,826 acres), the Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center (200-plus acres), and Cedar Mountain Nature Preserve (110 acres), making Cedar Hill one of the most trail-dense cities in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro.

Cedar Hill State Park

1,826 acres on Joe Pool Lake at 1570 FM 1382. Mountain biking on the DORBA Trail (15-plus miles of singletrack rated among the best urban mountain biking in Texas), a gravel swimming beach with picnic tables and playground, two lighted fishing jetties, and 350 developed campsites including full hookups. The Penn Farm Agricultural History Center offers self-guided tours daily. No lifeguards at the swimming beach; day-use and camping fees apply.

Mountain Biking Swimming Beach Fishing Camping History Center

Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center

More than 200 acres of limestone hills and creek beds at 1206 W. FM 1382, managed by the National Audubon Society. Guided nature programs, bird walks, and school field trips make it Cedar Hill's go-to destination for science-curious kids. Trails through the canyon are stroller-friendly in sections. Hours and program schedules posted at dogwood.audubon.org.

Nature Programs Bird Watching Canyon Trails

Lester Lorch Nature Preserve

An 86-acre preserve at 1823 Texas Plume Road and home to two full 18-hole disc golf courses (Beaver and Coyote courses) plus natural trails. A local favorite for disc golf players of all skill levels; the wooded terrain makes both courses legitimately challenging. Good shade coverage; restrooms on-site.

Disc Golf (2 Courses) Nature Trails Shaded

Valley Ridge Park

At 2850 Park Ridge Drive, Valley Ridge Park is one of the larger active parks in the city with open fields, playground equipment, and access to the trail network. The Music in the Park Concert Series takes place here in the summer. Stroller-accessible paths; good restroom availability.

Open Fields Playground Concert Venue Trail Access

Alan E. Sims Recreation Center Park

At 310 E. Parkerville Road, this park surrounds the city's main recreation center and provides a starting point for the nearby trail network. Athletic fields for league play, walking paths, and the proximity to the rec center's indoor amenities make it a multi-use stop for family outings.

Rec Center Access Athletic Fields Trail Access

Cedar Mountain Nature Preserve

A 110-acre nature preserve at 1300 W. FM 1382 offering one of the few significant natural ridge lines in the DFW metro. Wildlife habitat with native cedar and limestone outcroppings. Best for families with older kids comfortable on unimproved terrain; a quieter alternative to the more popular Dogwood Canyon.

Wildlife Habitat Ridge Views Natural Terrain

Virginia Weaver Park

At 631 Somerset Drive, Virginia Weaver Park is home to The Lagoon Aquatic Center, a community gathering point every summer with a zero-depth entry pool, lazy river, slide, and children's play features. Good shade coverage around the pool facility; restrooms and concessions on-site.

Aquatics (The Lagoon) Playground Picnic Areas

Calabria Nature Preserve

At 750 W. FM 1382, a quiet natural area with native limestone terrain and cedar woodland. Low-traffic and peaceful; good for families who want a shorter nature walk without the crowds of the state park. Free access; no formal amenities on-site.

Passive Park Native Woodland Free Access

Trail System Highlight

DORBA Trail at Cedar Hill State Park, 15-plus miles of singletrack crisscrossing 1,200 acres of limestone hills on Joe Pool Lake. Ranked among the top urban mountain biking destinations in Texas; also open to hikers. Trail closes when wet, so check conditions before heading out. Trailhead parking at the state park entrance on FM 1382.

Relocation Tip

Start with a morning at Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center to get a feel for Cedar Hill's nature identity, then drive five minutes to Cedar Hill State Park for a swim at the Joe Pool Lake beach in the afternoon. That combination in a single day tells you more about what family life here actually looks like than any neighborhood tour.

The city also offers disc golf on three courses and tennis lessons through the Alan E. Sims Recreation Center, rounding out the options for individual sport development.

Balls provided; bring your own racket. Contact the rec center at (972) 291-5130 for current session schedules.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Cedar Hill's entertainment identity is anchored in outdoor attractions rather than commercial venues, but the city has solid indoor options close by and is well-positioned for day trips to nearby Grand Prairie and Dallas attractions.

Cedar Hill State Park

Nature / State Park · 1,826 acres on Joe Pool Lake with mountain biking, swimming, fishing, camping, and the Penn Farm Agricultural History Center. The most substantial outdoor family destination in Southwest Dallas County. Entrance fees apply; annual Texas State Parks Pass pays for itself quickly for active families.

Dogwood Canyon Audubon Center

Nature Center / Education · 200-plus acres with guided nature programs, bird walks, and school field trips. The best science education experience available within Cedar Hill city limits. Programs rotate seasonally; check dogwood.audubon.org for current offerings and admission.

Kids Empire Cedar Hill

Indoor Playground · A large indoor play facility with climbing structures, slides, and dedicated toddler zones. Designed for kids ages 1-12; handles birthday parties. Climate-controlled and a good rain-day or summer-heat option for families with younger children.

The Lagoon Aquatic Center

Aquatic Facility · Nearly 10,000 square feet of water surface at Virginia Weaver Park (631 Somerset Dr), including a zero-depth entry pool, lap pool, lazy river, three-turn slide, and children's play features. 2026 season: May 30 through August 9 (open swim Tuesday through Sunday, 12pm-5pm). Check the city's Lagoon page for current-year hours and extended pop-up season dates.

Alan E. Sims Recreation Center

Recreation Center · At 310 E. Parkerville Road, the city's main rec center offers fitness equipment, a walking track, a basketball court, recreation classes, and a private event space. Youth basketball league games are held here in winter. Summer camps available for school-age children.

Cinemark 14 Cedar Hill

Movie Theater · 14 screens with RealD 3D and digital projection; stadium seating; standard concessions. A reliable weeknight family option within Cedar Hill, with matinee pricing that keeps it accessible for regular outings.

Day Trip from Cedar Hill

Joe Pool Lake extends beyond the state park boundary and is accessible from multiple public areas. For families who want more water recreation, the lake offers boating and sailing. The Loyd Park in Grand Prairie (about 15 minutes away) offers an additional 616-acre lake park with a swim beach, boat ramps, and camping as a complementary option to Cedar Hill State Park.

Family-Friendly Dining

Cedar Hill's dining scene is concentrated along Uptown Boulevard, Highway 67 near Hillside Village, and the broader FM 1382 corridor. The options lean toward casual and comfort-food, which fits well with a city where families tend to spend their days active outdoors.

Sam's Pizza
Best pizza night
New York-style pizza widely regarded as the top family restaurant in Cedar Hill. Large pies, solid pasta, generous portions. The community's reliable go-to for a simple, satisfying family dinner.
Babe's Chicken Dinner House
Best comfort food spread
Texas comfort food with fried chicken, cornbread, and family-style sides served in generous quantities. A Cedar Hill institution for extended-family dinners and post-game celebrations.
Dick's Uptown Cafe
Best local diner breakfast
A local diner with a loyal following for breakfasts and lunches. Known for Denver omelets and solid fried food at fair prices. The kind of place where the staff knows regulars by name.
Cotton Patch Cafe
Best weeknight family dinner
Texas comfort food with fried chicken salads, chicken-fried steak, and scratch sides. Consistent and affordable; the kind of family restaurant where no one argues about where to go.
Veracruz Cafe
Best Mexican for adventurous eaters
Mexican and Latin cuisine with a lively atmosphere and a more creative menu than most Tex-Mex in the area. Known for chimichanga dishes and the Mexican egg rolls. Popular and can get loud on weekends.
Akita Sushi & Hibachi
Best special occasion dinner
Japanese with hibachi and sushi options; one of the better-reviewed restaurants in Cedar Hill. Good for a slightly more special family dinner when the standard comfort-food rotation needs a break.
Saltgrass Steak House
Best steak night
Reliable Texas steakhouse with a full menu of steaks, sides, and a kids selection that handles multiple age groups in one outing. The safe choice when the group wants steak without a downtown drive.
Los Tapatios
Best casual Tex-Mex
Friendly Mexican restaurant with good chips, salsa, and traditional dishes. Clean, efficient, and popular with families looking for affordable Tex-Mex without a long wait.
Plato Loco
Best quick Mexican lunch
A Cedar Hill Mexican spot with a solid chip-and-hot-sauce combination and straight-forward Tex-Mex dishes. Fast, affordable, and well-suited for a quick lunch before an afternoon at the state park.
Red Robin Gourmet Burgers
Best for picky eaters
Bottomless steak fries, a wide burger menu, and a kids menu that satisfies even selective palates. Loud and welcoming; a reliable fallback after active days when the group just wants something easy.

Most sit-down dining is concentrated near Uptown Boulevard, the Highway 67 corridor near Hillside Village, and FM 1382 heading toward Cedar Hill State Park.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Cedar Hill's anchor shopping destination is Hillside Village, an open-air lifestyle center designed to be walkable and family-friendly, complemented by the everyday retail along the Highway 67 and FM 1382 corridors.

Hillside Village

Open-Air Lifestyle Center

A 615,000-square-foot open-air center at US Hwy 67 and Pleasant Run Road, designed in a new urbanism style to be pedestrian-friendly. Anchored by Dillard's, Dick's Sporting Goods, and Barnes and Noble, with 70-plus merchants including H&M, James Avery, and Grimaldi's Pizzeria. A Village Green with a performance stage, an interactive water fountain, a 2,500-square-foot children's play area, and an oversized chess and checkers board make it genuinely family-friendly.

Best for: a Saturday family outing that combines shopping, lunch, and the kids' play area without driving to a traditional enclosed mall.

Highway 67 / FM 1382 Corridor

Everyday Retail

The primary retail spine running through Cedar Hill, with grocery anchors, national chains, pharmacies, and service businesses. Most household needs can be handled along this corridor without leaving the city, and the route toward Cedar Hill State Park passes through this area naturally.

Best for: routine weekly errands and grabbing dinner or coffee before or after a park visit.

Uptown Boulevard District

Mixed Retail

A retail and commercial corridor near the Cedar Hill government center at 285 Uptown Boulevard. Home to Cinemark 14, additional restaurants, and service retail. The Cedar Hill Chamber of Commerce area serves as a secondary gathering point for local businesses and dining.

Best for: quick errands in the city center, a movie outing, or exploring the local business community.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Cedar Hill runs nine signature city-organized events throughout the year, from a spring block party to a December holiday celebration. Most are low or no cost and open to residents and non-residents alike.

March

Cedar Hill Block Party

The city's spring kickoff event, a community gathering designed to connect neighbors before the busy outdoor season begins. Family-friendly activities and entertainment; check the official city calendar at cedarhilltx.com for the confirmed date each year.

June

Music in the Park Concert Series

Free outdoor concerts held at Valley Ridge Park, bringing live music to the community through the summer. Family-friendly programming across genres; with live entertainment (check the city events calendar for this year's lineup). One of the most attended summer traditions in Cedar Hill.

June

Kid Fish Tournament

A youth fishing tournament organized by Cedar Hill Parks and Recreation, giving kids a structured and supervised introduction to fishing. Poles and bait often provided for participants; check cedarhilltx.com for registration and location details each year.

June

Juneteenth Celebration

An annual community celebration of Juneteenth with family activities, entertainment, and programming recognizing the significance of the holiday. Held annually; check the city events calendar for confirmed location and activities each year.

September

BSW Hispanic Heritage Festival

A community celebration of Hispanic heritage during National Hispanic Heritage Month. Cultural programming, food, and entertainment make it a multi-generational family event. Check the city's events calendar at cedarhilltx.com for the confirmed date and venue each year.

October

Country Day on the Hill

Held annually since 1937, this is one of the oldest community festivals in DFW. Typically on the second Saturday of October in Historic Downtown Cedar Hill, the event features a parade, a pageant, live music, and the Cook Off Games. Thousands of visitors attend each year; a genuine Cedar Hill institution.

October

Scare on the Square

A Halloween celebration in Historic Downtown Cedar Hill featuring trunk-or-treating, a parade, creepy stories, and family entertainment. A popular alternative to neighborhood trick-or-treating for families with younger kids who want a supervised, community-run event.

December

Holiday on the Hill

The city's signature holiday celebration featuring a kids' activity zone, live performances, an ice rink, photos with Santa, a snow maze, and the official lighting of the Cedar Hill community tree. Typically held in Historic Downtown Cedar Hill; check the city events calendar for the confirmed date each December.

Full calendar: Cedar Hill official events page

Indoor Activities for Kids

Cedar Hill has a solid set of indoor options for July and August heat days, with additional venues accessible within a short drive. The city's recreation center and library also run year-round programming that fills indoor time with something more useful than passive entertainment.

Kids Empire Cedar Hill

Type: Indoor Playground · Ages: Ages 1-12
Multi-level climbing structures, slides, and a dedicated toddler zone in a climate-controlled facility. Handles birthday parties well. Best for ages 2-10; older kids tend to outgrow it quickly.

Cinemark 14 Cedar Hill

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
14 screens with RealD 3D; first-run films at standard pricing with matinee discounts. A reliable weeknight and weekend option that doesn't require a drive to a neighboring city.

Alan E. Sims Recreation Center

Type: Recreation Center · Ages: All ages
Fitness equipment, a walking track, an indoor basketball court, recreation classes, and summer camps. Year-round programming for kids and adults; membership rates available for Cedar Hill residents. 310 E. Parkerville Road.

Cedar Hill Public Library

Type: Library · Ages: All ages; children's and teen sections
Year-round programming including storytimes, summer reading challenges, craft events, and after-school activities. The library is a quiet, consistent indoor option that's genuinely useful for families new to the area building their local routine.

The Lagoon Aquatic Center

Type: Aquatic Facility (seasonal outdoor) · Ages: All ages
Lazy river, zero-depth entry, slide, and children's play features at Virginia Weaver Park. Open seasonally May through August (2026: May 30 - August 9, Tuesday-Sunday); check cedarhilltx.com/3029 for current-year schedule and extended season announcements.

Hillside Village Children's Play Area

Type: Outdoor / Covered Play Area · Ages: Under 10
A 2,500-square-foot children's play area in the Village Green at Hillside Village shopping center, plus an interactive water fountain that kids use as a splash feature in warmer months. Free; pairs naturally with errands or lunch at the center.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

Cedar Hill's typical summer heat strategy is a morning at Cedar Hill State Park before 10am (lake breeze and shaded DORBA trails), The Lagoon for midday pool time through August, and Cinemark 14 or Kids Empire as the backup when neither parent wants to leave air conditioning. The Audubon Center's shaded canyon trails are also cooler than open-field parks and worth a July morning visit.
