What Trophy Club Families Actually Do Here

Trophy Club's identity is built around access to nature and a community that shows up for its events. The 1,000-acre Trophy Club Park on Grapevine Lake handles the outdoor recreation side with hiking trails, mountain biking, ATV tracks, disc golf, kayaking, and fishing. Harmony Park handles the team sports side with 10 soccer fields, pickleball courts, and two playgrounds. And the Parks and Recreation calendar handles the community side with 10+ signature events per year that bring the neighborhood together throughout every season. See the full Trophy Club relocation guide.

Trophy Club Park

At 1,000 acres on Grapevine Lake, this is one of the most diverse municipal parks in the DFW area: hiking trails, dedicated mountain biking loops, 7 miles of ATV and motocross motorized trails, an 18-hole disc golf course, a boat launch, kayaking, fishing, equestrian trails, and a birdwatching corridor. Few suburbs this size park on a lake and let kids ride ATVs legally within city limits.

Harmony Park's Sports Complex

Thirteen acres with 10 soccer fields, two age-specific playgrounds, six pickleball courts, two tennis courts, two bocce ball courts, and a 1.8-mile paved loop trail. This is where Northwest ISD families' weekend sports schedules play out spring through fall, and where the Greater Northwest Soccer Association runs its youth leagues.

10+ Signature Events Per Year

Trophy Club's Parks and Recreation department runs one of the most active community event calendars in the area for a town of 15,000: from the EGGstravaganza in spring to the Fourth of July Parade of Patriots and fireworks to the Harvest Festival and Winter Wonder Lights in December. The calendar is a real community glue.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Trophy Club manages over 1,000 acres of parks, and the standout is Trophy Club Park on Grapevine Lake: a rare large-scale wilderness park inside a residential suburb where families can hike, bike, kayak, disc golf, or let older kids ride ATVs without leaving the city. The town's trail network connects the park system to neighborhoods throughout.

Trophy Club Park

The city's 1,000-acre flagship sits at the northwestern tip of Grapevine Lake. Named hiking trails include the Great Blue Heron Trail (2.8 miles, moderate with excellent birdwatching), the Adventure Loop (0.5 miles, easy and family-friendly), and the Sunrise Bay Trail (0.6 miles with lake views). The Equestrian Trail runs 6 miles parallel to the lake. Mountain bike loops, 7 miles of designated ATV and motocross trails, an 18-hole disc golf course, a boat ramp, kayak launch, and fishing access complete the picture. Admission fee applies.

Hiking Mountain Biking ATV/Motocross Disc Golf Lake Access Kayaking

Harmony Park

Thirteen acres at the end of Indian Creek Drive that function as Trophy Club's community sports and recreation hub. Ten soccer fields host GNWSA league play throughout spring and fall. Two age-appropriate playgrounds (ages 2-5 and 5-12), six pickleball courts, two tennis courts, two bocce courts, and one basketball court give non-soccer families plenty of options. The 1.8-mile paved loop pathway connects all facilities; shade from mature trees near the playgrounds. Good restrooms and parking throughout.

Soccer Fields Playground Pickleball Tennis Trail Loop

Independence Park

Athletic complex with multiple fields used for TCRYBA baseball, youth softball, and competitive tournament play. The park is split into Independence East and West fields, with tournament lighting allowing play Friday and Saturday evenings until 11 p.m. and Sundays until 10:30 p.m. The Veterans Memorial at Independence Park honors the community's military service members and is a meaningful stop for veteran families who choose Trophy Club.

Baseball/Softball Lighted Fields Veterans Memorial

Community Pool & Splash Pad

Trophy Club's resident-only aquatic facility includes a swimming pool, splash pad, and hosts the Tritons Swim Team for competitive youth swimming. The Dive-In Movie nights at the pool (confirmed date: Luca on July 24, 2026) have become a summer institution. Swim lessons are also offered. Residents: check trophyclub.org for seasonal hours and programming schedules.

Splash Pad Pool Swim Team Residents Only

Freedom Dog Park

Off-leash dog park with fenced areas for both large and small dogs. A good community gathering spot in a town where neighbors genuinely know each other. The dog park culture in Trophy Club reflects the broader neighborhood feel: families stop, talk, and exchange school recommendations while their dogs run.

Off-Leash Area Fenced Dog Park

Trophy Club Country Club Grounds

The Trophy Club Country Club's two 18-hole golf courses, tennis and pickleball facilities, and club pool add a private-club dimension to recreation for residents who join. The club dates to the founding of Trophy Club as a golf-centric community in the 1970s and remains central to the social fabric of the older residential sections of the town. Junior golf programs available.

Golf Tennis Country Club Members Only

City Trail Network

Trophy Club's city trail system connects parks, neighborhoods, and the town center through a network of sidewalks, greenway paths, and dedicated multi-use trails. The system weaves through the medians and open spaces that define TC's residential character. Combined with the trails inside Trophy Club Park, residents can log miles of varied terrain without leaving the city.

Multi-Use Trails Greenways Neighborhood Paths

Trail System Highlight

Great Blue Heron Trail at Trophy Club Park runs 2.8 miles through wooded lakeside terrain with reliable birdwatching. The trail opens near the park entrance and eventually circles Grapevine Lake's edge with views of the water through the tree cover. Named for the great blue herons that nest in the area, this is the flagship hiking route and the reason birding families specifically choose Trophy Club Park over other DFW area parks.

Relocation Tip

If you're scouting Trophy Club, go to Trophy Club Park on a Saturday morning before 10 a.m. The hiking trails are quiet before the ATV crowd arrives, the lake views are at their best in morning light, and you'll likely spot herons at the water's edge. Then drive to Harmony Park to see what 10 soccer fields in use actually looks like on a league Saturday. Those two stops tell you everything about why TC families say "the outdoors here is different."

Harmony Park's 10 soccer fields and Independence Park's lighted baseball and softball fields are the primary venues. Featured Sports Facility

Harmony Park at the end of Indian Creek Drive is the primary athletic hub for Trophy Club. The 10 GNWSA soccer fields are the single largest concentration of youth soccer fields within the town, and on a typical Saturday in April or October the park functions as a stadium without walls: parents lining multiple fields, kids cycling between warmups and games, and the pace of a functioning youth sports community in full operation. The 1.8-mile paved loop around the park gives parents a way to log miles while kids practice.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Trophy Club's entertainment leans heavily outdoor, which suits most of its residents who specifically chose the town for its natural character. When families need indoor options, the proximity to Keller, Fort Worth, and the broader Metroplex fills the gap quickly.

Trophy Club Park ATV Tracks

Outdoor adventure park · Seven miles of designated ATV and motocross motorized trails make Trophy Club Park one of the few municipal parks in DFW where families with UTVs, ATVs, and dirt bikes can ride legally within city limits. The trail system is separated from hiking areas and uses a fee system. Contact the park at 682-237-2993 for current OHV fees and trail access rules before visiting with young riders.

Disc Golf at Trophy Club Park

Outdoor recreation · An 18-hole disc golf course inside Trophy Club Park runs through wooded terrain with lake views on several holes. Disc golf is one of the best low-cost family sports because the learning curve is short enough for kids 8 and up to have fun in a first session, and the course at TCP is more scenic than most DFW municipal courses.

Kayaking & Fishing on Grapevine Lake

Lake recreation · The boat ramp and kayak launch at Trophy Club Park give families direct access to Grapevine Lake. Fishing from the shores and kayaking the lake's coves are low-cost activities that work well for school-age kids. The lake setting is genuinely peaceful on weekday mornings before the motorized boats arrive. A fishing license is required for anglers 17 and older.

Movie Night in the Park

Community outdoor cinema · Trophy Club Parks and Recreation hosts seasonal outdoor movie nights in the park. The June 12, 2026 screening of Jumanji and the July 24, 2026 Dive-In Movie (Luca at the community pool) are the confirmed 2026 dates from the city calendar. Families bring blankets and lawn chairs; check trophyclub.org for the full schedule each season.

Trophy Club Country Club

Private club · Two 18-hole golf courses, a pool, tennis and pickleball courts, and dining for members. Junior golf programs make this a draw for golf families. The country club has been part of Trophy Club's identity since the town was founded as a golf community, and club membership remains part of the social fabric for a significant portion of longtime residents.

Birding at Trophy Club Park

Nature activity · The Great Blue Heron Trail and the lake corridor at Trophy Club Park are known in the DFW birding community for consistent great blue heron sightings and diverse migratory species along Grapevine Lake. A guided birding walk here makes for a different kind of family morning than a playground, and it's a memorable experience for kids who have never seen a heron up close.

Day Trip from Trophy Club

Keller's Bear Creek Community Complex and Grapevine Mills are the closest day-trip options from Trophy Club. Keller's family activities scene is about 10 minutes south and worth exploring for families who want to compare the two before deciding on a suburb. Grapevine Mills (SEA LIFE Aquarium + Legoland Discovery Center) is about 15 minutes east via Hwy 114 and is the top family-entertainment day trip from TC. For a longer outing, the Fort Worth Cultural District (Kimbell Art Museum, Fort Worth Museum of Science and History, Fort Worth Zoo) is about 25 minutes southwest.

Family-Friendly Dining

Trophy Club's dining scene is concentrated at Trophy Club Town Center and along the city's main commercial corridor. The selection is more limited than larger neighboring cities, but the town's official restaurant guide lists more than 25 dining options covering a solid range of cuisines.

Anamia's Tex Mex
Best Tex-Mex with kids
Fresh Tex-Mex at Trophy Club Town Center with sizzling fajitas, homemade tamales, a vibrant atmosphere, and a kids' menu. One of the city's newer restaurant additions that quickly became a neighborhood staple.
Hutchin's BBQ
Best Texas BBQ night
Award-winning Texas BBQ chain with a reputation for peppered brisket and ribs. Hutchin's is one of the most respected regional BBQ names in DFW; the Trophy Club location brings the quality close to home for TC residents.
Vinny's Italian Restaurant
Best picky-eater pasta night
Italian restaurant on the town's dining list with a classic pasta and entree menu. A reliable family option for nights when the household's palate splits between pizza and pasta.
Fish & Knife Japanese Cuisine
Best for sushi night out
Japanese restaurant offering sushi, hibachi-style dishes, and Asian entrees. A welcome addition to Trophy Club's dining roster that handles both the quick sushi craving and a more sit-down Japanese dinner.
Cristina's Mexican Restaurant
Best quick family Mexican
Mexican restaurant on the town's dining list; a local option for families who want Mexican food outside of the Tex-Mex format. Good for weeknight dinners when TC's limited restaurant supply is working in your favor rather than against you.
Komrai Thai
Best for adventurous palates
Thai restaurant serving traditional Thai dishes. One of Trophy Club's ethnic dining options that reflects the community's growing diversity. Good for families with older kids who are branching out from comfort-food staples.
Dan's Bagels
Best weekend breakfast
Bagel shop and breakfast option that doubles as a neighborhood gathering spot on weekend mornings. The kind of casual breakfast place that Trophy Club's community feel requires: somewhere families run into each other before heading to Harmony Park for a soccer game.
HG SPLY Co.
Best health-conscious lunch
Health-focused restaurant and bar concept serving globally inspired food with a focus on whole ingredients. A natural fit for a city where residents spend significant time outdoors and eat accordingly.
Pastaza
Best quick dinner after practice
On the town's official dining list; a dining option for quick meals that fits the after-sports-practice schedule that dominates Trophy Club families' weeknight routines in fall and spring.
Big Chill Ice Cream
Best post-game treat
Local ice cream shop on the town's dining list and the natural reward stop after a soccer game at Harmony Park or a Saturday outing at Trophy Club Park. The kind of small-city amenity that makes TC feel complete.

Most Trophy Club dining concentrates at Trophy Club Town Center and along the main commercial corridor near Trophy Wood Drive. For a broader restaurant selection, Keller and Southlake (10-15 minutes south) significantly expand the options without a long drive.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Trophy Club is deliberately small-scale on the retail side, which is part of what families choose it for. The town center handles everyday needs, and Keller and Southlake absorb the demand for larger shopping destinations.

Trophy Club Town Center

Town Center

Mixed-use development anchoring Trophy Club's main commercial area with retail space surrounding a park, restaurant patio dining, an Aloft Hotel, apartments, and townhomes. The development's park-central design reflects TC's outdoor orientation: retail wraps a green space rather than a parking lot.

Best for: everyday dining and quick errands without leaving Trophy Club. The green space makes it a pleasant lunch stop.

Keller Town Center (10 min south)

Adjacent City

Keller's retail corridor along Keller Pkwy and Hwy 377 provides big-box retail, grocery stores, specialty shops, and additional dining options about 10 minutes south. TC residents who need Target, Home Depot, or a broader grocery selection typically make the short drive to Keller.

Best for: big-box retail, weekly groceries, and any shopping that requires more than Trophy Club Town Center offers.

Southlake Town Square (15 min east)

Full Shopping Day

Southlake Town Square about 15 minutes east along Hwy 114 is the closest full-scale lifestyle retail and dining destination. National retail anchors, restaurants, and an outdoor main-street shopping environment that TC residents use for occasions that require more than the town center can provide.

Best for: a full family shopping outing combining retail, dining, and entertainment in one destination.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Trophy Club's Parks and Recreation department runs more than 10 Signature Community Events per year for a town of 15,000 people. The Fourth of July celebration in particular has a reputation across the northern DFW suburbs as one of the best small-city Independence Day events in the area.

Spring

EGGstravaganza

Trophy Club's Easter egg hunt and spring family event. One of the most-attended events on the TC calendar; the community turnout reflects the town's genuine neighborhood feel. Check trophyclub.org each spring for the confirmed date and location.

Spring

Touch A Truck

Kids interact with fire trucks, construction equipment, police vehicles, and other large vehicles in a hands-on event that draws heavy attendance from families with children ages 2-10. One of those events that sounds simple but produces the kind of kid-sized wonder that makes a lasting memory.

June 12, 2026

Movie Night in the Park

Outdoor screening of Jumanji at the park. Families bring blankets, lawn chairs, and picnics. One of the more casual TC events with a friendly, neighborhood-gathering atmosphere.

July 4, 2026

Fourth of July Celebration

Trophy Club's signature event: a full-day celebration including the Patriot 5K and Fun Run, the Parade of Patriots through town, community flag planting, and a fireworks show that draws families from across the northern DFW suburbs. With live entertainment (check the city's events page each year for lineup details).

July 24, 2026

Dive-In Movie: Luca

Outdoor movie screening at the Trophy Club Community Pool (resident access). Watching a movie from the water on a July evening is one of those town-specific experiences that makes TC families genuinely enthusiastic about where they live.

September 12, 2026

Doggie Dive In

Dogs take over the community pool at the end of swim season for an off-leash swim event. A beloved TC tradition that signals the unofficial end of summer and fills the pool with canine chaos to the delight of residents and their dogs.

Fall

Harvest Festival & Pet Fest

Two fall events that round out the TC outdoor season: the Harvest Festival as a community autumn celebration, and Pet Fest as a pet-focused community event. Both reflect the town's small-city character and the Parks and Recreation department's commitment to filling every season with reasons to gather.

December

Winter Wonder Lights

Trophy Club's signature December holiday event with seasonal lighting and community activities. Combined with Wreaths Across America TC in December, the end-of-year calendar gives the community a genuine holiday identity that larger cities often lack.

Full calendar: Trophy Club signature community events page

Indoor Activities for Kids

Trophy Club is an outdoor-first community by design, and its in-city indoor activity footprint is smaller than many DFW suburbs. But the proximity to Keller (10 min) and Fort Worth (25 min) means the full range of DFW indoor entertainment is a short drive away on hot July afternoons.

Trophy Club Community Pool

Type: Aquatic Facility · Ages: All ages (residents)
The town's resident swimming pool with a splash pad, swim lessons, competitive Tritons Swim Team, and seasonal Dive-In Movie events. When Trophy Club Park and Harmony Park are too hot, the pool is the standard fallback. Residents only; check the seasonal schedule at trophyclub.org.

Trophy Club Library

Type: Public Library · Ages: 0-12 children's programming
Trophy Club's public library serves the community with children's programming, storytime, summer reading challenges, and family events. The library is also part of the Denton County library cooperative, giving residents access to resources across the county system.

Launch Family Entertainment (Keller, 10 min)

Type: Trampoline Park / Entertainment · Ages: 3 and up
Launch's nearest location to Trophy Club is in the Keller area. Trampoline courts, ninja courses, bowling, rock climbing, VR, and a full restaurant make it the standard rainy-day destination for TC families. About 10 minutes south on Hwy 114.

Main Event Entertainment (Keller area, 12 min)

Type: Bowling / Arcade / Entertainment · Ages: 5 and up
Bowling, laser tag, billiards, glow mini-golf, and arcade in one facility. About 12 minutes south and the go-to for birthday parties when Trophy Club families want a full-facility entertainment center rather than an outdoor activity.

Fort Worth Museum of Science & History (25 min)

Type: Children's Museum / Science Center · Ages: All ages
The Fort Worth Museum of Science and History, the Kimbell Art Museum, and the Fort Worth Zoo form a cultural district about 25 minutes southwest. For half-day or full-day indoor excursions on cold winter days or peak summer heat, this district absorbs the gap that Trophy Club's own indoor options can't fill.

SEA LIFE Aquarium (Grapevine, 15 min)

Type: Aquarium / Indoor Attraction · Ages: All ages
SEA LIFE Grapevine at Grapevine Mills (Legoland Discovery Center is attached) is about 15 minutes east via Hwy 114. The walk-through shark tunnel and stingray touch pool work well for all ages. A predictable school-break and rainy-day choice for TC families who have done the Fort Worth circuit and want something different.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

Trophy Club families run a clear summer heat strategy: Trophy Club Park before 9 a.m. (the trails are shaded and the lake is calm before motorized boats arrive), community pool midday (residents), and a drive to Keller or Grapevine Mills for indoor entertainment when the afternoon heat becomes unworkable. The park at 7 a.m. in July is genuinely beautiful, and most Trophy Club families have learned to schedule their Trophy Club Park visits for early morning from June through August rather than abandoning outdoor activity entirely.
