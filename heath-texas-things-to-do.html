What Heath Families Actually Do Here

Heath is a small, affluent lakeside city in Rockwall County, and it markets itself, accurately, as a premier outdoor community. Family life here revolves around Lake Ray Hubbard, the new Towne Center Park, and the kind of large-lot, semi-rural feel that is rare this close to Dallas. Part of Rockwall ISD, with most retail and dining a quick drive into Rockwall, Heath trades density for space and a quieter pace. See the full Heath relocation guide →

Life on Lake Ray Hubbard

Boating, sailing, fishing, and sunset views define Heath, with a two-lane boat ramp at Terry Park and marina access at the Heath Golf & Yacht Club.

A Brand-New Signature Park

Towne Center Park brings a splash pad, eight pickleball courts, sports fields, playgrounds, and a 2,000-capacity event lawn to the center of town.

Trails, Space & Quiet

The Reserve's 70-plus acres of nature trails and a 3-acre dog park give Heath an outdoor footprint most small cities cannot match.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Heath calls itself a premier lakeside open-space community, and its parks back that up. The city just opened Towne Center Park, a signature park with a splash pad, sports fields, and eight pickleball courts, while Terry Park puts a two-lane boat ramp and sunset views right on Lake Ray Hubbard. Add the Reserve's 70-plus acres of nature trails and a 3-acre dog park, and a small city offers a lot of outdoors.

Towne Center Park

Heath's new signature park at 200 Laurence Drive: a splash pad, the Memorial and Butterfly playgrounds, multi-use sports fields and a baseball field, two lighted basketball half-courts, eight pickleball courts (six covered and lighted), a Dry Creek nature walk, a Festival Street for food trucks, and a 2,000-capacity event lawn. Big parking lots, restrooms, and a paved trail loop into the Highlands of Heath. Shade is still maturing, so bring hats in summer.

Splash Pad Playgrounds Pickleball Sports Fields Trails

Terry Park

Heath's lakeside park at 599 Terry Lane, on the shore of Lake Ray Hubbard. A state-of-the-art two-lane boat ramp with two courtesy docks, walking trails, a covered pavilion with BBQ grills, grass picnic areas, a children's playground, and a sunset swing bench under a big shade tree. The best spot in town for boating and sunset family evenings.

Boat Ramp Lake Access Playground Picnic

The Reserve Nature Trails

Over 70 acres of natural-surface trails off Chris Cuny Parkway, with three signed trailheads, leuder stone benches, and plenty of wildlife and wildflowers. One trailhead sits next to Heath High School. The place for a real nature walk close to home; wear closed shoes and bring water.

Nature Trails Wildlife Trailheads

Heath Paw Grounds

A 3-plus-acre dog park at 305 Laurence Drive with fenced areas for different-sized dogs, real fire hydrants, water and bowls, parking, and bench seating. An easy add-on for families who bring the dog along.

Dog Park Fenced 3+ Acres

Antigua Bay Park

A quiet 1.7-acre neighborhood park at 610 Scenic Drive with a short walking trail, a covered pavilion and BBQ grills, picnic tables, open space, and a children's playground. A calm local green space best for nearby residents.

Playground Pavilion Neighborhood

City-Wide Trail System

Heath has a growing, connected hike-and-bike trail network that links Towne Center Park into the Highlands of Heath and surrounding neighborhoods, a mix of paved and natural surfaces. Good for golf-cart-and-stroller family loops.

Hike & Bike Connected Paved

Trail System Highlight

The Reserve Nature Trails, more than 70 acres of City-HMBC land with three trailheads and a mix of paved and natural surfaces, are the centerpiece of Heath's trail network. Great for a family nature walk and an easy off-pavement bike ride.

Relocation Tip

Start at Terry Park for the lake, the boat ramp, and the sunset swing bench, then drive over to the new Towne Center Park for the splash pad and pickleball courts. Together they show exactly why Heath markets itself as a lakeside, open-space community, which is most of why families choose it.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Heath is small, so youth sports run through a mix of city partnerships, independent leagues, and the broader Rockwall scene next door. The new Towne Center Park fields have become the home base, and the lake adds sports most suburbs simply do not have.

YMCA Youth Sports

Provider: City of Heath in partnership with the J.E.R. Chilton YMCA
Ages: young kids through elementary
Season: Spring and fall soccer, t-ball, and more, played on the Towne Center Park fields

Multi-Sport Leagues

League: i9 Sports, based at Rockwall-Heath High School
Ages: 3 to 14
Season: Rotating seasons of baseball, soccer, flag football, and basketball

Pickleball

Facility: City of Heath, Towne Center Park (eight courts, six covered and lighted)
Ages: all ages
Season: Year-round, with hourly rates and annual resident permits

Boating & Sailing

Where: Lake Ray Hubbard, from the Terry Park boat ramp and the Heath Golf & Yacht Club marina
Ages: family lake sports
Season: Year-round, with summer the peak

Junior Golf

Facility: Heath Golf & Yacht Club (private, 18 holes)
Ages: youth members
Season: Year-round junior golf for member families

Rockwall Area Youth Leagues

League: Rockwall Youth Community Athletics and Rockwall Texans Youth Sports
Ages: youth
Season: Seasonal baseball, softball, and more, a short drive into Rockwall

Featured Facilities

Towne Center Park is the new hub, with multi-use fields, a baseball field, two basketball half-courts, and eight pickleball courts. Rockwall-Heath High School (Rockwall ISD), home of the Hawks at Wilkerson-Sanders Stadium, hosts the i9 Sports leagues, and Lake Ray Hubbard plus the Heath Golf & Yacht Club round out the water and golf options.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Heath's attractions are mostly about the water and the open air, with the bigger entertainment, dining, and shopping a quick drive into Rockwall.

Lake Ray Hubbard & Terry Park

Lake Recreation · The heart of Heath's appeal. Terry Park's two-lane boat ramp opens the lake for boating, sailing, fishing, and the sunset evenings families come here for.

Heath Golf & Yacht Club

Private Club · A lakeside community built around an 18-hole golf course and a yacht-club marina on Lake Ray Hubbard, with golf, sailing, dining, and fishing for member families.

The Reserve at Heath

Dining & Entertainment · Heath's new dining and entertainment development, anchored by the upscale 1880 Kitchen, a contemporary grill, bringing a true sit-down destination into the city itself.

The Harbor at Rockwall

Waterfront Destination · About 15 minutes away, the lakeside Harbor offers waterfront dining, shops, an antique carousel, fountain shows, and summer concerts. Heath's go-to for a bigger night out.

Day Trip from Heath

Dallas Arboretum & Botanical Garden (about 30 minutes west across Lake Ray Hubbard), where the Rory Meyers Children's Adventure Garden makes a favorite full-day outing for Heath families.

Family-Friendly Dining

For a city its size, Heath is building a real dining lineup, from a community gathering spot to new upscale options, with Rockwall's waterfront restaurants a short drive away when you want the full lake-dinner experience.

Standard Service, Best community hangout
At 4240 Ridge Road, a casual Heath gathering spot and event venue (it hosts Christmas in Heath) with food, drinks, and a relaxed, neighborly feel families gravitate to.
Blue Koi Japanese Bistro, Best sushi night
A local Japanese bistro with sushi and hibachi-style plates, a reliable sit-down option for a family who wants more than a burger.
Manny's Tex-Mex, Best family Tex-Mex
Classic Tex-Mex plates, a kids' menu, and the kind of easygoing room that handles toddlers and teenagers in the same booth.
Gianni's Italian Bistro, Best pasta night
Family-friendly Italian with pasta and pizza, a comfortable pick for a relaxed weeknight or a visit from the grandparents.
Pablo's Restaurant and Cantina, Best weekend family dinner
A sit-down Mexican cantina with a full menu and a laid-back atmosphere, a Heath-area standby for a casual Friday night.
Wings over Heath, Best quick dinner after practice
Wings and casual bites for the nights practice ran long and nobody wants to cook. Quick, kid-friendly, and easy.
1880 Kitchen at The Reserve, Best new date night
A new upscale, full-service restaurant opening at The Reserve, with contemporary grill dishes built around quality ingredients. Heath's nicest dinner option as it comes online.
The Harbor at Rockwall, Best lakefront dinner (nearby)
About 15 minutes away, the Harbor's waterfront restaurants (from The Nolen to the casual Salty Sailor) give Heath families a true lake-view dinner without the wait of a drive into Dallas.

Heath's own dining clusters along Ridge Road and FM-740 and at the new Reserve development, while the waterfront restaurants at The Harbor in Rockwall are about 15 minutes north for a lake-view occasion.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Heath keeps retail light and residential, so most shopping happens close to home for essentials and a short drive into Rockwall for everything else.

Heath Ridge Road & FM-740

Everyday

Heath's everyday shopping is anchored by a Tom Thumb grocery near Towne Center Park plus a handful of neighborhood retail and service stops.
Best for: weekly groceries and quick errands without leaving town.

The Harbor at Rockwall

Waterfront Center

About 15 minutes north, a lakeside center mixing shops, restaurants, a carousel, and entertainment along Lake Ray Hubbard.
Best for: a full Saturday of shopping, dinner, and lake views in one spot.

Rockwall Retail Corridor

Everyday + Big Box

The I-30 and SH-205 corridor in Rockwall covers the big-box stores, restaurants, and services Heath does not keep in town, about 12 to 15 minutes away.
Best for: bigger shopping runs and back-to-school trips.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Heath leans into its small-town spirit, and a handful of signature events bring neighbors together at the new Towne Center Park and around the city each year.

April

Heart of Heath 5K & Family Run

Held annually since 1994, the city's signature run and 1-mile family fun run now returns to Towne Center Park, the park its early years helped build.

July

Independence Day Parade

Heath's Fourth of July parade and opening ceremony on Laurence Drive honoring veterans and active military, with a live barbershop quartet and free popsicles, popcorn, and cold drinks.

Seasonal

Towne Center Food-Truck & Concert Nights

The new park's Festival Street and event lawn are built for food-truck nights, concerts, and community gatherings as the city programs its signature space.

October

National Night Out

Heath's neighborhood-and-public-safety night, with block gatherings and a chance for families to meet the city's first responders.

December

Christmas in Heath

A holiday favorite at Standard Service on Ridge Road, with Santa, live reindeer, miniature train rides, home-baked cookies, food trucks, and live music.

Indoor Activities for Kids

Heath is small enough that most rainy-day options are a short drive into Rockwall, which has the trampoline parks, indoor sports, and theaters covered just 12 to 15 minutes away.

Heath Community Center

Type: Community Center · Ages: all ages
The city's indoor community space hosts programs and rentals, a useful in-town spot for classes and gatherings out of the heat.

Rockwall County Library

Type: Library · Ages: all ages
About 12 minutes away, the nearest full library for Heath families, with children's storytimes and youth programs.

Urban Air Adventure Park, Rockwall

Type: Trampoline Park · Ages: 4+
About 12 minutes away in Rockwall, a full adventure park with trampolines, climbing, ropes, and a sky rider for the energy-burn days.

RISE Rockwall Indoor Sports Expo

Type: Indoor Sports · Ages: youth and up
An indoor sports complex in Rockwall with courts and turf for basketball, volleyball, and more, handy for rainy-day practices and camps.

Cinemark Rockwall

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: all ages
The nearest multiplex, about 15 minutes away in Rockwall, covering first-run films and discounted summer kids' series.

Historic Downtown Rockwall Square

Type: Shops & Eateries · Ages: all ages
About 12 minutes away, the walkable courthouse square has boutiques, ice cream, eateries, and escape rooms for an easy browse when the weather turns.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

Heath's best summer move is the water itself: families head to Terry Park early for the boat ramp and lake, and to the new Towne Center Park splash pad before the worst afternoon heat. When it is 100-plus and even the lake is too much, the air-conditioned options are a 12-to-15-minute drive into Rockwall, from Urban Air and RISE to the Cinemark and The Harbor.
