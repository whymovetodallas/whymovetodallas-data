Rockwall ISD is a fast-growing suburban district serving approximately 25,000 students across 28 campuses in Rockwall County — one of the fastest-growing counties in the United States. The district primarily serves the cities of Rockwall and Heath, along with portions of Fate, Royse City, and Wylie that fall within Rockwall County. For families relocating to the eastern corridor of DFW, Rockwall ISD is consistently the district to evaluate first: it carries a TEA B rating, a Niche A grade, two comprehensive high schools, and a tax rate that district officials describe as among the lowest of comparable DFW districts after years of intentional reductions.

The two-high-school structure is one of the most important things families should understand about Rockwall ISD. Rockwall High School and Rockwall-Heath High School are both comprehensive campuses offering the full range of AP, dual credit, CTE, and extracurricular programs. Which high school your child attends depends entirely on your address. Both campuses are well-regarded, and the district is structured so that no family is disadvantaged by living on one side of the boundary versus the other — programs, staffing, and funding are intentionally balanced across both campuses. For families moving from a district with a single high school, this two-campus model is worth understanding before you start narrowing down neighborhoods.

Growth has been the defining story of Rockwall ISD for the past decade. The district has added campuses, managed enrollment pressure, and absorbed new communities at a pace that few suburban districts match. New construction is active in Heath, Fate, and the eastern Rockwall city limits. Families who buy in those communities today should expect the district to continue evolving — new elementary campuses, boundary adjustments, and program expansions are a regular part of life in a district this size and this trajectory.

Best Fit For Families Who…

  • Want a TEA B-rated, Niche A district with strong academic programs at both high schools
  • Are drawn to Rockwall or Heath and want a district with two full-service comprehensive high schools
  • Value a lower property tax rate relative to comparable DFW districts
  • Have a student interested in AP, dual credit, CTE, or Gifted & Talented programs available districtwide
  • Are relocating from a growing suburb and are comfortable with a district that is still adding campuses and adjusting boundaries
  • Want lakeside community access — Rockwall sits on Lake Ray Hubbard, a genuine lifestyle differentiator

May Not Be the Best Fit If…

  • You need a TEA A-rated district — Rockwall ISD's B rating reflects solid but not top-tier STAAR proficiency across all student groups
  • You are buying in Fate or Royse City and haven't verified which county your address falls in — not all Fate and Royse City addresses are in Rockwall ISD
  • You are looking for a small-district environment — at 25,000 students across 28 campuses, this is a mid-large district
  • Your commute is to west or northwest DFW — Rockwall County is on the east side of the metro, and commute times to Frisco or Fort Worth can be significant
Boundary Note — Read Before Making an Offer

Rockwall County is the core of Rockwall ISD territory, but not every address in the county's incorporated cities automatically falls within the district. Fate and Royse City both have portions served by Rockwall ISD and portions served by other districts (including Royse City ISD). Always verify your specific address using Rockwall ISD's attendance zone tool at rockwallisd.com before making an offer on any property in these boundary-edge communities.

TEA Accountability Rating

Rockwall ISD earned an overall TEA score of B (89) on its 2024 A–F Accountability Rating. Texas rates districts and campuses across three domains, each measuring a different dimension of student success. A score of 89 places Rockwall ISD solidly above the state average and among the stronger-performing mid-to-large suburban districts in the DFW area, while stopping just short of the A threshold that districts like Allen ISD and Frisco ISD hold.

The Three Accountability Domains

Student Achievement measures how well students perform on STAAR exams and other assessments in a single year. It rewards high mastery rates across the student population. Districts with high proportions of students performing at grade level score well here.

School Progress looks at two things: how much growth individual students make year over year (regardless of where they start), and how a district compares to other districts with similar economic demographics. Rockwall ISD performs above the state average on this domain, reflecting meaningful academic growth relative to its peer districts.

Closing the Gaps examines whether outcomes are improving for historically underserved student groups. Rockwall ISD's B rating reflects solid but not uniform performance across this domain — an honest tradeoff that is typical of fast-growing districts absorbing diverse new populations.

What a B (89) means for families: A district-level B rating means Rockwall ISD is a strong performer by Texas standards. Individual campus scores within the district vary — elementary campuses in established neighborhoods tend to score higher on the achievement domain, while campuses in rapidly developing areas sometimes reflect the enrollment volatility typical of fast-growth communities. Families should look at individual campus scores at txschools.gov, particularly for the specific campus that would serve their address.

TEA Data Note

The 2024 accountability ratings are the most recently released as of this writing. Visit txschools.gov for current domain scores and campus-level breakdowns for all Rockwall ISD campuses.

Academic Performance

Rockwall ISD's graduation rate is approximately 97%, well above the Texas state average. STAAR proficiency rates in reading and math are above the state average across most grade levels, consistent with a B/Niche-A district whose student population reflects the demographics of a growing, generally middle-to-upper-middle-class suburban county. For families making a relocation decision with academics as the primary driver, Rockwall ISD delivers solid performance without requiring families to pay the premium housing prices of the highest-rated districts in Collin and Denton counties.

Advanced academic programs are available at both high schools, not concentrated at a single campus. Both Rockwall High School and Rockwall-Heath High School offer a full AP course catalog, dual credit options through Collin College and Dallas College partnerships, and CTE pathways aligned with in-demand career fields. The Gifted & Talented program begins in elementary school and is available districtwide, an important access consideration for families specifically seeking GT services from the start of their child's education.

Key Academic Data Points

  • Graduation rate: approximately 97% (well above the Texas state average of ~90–91%)
  • AP enrollment: both high schools offer 30+ AP courses; district AP participation rates are above the state average
  • Dual credit: available at both high schools through partnerships with Collin College and Dallas College; students can earn college credit at no additional cost
  • Gifted & Talented: districtwide program beginning in elementary school; families should contact their campus counselor for current identification timelines
  • STAAR proficiency: above state average in reading and math at most campuses; visit txschools.gov for the most current campus-specific data
  • College readiness: Niche A grade reflects strong third-party assessment of overall academic quality and college-prep outcomes

Campuses

Rockwall ISD operates 28 campuses: approximately 19 elementary schools, 5 middle schools, and 2 comprehensive high schools, with additional specialized and alternative campuses. The two-high-school structure is the defining feature of the district for most relocating families — both campuses offer equivalent programs, and which one your child attends depends entirely on your home address.

Rockwall High School

High School (Grades 9–12) ~3,500 students Full AP & CTE Catalog

Rockwall High School is the district's original comprehensive campus, serving students primarily from Rockwall's western and central neighborhoods. The campus offers a full AP course catalog, dual credit partnerships, CTE pathways, GT programs, and robust athletics and fine arts. Students who live in the Rockwall city limits west of the boundary line will attend this campus.

Rockwall-Heath High School

High School (Grades 9–12) ~3,000 students Full AP & CTE Catalog

Rockwall-Heath High School serves primarily the Heath community, eastern Rockwall, and portions of Fate. The campus mirrors Rockwall High School's program catalog — same AP courses, same dual credit access, same CTE pathways, and equivalent athletics and fine arts programs. The district intentionally balances resources across both campuses so that families on the Heath side are not educationally disadvantaged relative to families on the Rockwall side.

Middle Schools (5 Campuses)

Grades 6–8 GT Pathways Available

Rockwall ISD operates five middle school campuses: Cain Middle School, Herman E. Utley Middle School, JW Williams Middle School, Rockwall-Heath (6th Grade), and additional campuses serving growing areas. Middle schools offer pre-AP coursework, Gifted & Talented continuation, fine arts, and athletics. Your campus assignment is determined by your elementary school's feeder pattern; contact the district to confirm your specific feeder path.

Elementary Schools (~19 Campuses)

Grades PK–5

The district's elementary campuses span Rockwall, Heath, Fate, and adjacent communities. Campus-level TEA performance varies; families should review individual campus ratings at txschools.gov and GreatSchools.org for the campus that serves their specific address. In a fast-growing district like Rockwall ISD, campus boundaries and grades served can change as new schools open — always verify your campus assignment directly with the district.

Specialty Programs

Both Rockwall ISD high schools offer the same program access — a deliberate design choice to ensure that where a student lives within the district does not determine what programs are available to them. For families evaluating specialty programs, the key question is usually whether their student's timeline aligns with program entry points (particularly for GT and AP), not which side of the high school boundary they are on.

Gifted & Talented (GT)

Districtwide, beginning in elementary school. Rockwall ISD's GT program identifies and serves students from the elementary level through high school. Identification typically begins in 2nd grade with testing and teacher/parent referrals, though students can be referred for evaluation at any grade level. GT students access differentiated instruction and advanced content aligned with their academic level. Families relocating from another Texas district should bring existing GT identification documentation — the receiving district will review prior evaluations as part of a transfer assessment. Contact your campus counselor for current identification timelines and procedures.

Advanced Placement (AP)

Both high schools. Both Rockwall High School and Rockwall-Heath High School offer a full catalog of 30+ AP courses across core subjects, electives, and specialty areas. AP participation and exam pass rates are above the state average, reflecting a student population with strong college-prep academic preparation. Students begin accessing pre-AP coursework in middle school, which is the pipeline into AP success at the high school level. Families should confirm which specific AP courses are offered at each campus and when pre-AP sequences begin in middle school.

Dual Credit

Both high schools, through Collin College and Dallas College partnerships. Dual credit courses allow high school students to earn college credit simultaneously with their high school credit at no additional cost to the family. Both campuses partner with community colleges to offer these courses, which can significantly reduce a student's college tuition burden. Dual credit is available for a wide range of subjects and is particularly valuable for students who want college credit without the pressure of an AP exam. Contact the high school's counseling office for current course offerings and college partner details.

Career & Technical Education (CTE)

Both high schools, 25+ pathways. Rockwall ISD's CTE department offers career pathways aligned with high-demand industries including healthcare, technology, business, engineering, and skilled trades. Students can earn industry-recognized certifications while completing their diploma. CTE programs are available at both high school campuses, and the district actively aligns its pathway offerings with Rockwall County's growing employment base. For students who are more career-focused than college-bound, or who want certifications in addition to a traditional four-year path, CTE is a substantive option.

Fine Arts

Districtwide, all campuses. Rockwall ISD supports fine arts from elementary through high school, including band, choir, orchestra, theater, dance, and visual arts. Both high schools have active fine arts programs with competitive performance ensembles that participate in UIL competitions. For families with students serious about performing arts, the district's two-campus structure means programs exist at comparable levels at both high schools rather than being concentrated at a flagship campus.

Athletics

Districtwide, both high school programs compete in UIL. With two high school campuses, Rockwall ISD fields two sets of varsity programs that compete in UIL, the Texas state athletic association. Both schools compete in Class 6A, the largest classification, and have produced athletes who go on to play at the college level. The district's overall athletic culture is strong — Rockwall County's growth has brought in family demographics where youth sports and high school athletics are deeply valued. Both campuses offer the full spectrum of UIL sports for boys and girls.

Enrollment Guide

If you're relocating to Rockwall ISD territory, here's how enrollment typically works. All time-sensitive details below should be verified at rockwallisd.com/enrollment for the current school year's specific dates and requirements.

Step-by-Step Enrollment

  1. Verify your address falls within Rockwall ISD — use the district's boundary tool or contact the district directly. Fate and Royse City addresses require special attention; not all fall within Rockwall ISD.
  2. Gather residency documents — typically: current lease or mortgage statement, two utility bills with service address, and government-issued ID. Exact requirements are on the enrollment page.
  3. Complete online registration — Rockwall ISD uses an online registration system. New student registration and re-enrollment are managed through the district's parent portal.
  4. Enrollment window — Re-enrollment for returning students typically opens in spring. New student enrollment is accepted year-round for in-boundary families. Contact the district for current-year window dates.
  5. Intra-district transfers — Families within Rockwall ISD boundaries may request a transfer to a different campus. Transfer approval depends on available capacity and is not guaranteed; contact the district's Student Services office for the current transfer application window.
  6. Inter-district transfers — Families from outside Rockwall ISD wishing to attend must apply through the district, subject to available capacity and board approval. Families within Rockwall ISD wishing to transfer to a neighboring district must apply through the receiving district.
  7. IEP/504 transfers — Texas law requires an ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meeting within 30 days of a student with an active IEP transferring to a new Texas district. Bring all prior IEP, evaluation, and 504 documentation to enrollment. Contact Rockwall ISD's Special Education department before finalizing your home purchase to understand current service capacity for your child's specific needs.

GT and AP Program Enrollment

Gifted & Talented identification is a campus-level process. Families relocating from another Texas district with prior GT identification should present existing documentation at enrollment — Rockwall ISD will review it as part of its own assessment process. For AP and dual credit course enrollment at the high school level, contact the high school's counseling office for current registration procedures and prerequisite requirements. Pre-AP course selection in middle school is the main pipeline for high school AP success, so families arriving in 6th or 7th grade should discuss placement with the campus counselor promptly.

Boundary Verification

Rockwall ISD primarily serves Rockwall County, but the boundary situation for families in Fate and Royse City requires careful attention. The district also has an internal high school boundary that determines whether your child attends Rockwall High School or Rockwall-Heath High School.

Cities and Areas Served

  • Rockwall, TX — The district's primary city. Most Rockwall addresses fall within Rockwall ISD, with an internal line that routes students to either Rockwall HS or Rockwall-Heath HS based on neighborhood. Verify your specific campus assignment when buying.
  • Heath, TX — The affluent lakeside community on Lake Ray Hubbard is largely within Rockwall ISD, with students attending Rockwall-Heath HS. Heath is one of the most desirable addresses in the district for families seeking both top-tier school access and a distinctive waterfront lifestyle.
  • Fate, TX (Rockwall County portion) — Fate is a fast-growing city, and its Rockwall County addresses feed into Rockwall ISD. However, any Fate addresses in Kaufman County fall outside Rockwall ISD. In a city that is growing rapidly across county lines, boundary verification is essential before making an offer.
  • Royse City, TX (Rockwall County portion) — Parts of Royse City that fall within Rockwall County are served by Rockwall ISD. The majority of Royse City is served by Royse City ISD, which is a separate district. A property's county determines which district applies, not its city limits.
  • Wylie, TX (Rockwall County portion) — Small portions of Wylie in Rockwall County may be within Rockwall ISD territory. Most of Wylie is served by Wylie ISD (Collin County) or Garland ISD. Verify any Wylie address individually.

The Internal High School Boundary

Within Rockwall ISD, a secondary attendance boundary determines which of the two high schools your child attends. This line runs through Rockwall city and assigns students to either Rockwall High School or Rockwall-Heath High School. Both campuses offer equivalent programs, but the high school your child attends is determined by your specific address. If this matters to your family, ask the district to confirm your high school assignment before finalizing a purchase — not just your elementary campus.

How to Verify Your Specific Address

  • Rockwall ISD Contact/Boundary Tool: rockwallisd.com/about-us/contact-us
  • TEA School Locator: tea.texas.gov — school district locator
  • Call the district: Rockwall ISD main office, (972) 771-0605
Home Search Implication

In a fast-growing district like Rockwall ISD, campus boundaries adjust regularly as new schools open and populations shift. A boundary map that is six months old may already be outdated. For any new construction community in Fate, Royse City, or the eastern Rockwall city limits, verify the current campus assignment with the district — not with the builder's sales representative — before committing to a specific lot or address.

School Finance & Tax Rate

Rockwall ISD's 2025 certified total tax rate is approximately $1.0669 per $100 of assessed value, one of the lower rates among comparable B-rated DFW suburban districts. The district has reduced its rate by approximately 39 cents since the 2016–17 school year through a combination of state formula adjustments and an I&S (debt service) rate reduction enabled by bond refinancing. For a home assessed at $450,000, after Texas's $100,000 homestead exemption ($350,000 taxable value), the annual Rockwall ISD school tax is approximately $3,734.

VATRE context (November 2025): Rockwall ISD voters approved a Voter-Approved Tax Rate Election (VATRE) in November 2025 that added approximately 12 cents to the M&O (Maintenance & Operations) rate to fund teacher compensation and operational needs. Simultaneously, the district reduced its I&S (Interest & Sinking/Debt Service) rate through bond refinancing by approximately 8 cents. The net result is the approximately $1.0669 total rate — a modest net increase while honoring prior commitments to rate reductions where possible.

Comparison context: Among B-rated, comparable-size DFW suburban districts, Rockwall ISD's tax rate is on the lower end. Allen ISD and Wylie ISD are broadly in the same range; Frisco ISD and Prosper ISD in Collin County are slightly lower on the ISD rate but carry much higher home prices. Always check the most current certified rate at the Texas Comptroller's website before closing.

Texas law provides a $100,000 homestead exemption off the appraised value for school district taxes on a primary residence. Homeowners over 65 receive an additional exemption and a freeze on the school district portion of their tax bill. Veterans with a 100% disability rating may be fully exempt from school district property taxes — an important consideration for veteran families. Rockwall County's strong growth means appraised values have risen significantly in recent years; the effective annual tax bill on a new-construction home in Heath or eastern Rockwall should be modeled using the current appraised value, not the purchase price.

Community Feel

Rockwall ISD families often describe the district through the lens of growth — nearly every family has arrived in the last 10 years, and the district's culture reflects that newcomer energy. Relocating families from California, Illinois, and other high-cost states are extremely common in Rockwall County, and the community has a distinctly welcoming orientation toward people who are new to Texas. Parent involvement at the campus level is high, booster clubs are well-funded and active, and the two-high-school structure means each campus develops its own community identity and rivalry without one dominating the other.

Heath deserves a specific note for families seeking a distinctive lifestyle alongside strong schools. Heath is an established, upscale community on Lake Ray Hubbard where homes often sit on large lots, some with lake access or dock privileges. The Rockwall-Heath attendance zone makes Heath one of the few communities in DFW where families can access a high-performing suburban school district while also living on a lake. That combination commands a price premium, but for families who value outdoor lake lifestyle — boating, waterfront parks, lakeside dining — Heath is a genuine outlier in the DFW market.

The practical context for families considering growth-area communities like Fate: these are newer neighborhoods where infrastructure is still catching up to population. Schools are newer builds and well-equipped, but road congestion, commercial development, and campus boundary adjustments are part of the growth-area reality. Families who have moved from established suburban neighborhoods in other states sometimes find the pace of change in fast-growth DFW suburbs either energizing or exhausting — it is worth calibrating expectations before committing to a specific community.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it matter which side of Rockwall ISD's internal boundary I live on? ▾
For program access, no — both high schools offer the same AP catalog, dual credit, CTE, GT, fine arts, and athletics. The district intentionally balances resources across both campuses. For community identity and athletics rivalry, yes — both schools have their own culture, booster community, and UIL program. Families with a strong preference for one campus over the other should confirm their address is on the correct side of the boundary before finalizing a purchase. The district can confirm your high school assignment for any specific address by calling (972) 771-0605.
Are all Fate and Royse City addresses in Rockwall ISD? ▾
No. Both Fate and Royse City straddle county lines. Fate addresses in Rockwall County are served by Rockwall ISD; Fate addresses in Kaufman County are served by Forney ISD or another Kaufman County district. Similarly, Royse City addresses in Rockwall County may be served by Rockwall ISD, while the majority of Royse City (in Hunt County) is served by Royse City ISD. Do not assume that a Fate or Royse City address means Rockwall ISD. Verify your specific address at the TEA school locator at tea.texas.gov or by calling Rockwall ISD at (972) 771-0605.
How does Rockwall ISD's GT program work, and can we transfer our child's existing identification? ▾
Rockwall ISD's Gifted & Talented program is districtwide and begins at the elementary level, with formal identification typically starting in 2nd grade. Families relocating from another Texas district with a child who already holds GT identification should bring all existing documentation (testing reports, placement records, prior IEP if applicable) to enrollment. Rockwall ISD will review the prior district's documentation and determine how to place your child through its own assessment process — Texas districts are not required to automatically honor another district's GT designation, though prior documentation often accelerates the process. Contact your assigned campus counselor as soon as enrollment is complete to initiate the review. Families arriving mid-year should not assume their child will be immediately placed in a GT-differentiated setting without completing the district's intake process.
What is the tax rate for Rockwall ISD and how does it compare to other DFW districts? ▾
Rockwall ISD's 2025 total certified tax rate is approximately $1.0669 per $100 of assessed value. On a home with a $450,000 appraised value, after Texas's $100,000 homestead exemption, the taxable value is $350,000 and the annual Rockwall ISD school tax is approximately $3,734. This rate is on the lower end among comparable B-rated DFW suburban districts — Frisco ISD and Allen ISD are modestly lower on their ISD-specific rate, but homes in those districts typically carry significantly higher prices. Rockwall ISD has intentionally reduced its rate by approximately 39 cents from its 2016–17 peak. Always verify the current certified rate at the Texas Comptroller's website before closing, as rates are set annually.
How does Rockwall ISD handle special education transfers from out of state? ▾
Texas law requires Rockwall ISD to hold an ARD (Admission, Review, and Dismissal) meeting within 30 days of a student with an active IEP enrolling in the district. At that meeting, the team reviews the prior IEP and determines how to implement or adapt it within the Texas education system. Bring all existing IEP documents, recent evaluations, eligibility documentation, and any 504 plans to enrollment. For students with significant or complex needs, contact Rockwall ISD's Special Education department directly before finalizing your home purchase — call the district at (972) 771-0605 and ask to speak with Special Education Services. The district's rapid growth means caseloads and service capacity vary by campus; a direct conversation before committing to a specific address is always the most reliable approach.
Why does Rockwall ISD have a TEA B rather than an A? ▾
Rockwall ISD's B (89) rating reflects strong but not uniform STAAR proficiency across all student groups. The district's rapid population growth has brought in a more economically and demographically diverse student body over the past decade, which affects the Closing the Gaps domain in particular. Individual campus scores vary meaningfully within the district — some elementary campuses score at or near A-level performance, while newer campuses in high-growth areas reflect the enrollment instability typical of fast-growth communities. For families whose primary criterion is a TEA A district, Allen ISD to the northwest is the closest comparable, though home prices in Allen are notably higher. For most relocating families balancing program quality, tax rate, and housing cost, Rockwall ISD's B (89) represents a strong value proposition in the eastern DFW market.
What is Heath, TX like for families, and how does it fit within Rockwall ISD? ▾
Heath is an established, upscale community on the western shore of Lake Ray Hubbard in Rockwall County. It is known for its larger lot sizes, custom homes, mature trees, and waterfront access that is rare in the broader DFW market. Families who value a lakeside lifestyle — boating, fishing, waterfront parks — alongside strong schools find Heath to be one of the very few DFW communities that delivers both. Heath falls within the Rockwall-Heath High School attendance zone. Home prices in Heath are meaningfully higher than in other Rockwall ISD communities, reflecting both the quality of the housing stock and the lifestyle premium of lake access. The community itself is small and tight-knit relative to the broader Rockwall metro growth area, which appeals to families who want a more established neighborhood feel in a county that is otherwise defined by rapid new construction.
