Moving From North Carolina to Dallas, Texas: A Worksite-First Guide

Build the complete monthly budget and weekday routine before you choose a DFW suburb.

Kristen CarpentierLicensed TX Realtor® · October 28, 2026 · 11 min read

Quick answer: moving from North Carolina to Dallas, Texas is not automatically a lower-cost move. Texas has no individual income tax, but the exact DFW home can carry a substantial local property-tax bill, different insurance deductibles, tolls, and summer electricity use. Start with the actual work address, set a complete monthly ceiling, then verify schools and recurring costs for each property.

This short DFW suburb-selection guide explains the four levers used below: worksite, budget, schools, and daily routine.

Charlotte and Raleigh households often arrive with useful instincts: compare employers, school options, airport access, and the kind of neighborhood that makes weekdays manageable. The mistake is treating Dallas-Fort Worth like one city. DFW is a large network of job centers and suburbs. A home that looks affordable can become a poor fit when the office, campus sequence, toll route, or full tax bill enters the picture.

Start with the exact DFW worksite, not the company name

Ask for the street address, normal arrival time, expected office days, and whether the role may move between sites. A company can have more than one North Texas office. A regional label such as “Dallas” can describe a job in Dallas, Irving, Plano, Fort Worth, or somewhere between them. Those locations create very different home-search maps. The worksite-based DFW guide shows how to narrow the region around the actual destination.

Charlotte finance and corporate transferees may initially recognize Las Colinas, downtown Dallas, and the Plano or Legacy area as familiar kinds of employment centers. Raleigh and Triangle households may focus on North Dallas, Plano, Richardson, or other technology and professional-service corridors. That is orientation, not a promise about any employer or commute. Verify the employee's assigned site.

  1. Pin the actual office. Include the parking entrance or transit stop, not only the corporate mailing address.
  2. Write the real routine. Add office days, school drop-off, childcare, medical appointments, activities, and airport trips.
  3. Test candidate routes. Drive at the normal departure time in both directions. Use the North Texas Tollway Authority trip tools for route-specific toll orientation, while noting that its calculator does not cover every North Texas toll facility.
  4. Set a drive tolerance. Make it a household decision before a larger house moves the search farther away.
Charlotte and Raleigh comparison

Do not search for “the DFW version” of an entire city. Search for the home-and-routine combination that works around your specific job destination. The right shortlist for an Irving office can be very different from the right shortlist for a Plano office.

Compare North Carolina and Texas taxes without inventing savings

North Carolina's individual income-tax rate is 3.99 percent for tax year 2026, according to the North Carolina Department of Revenue. NCDOR says additional rate changes may apply beginning in 2027 if statutory revenue triggers are met, so recheck the rate for the household's move and filing year. The Texas Comptroller's revenue guide reports that Texas has no state or local individual income tax. That difference matters, but it does not establish how much a particular household will save. Taxable income, residency, deductions, payroll, property choice, and professional advice all matter.

Texas also has no state property tax. Local taxing units set and collect property taxes, so two similarly priced homes can produce different bills. As verified November 1, 2026, a qualifying residence homestead receives a $140,000 school-district exemption, but exemptions do not apply identically to every local taxing unit. Use the county appraisal record and local tax offices for the parcel, and ask a qualified tax professional about your household.

Do not project a buyer's bill solely from the seller's capped appraised or taxable value. Review the current market value, every taxing unit and exemption, and a reasonable purchase scenario with the appraisal district or a tax professional. The Texas Comptroller's explanation of the homestead appraisal limitation under Tax Code §23.23 says it starts January 1 after the year the owner qualifies and expires January 1 after the year the owner no longer qualifies. That is not an automatic reset to the purchase price; ask the appraisal district how the rules apply to the specific ownership change. The Texas property-tax guide for out-of-state buyers explains the records to compare.

The honest comparison is side by side: expected North Carolina income tax for your circumstances versus the exact Texas property's tax bill, insurance, HOA, special assessments, utilities, and commute. Do not subtract one statewide percentage from another and call the result savings.

Verify schools by exact address

School district, city, mailing address, and assigned campus are not interchangeable. The Texas Education Agency says its district maps are approximate and that TEA does not collect attendance zones. The district or receiving campus must confirm the actual assignment.

  1. Search the full address. Use the district's current attendance tool, including the apartment or unit number where applicable.
  2. Confirm the complete sequence. Ask the district or receiving campuses to verify elementary, middle or intermediate, and high school for the intended school year, plus any capacity or boundary notice.
  3. Ask about enrollment and placement. TEA's enrollment guidance says the receiving district evaluates records and uses local assignment policies. Ask about documents, course placement, services, activities, transportation, and transfer rules.

Ratings can help frame questions, but they do not promise a future experience or establish eligibility. Review the reporting year and source, visit when possible, and keep the district's dated address confirmation with the property file. The DFW school district guide explains the broader process.

Recurring costs North Carolina buyers should price early

  • Home insurance: compare the policy form, wind and hail deductible, roof terms, exclusions, and flood coverage, not only the premium.
  • Electricity: parts of North Texas are open to retail electric competition. The state's Office of Public Utility Counsel points shoppers to the Public Utility Commission's Power to Choose website; price each plan against expected usage.
  • Tolls: price the route you will actually drive, and check whether every road is included in the calculator you use.
  • Foundation, roof, and drainage: use qualified inspectors and specialists for the specific property. A regional concern is not a diagnosis of a home.
  • Special districts and HOA: read the tax record, title material, resale certificate, and governing documents before the applicable deadlines.

Plan the move and the first 90 days

If you hire an interstate mover, federal rules in 49 CFR Part 375 require the mover to give you a written estimate before you sign the bill of lading and to provide, or link to, the federal booklet “Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move.” Before booking, ask for the company's USDOT number and confirm its registration at FMCSA Protect Your Move.

After arrival, follow the state sequence: obtain acceptable insurance; obtain the applicable emissions inspection if the vehicle is kept in a listed DFW emissions county, while commercial vehicles retain safety-inspection requirements; register the vehicle and complete any applicable title step at the county office; then apply for the Texas driver license. Since January 1, 2025, noncommercial vehicles no longer need a safety inspection before registration, according to the Texas Department of Public Safety, so there is no blanket safety-inspection step for every passenger vehicle. A new resident has 30 days to register a vehicle, and Texas DPS says a valid, unexpired out-of-state driver license may be used for up to 90 days after moving. Check the current TxDMV new-resident checklist, TxDMV FAQ, and DPS license guide for county, vehicle, identity, and appointment requirements.

A practical scouting trip

Tour fewer homes and test more routines. Drive the office route, visit the candidate district, check the nearest daily errands, and review one complete tax and insurance file for each finalist. That evidence is more useful than trying to see every suburb in one weekend.

Frequently asked questions

Will I save money moving from North Carolina to Dallas?

Maybe, but the income-tax difference alone cannot answer it. North Carolina's individual income-tax rate is 3.99 percent for tax year 2026, and NCDOR says additional changes may apply beginning in 2027 if statutory triggers are met. Texas has no individual income tax. Recheck the rate for the household's move and filing year, then compare the household's actual tax situation with the exact DFW home's property taxes, insurance, HOA, utilities, tolls, and maintenance.

Which DFW suburb is most like Charlotte or Raleigh?

There is no reliable one-to-one match. Start with the exact worksite, monthly ceiling, school needs, home type, and preferred routine. An Irving job, a Plano job, and a Fort Worth job produce different shortlists even for households with the same budget.

How much house can I afford in Dallas?

Use a complete monthly ceiling rather than a headline price. Include loan terms, taxes, insurance, HOA, special assessments, utilities, tolls, and maintenance. A lender can explain qualification; your own budget should also reflect the life you want after closing.

Are Texas property taxes higher than North Carolina's?

Do not rely on a state average for a purchase. Texas has no state property tax; local units set the bill. Pull the parcel record and all taxing units for the address, check exemptions and special districts, and compare that result with the North Carolina property you actually own or would buy.

How do I know which Texas school a house is assigned to?

Use the district's current address tool, then ask the district or receiving campus to confirm the complete campus sequence for the intended year. TEA says its district maps are approximate and does not maintain local attendance zones.

Should I buy before selling my North Carolina home?

That depends on financing, contingency risk, timing, and tolerance for two housing payments. Ask your lender and agents to compare a sale contingency, longer close, temporary housing, and other documented options before choosing a sequence.

The bottom line

A good North Carolina-to-DFW decision is specific. Pin the worksite, price the complete month, verify the school path, and test the routine from each serious address. The goal is not to prove Texas is cheaper. It is to find a home whose cost and weekday life still work after every important line is visible.
