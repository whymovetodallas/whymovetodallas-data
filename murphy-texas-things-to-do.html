Things to Do in Murphy, TX with Kids

Murphy packs a lot into a small footprint: 10 city parks, the Maxwell Creek trail greenbelt, a 2,500-seat amphitheater, one of North Texas's largest youth sports complexes, and a calendar of free community events that runs year-round.

What Murphy Families Actually Do Here

Murphy is a small, established city in Collin County where the family infrastructure is more developed than the city's size suggests. Ten parks, a master-planned creek greenbelt with 5-plus miles of trails, a 2,500-seat outdoor amphitheater, and PSA Murphy, one of the largest youth sports complexes in North Texas, give families a lot to work with without ever leaving their zip code. Families evaluating Murphy often compare notes with Wylie's family scene just to the south. See the full Murphy relocation guide →

Maxwell Creek Greenbelt

The Maxwell Creek trail system threads through Murphy with concrete hike-and-bike paths, off-road dirt trails, granite nature trails, footbridges, and interpretive kiosks along a natural creek corridor. The system is designed to link north to Parker and south to Wylie as part of the regional Collin County trails network.

PSA Murphy Sports Complex

Plano Sports Authority's Murphy campus at 330 N. Murphy Road offers basketball, soccer, baseball, softball, football, volleyball, cheer, martial arts, wrestling, and more for ages 3 through 18. Over 38 courts and 4 indoor grass stadiums make it one of the most complete youth sports facilities in Collin County.

Free Community Events

Murphy runs a full calendar of free events year-round: the Sounds at Sundown Friday concert series, Murphy Maize Days, Food Truck Fridays, a holiday campout, a Christmas in the Park celebration, and more. Most events center on Murphy Central Park and its 2,500-seat amphitheater.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Murphy maintains 10 city parks totaling more than 200 acres of parkland. The Maxwell Creek trail system forms the backbone of the city's green space, connecting multiple parks through a creek-side greenbelt with 5-plus miles of developed trails.

Murphy Central Park and Amphitheater

Murphy's signature park at 550 N. Murphy Road. 60 acres total with 52 acres of parkland, a 2,500-seat outdoor amphitheater, a splash pad (open the Saturday before Memorial Day through Labor Day, 9 AM to 8 PM), a 2-acre stocked fishing pond, covered playground (ages 5 to 12), 0.6-mile concrete trail connecting to the Maxwell Creek system, pavilion, picnic areas, concession stand, and outdoor education area. Restrooms on-site. The most-visited park in Murphy for family outings.

Preserve at Maxwell Creek

Located at 670 N. Murphy Road behind Kimbrough Stadium, this trailhead park is the quiet gem of Murphy's park system. A granite nature trail winds through native plantings with interpretive kiosks explaining local ecology, a long footbridge spans the rock-bottom creek, and benches under tall shade trees offer a genuine creek-side retreat. Pavilion, half basketball court, and backstop also on-site. Best for families who want a nature walk without driving anywhere.

Nature Trail Creek Footbridge Interpretive Kiosks Shade Trees

Waters Edge Park

At 403 Waters Edge Way, this is the active hub of the Maxwell Creek trail system. A concrete trail extends 1 mile north to McMillen Road and 1 mile south to McCreary Road, giving families a reliable 2-mile paved loop. Soccer fields, a playground, drinking fountain, and shaded picnic area fill out the park. The footbridge west connects to the Preserve at Maxwell Creek. Flat, paved, and stroller-accessible throughout.

Mustang Park

A neighborhood park with athletic fields available for reservations and organized play. Used regularly by youth sports organizations for practices and informal pickup games. Connects to the broader Murphy parks system and is well-positioned for families in the central part of the city. Contact Recreation at 972-468-4444 for reservation details.

North Hill Park

A neighborhood park serving the northern section of Murphy with playground equipment and open space. One of several smaller parks that give families walkable green space within their own neighborhoods without needing to drive to Murphy Central Park for a quick outing. Good for ages 3 to 10 on weekday afternoons.

Playground Open Space

Timbers Nature Park

A natural preserve park in Murphy with a wilder, less-developed character than the athletic parks. Good for families who want a more nature-focused outdoor experience. The natural setting with tree cover makes it notably cooler than open athletic fields on summer mornings, which is a meaningful advantage in July and August.

Trail System Highlight

Maxwell Creek Trail System is a master-planned greenbelt extending through Murphy along the Maxwell Creek floodplain. The current developed network includes concrete hike-and-bike trails, off-road secondary dirt trails, granite nature trails, bridges, and benches. Waters Edge Park offers the most accessible 2-mile paved loop. The system is designed to ultimately link to Parker to the north and Wylie/Sachse to the south as part of the regional Collin County trail network.

Relocation Tip

Start with Murphy Central Park and Preserve at Maxwell Creek on the same afternoon. Central Park shows you the event infrastructure (splash pad, amphitheater, stocked pond) that makes Murphy's community calendar possible. The Preserve shows you the natural side of the city that most visitors don't expect from a small suburban city. The footbridge over the rock-bottom creek is genuinely one of the better nature moments available in east Collin County.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Murphy's primary youth sports hub is PSA Murphy, the Plano Sports Authority campus at 330 N. Murphy Road, adjacent to Kimbrough Stadium. It's one of the most complete multi-sport facilities in Collin County and serves ages 3 through 18 in both recreational and competitive programs.

Soccer

League: Plano Sports Authority (PSA Murphy)
Ages: 3 through 18
Season: Year-round
PSA Murphy runs year-round soccer programs for 500-plus teams with recreational, competitive, and skills training options. A major program with deep local roots and multiple field locations in the Murphy area.

Basketball

League: Plano Sports Authority (PSA Murphy)
Ages: 3 through 18
Season: Year-round
PSA Murphy has 38-plus basketball and volleyball courts with year-round recreational and advanced basketball programs. The facility scale means shorter waitlists than smaller local rec programs and consistent court availability.

Baseball and Softball

League: Plano Sports Authority (PSA Murphy)
Ages: 3 through 18
Season: Year-round
Recreational and Advanced programs available, with 4 indoor grass stadiums on the PSA Murphy campus. Indoor stadiums allow year-round play independent of weather, which is valuable during both February cold snaps and June rain weeks.

Football and Cheer

League: Plano Sports Authority (PSA Murphy)
Ages: 3 through 18
Season: Year-round
PSA runs flag and tackle football programs for 4,000-plus players across multiple Metroplex locations. Cheerleading programs run alongside football. PSA awards scholarships based on financial need, with a sliding-scale fee structure.

Volleyball, Wrestling and More

League: Plano Sports Authority (PSA Murphy)
Ages: 3 through 18
Season: Year-round
PSA Murphy also offers volleyball, wrestling, martial arts, dance, and preschool sports programs, making it one of the few single-facility options where families can enroll multiple kids in different sports under one roof.

Fishing

Facility: Murphy Central Park Stocked Pond
Ages: All ages
Year-round
The 2-acre stocked pond at Murphy Central Park is a free, accessible fishing option for families. The Rainbow Trout Roundup held annually brings organized fishing competition to the pond in winter. A low-commitment outdoor activity that works well for younger kids new to fishing.

Featured Sports Complex

PSA Murphy at 330 N. Murphy Road, adjacent to Tom Kimbrough Stadium, is the primary youth sports hub for Murphy families. With 38-plus courts, 4 indoor grass stadiums, and programs for ages 3 through 18 across a dozen sports, it serves over 100,000 youth annually across North Texas locations. Visit psaplano.org for current registration windows, scholarship information, and scheduling.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Murphy's entertainment centers on its parks and sports complex, with major regional attractions in Plano, Allen, and Garland within 20 minutes.

Murphy Central Park Amphitheater

Outdoor Venue · The 2,500-to-3,000-seat amphitheater at Murphy Central Park hosts the Sounds at Sundown summer concert series and major city events including Christmas in the Park. One of the larger outdoor event venues in east Collin County and the anchor for Murphy's community calendar.

PSA Murphy Sports Complex

Sports Complex · The PSA facility at 330 N. Murphy Road is a destination in itself with 38-plus courts and 4 indoor grass stadiums. Tournament weekends draw families from across the Metroplex. Even if your kids aren't in PSA programs, the complex hosts open gym sessions and recreational events throughout the year.

Firewheel Town Center

Open-Air Lifestyle Center · About 15 minutes west in Garland, Firewheel Town Center is the closest major lifestyle retail and entertainment complex for Murphy families. Dining, shopping, a movie theater, and seasonal events like outdoor ice skating make it a versatile full-outing destination for weekends.

Murphy Community Center

Community Recreation · Murphy's Community Center offers Open Gym sessions, memberships, and facility rental for residents. An accessible indoor option close to home for families who want organized recreation without driving to a major commercial sports complex. Contact Parks and Recreation at 972-468-4444 for current programming.

Day Trip from Murphy

National Videogame Museum in Frisco (about 30 minutes northwest) is one of the most unique family day trips from Murphy: an interactive museum covering the full history of video gaming, with playable consoles from every era. Good for kids ages 8 and up who need a break from outdoor sports, and genuinely interesting for adults who grew up gaming in the 80s and 90s.

Family-Friendly Dining

Murphy's dining scene clusters primarily along the FM 544 corridor, with a mix of local independent restaurants and a handful of well-reviewed spots that have become neighborhood regulars. The proximity to Plano and Sachse expands the options considerably.

BackYard Murphy
Best casual family dinner
Chargrilled burgers, tacos, and BBQ with an outdoor patio and live music on weekends. Genuinely kid-friendly atmosphere where families with young children feel comfortable. One of Murphy's most popular local spots since 2020.
South Fork Texas Steakhouse
Best weekend dinner
At 217 E FM 544, a full steakhouse with a bar and lounge and outdoor patio. Works well for family dinners on weekends or for parents who want a proper sit-down meal after a week of sports pickups.
Pho 544
Best quick weeknight dinner
At 121 E FM 544, fresh Vietnamese pho and rice dishes. Established in 2017 and consistently well-reviewed. Kids who like noodle soup are regulars. Fast enough for a weeknight after-practice dinner.
Foxiis Restaurant and Bar
Best game-watching dinner
At 208 W FM 544, an American restaurant and sports bar with multiple TVs, a large dining area, and ample parking. More of a family-friendly restaurant on weekdays that transitions to a bar atmosphere on weekend evenings.
Mehfil Indian Cuisine
Best for adventurous families
Authentic Indian cuisine that's become a Murphy staple for families who want something beyond the standard suburban rotation. Good for older kids comfortable with bold flavors. Worth a try soon after arriving in Murphy.
Country Burger
Best with toddlers and picky eaters
A local burger spot that delivers exactly what it promises: simple, reliably good burgers and fries. The go-to for parents of picky eaters who just need something fast and acceptable after a long sports Saturday.
Plano dining (10 min west)
Best full dining destination
Murphy families regularly drive to Plano for a broader dining selection, particularly along the Legacy Drive corridor and the Shops at Legacy area, both about 15 minutes away and covering every cuisine type imaginable.
Sachse and Wylie corridors (nearby)
Best for variety
Adjacent Sachse and Wylie add additional dining options within 5 to 10 minutes. The tri-city corridor of Murphy, Sachse, and Wylie gives families a combined dining scene that outperforms any single city's count.

Most Murphy restaurants are concentrated along FM 544 between Murphy Road and state highway access points. Plano's dining corridors are the go-to for date nights and larger family celebrations.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Murphy's FM 544 corridor handles everyday shopping needs, with Firewheel and major Plano and Allen retail destinations within 15 to 20 minutes for larger family shopping trips.

FM 544 Corridor

Everyday Retail

Murphy's primary commercial strip runs along FM 544 with grocery stores, pharmacies, national chains, and local businesses covering daily household needs. Everything most families need in a given week is accessible within a short drive of most Murphy neighborhoods.

Best for: routine weekly errands and quick stops without navigating a larger shopping center.

Firewheel Town Center

Lifestyle Center

About 15 minutes west in Garland, Firewheel is an open-air lifestyle center with Target, anchor stores, dozens of dining options, a movie theater, and seasonal events including outdoor ice skating. The most accessible large lifestyle retail destination for Murphy families who want a full-day outing.

Best for: full Saturday shopping and entertainment outings where the family needs both retail and dining.

Legacy West (Plano)

Premium Retail

About 20 minutes northwest, Legacy West in Plano is a premium outdoor district with Whole Foods, high-end dining, boutique shopping, and a walkable lifestyle that differs from typical suburban strip malls. Worth the drive for back-to-school shopping or a special-occasion family dinner at a wider selection of restaurants.

Best for: special-occasion dinners and premium shopping that FM 544 can't cover.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Murphy's Parks and Recreation department runs one of the most active community event calendars in east Collin County, with most events free to residents and centered on Murphy Central Park and its amphitheater.

February / March

Daddy Daughter Dance

A formal dance event organized by Murphy Parks and Recreation for fathers and daughters. A well-attended annual tradition that sells out most years. Registration opens several weeks in advance through the Murphy Recreation online portal at murphytx.org.

Winter / Spring

Rainbow Trout Roundup

An annual fishing derby at Murphy Central Park's stocked pond. The city stocks the pond with rainbow trout for the event, and participants compete in a derby format at the 2-acre pond. A family-friendly outdoor event that introduces younger kids to fishing in a structured, low-pressure setting.

March / September

Food Truck Fridays

Murphy hosts Food Truck Fridays twice in spring (typically mid-March) and twice in fall (typically early September) at Murphy Central Park. Multiple food trucks set up for an outdoor community dinner with a relaxed neighborhood atmosphere. A quick and easy evening out without driving anywhere.

June

Sounds at Sundown

A free outdoor concert series held every Friday in June at Murphy Central Park Amphitheater. Bands take the stage from 8 to 10 PM with a variety of music genres across the four Fridays. Families bring chairs and blankets to the 2,500-seat amphitheater lawn. One of Murphy's most beloved warm-weather traditions.

July

Parks and Rec Month

Murphy Parks and Recreation celebrates National Parks and Rec Month with special programming, park activations, and community events throughout July. A good time for new residents to discover parks and programs they might not have known existed, particularly useful in the first summer after a move.

Summer

Murphy Campout

An annual overnight camping event held at Murphy Central Park where families pitch tents and camp together in the park. A genuinely unique community event that gives families who don't normally camp a low-stakes outdoor overnight experience right in their own city.

October

Murphy Maize Days

A free, full-day fall festival held annually in October at Murphy Central Park. Corn-themed fun with carnival activities, live entertainment (check the official events page for this year's lineup), food, vendors, and a car, truck, and motorcycle show. One of the largest free community events in east Collin County.

December

Christmas in the Park and Jingle Jog

December brings two signature events: Christmas in the Park, a holiday celebration at Murphy Central Park Amphitheater with seasonal entertainment and family activities; and the Jingle Jog 5K with a 1-mile Reindeer Dash, a festive holiday run open to all fitness levels. Both events close out Murphy's community calendar year.

Full calendar: Murphy official events page

Indoor Activities for Kids

Murphy's indoor options range from the PSA complex and Community Center close to home, to trampoline parks, bowling, and attractions within 15 to 20 minutes in Plano, Garland, and Allen.

PSA Murphy Indoor Stadiums

Type: Indoor sports complex · Ages: All ages
The 4 indoor grass stadiums at PSA Murphy stay busy year-round with baseball, softball, and soccer practice. Open gym sessions are scheduled separately; check psaplano.org for current programming. Air-conditioned and fully enclosed, making it a practical option during summer heat waves.

Murphy Community Center

Type: Community recreation · Ages: All ages
Open Gym sessions, memberships, and a variety of recreation programs for Murphy residents. A lower-cost indoor option close to home for families who want organized recreation without driving to a larger commercial complex. Contact 972-468-4444 for current Open Gym schedule.

Urban Air (Allen, ~15 min)

Type: Trampoline park · Ages: All ages
A full trampoline park and adventure complex with a toddler area, climbing walls, warrior course, and arcade. The most popular after-sports-practice indoor stop for Murphy families who need to burn remaining energy in an air-conditioned environment.

Bowlero (Garland, ~15 min)

Type: Bowling and arcade · Ages: All ages
Bowling, arcade games, and food in one air-conditioned complex. A reliable all-weather option for families with mixed-age kids who need an activity with clear structure and a defined endpoint. Glow bowling on weekend evenings for older kids.

Firewheel Town Center Cinema

Type: Movie theater · Ages: All ages
The cinema at Firewheel Town Center in Garland (about 15 minutes west) is the closest full-service movie theater to Murphy. Works well paired with dinner at the surrounding restaurants for a full-evening family outing that doesn't require a long drive.

Collin County Libraries

Type: Library · Ages: All ages
Collin County library branches serve Murphy with summer reading programs, story times, STEM events, and programs for school-age children. Free and accessible, with a strong summer programming calendar that helps newly arrived families fill weekday mornings during the first summer after a move.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

When July temperatures push past 100 degrees, Murphy families rotate through three indoor stops: PSA Murphy's indoor stadiums for morning sports practice, the Community Center for midday Open Gym when the kids need structure, and Urban Air in Allen for an afternoon that genuinely exhausts everyone before dinner. The FM 544 corridor's proximity to both means very little driving time between stops.
