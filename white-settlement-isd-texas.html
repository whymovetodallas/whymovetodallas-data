White Settlement ISD at a Glance

White Settlement Independent School District serves the city of White Settlement and adjacent areas in west Tarrant County, including Lake Worth and portions of western Fort Worth. The district is small by DFW standards, operating 8 campuses for roughly 6,900 students, and its core community sits just outside the western edge of Fort Worth near Naval Air Station Fort Worth Joint Reserve Base.

White Settlement ISD earned a C rating (73/100) from the Texas Education Agency in 2024. For families specifically researching this district, the headline number requires context. The district's most notable asset is the Fine Arts Academy at Tannahill, an elementary magnet that draws families from across the district with a dedicated arts-integrated curriculum. Wide campus variability is a defining feature here: individual schools within the district can differ significantly in academic outcomes, and Tannahill earns strong marks while other campuses score lower.

Who This District Is Best For

Best fit for families who:

  • Are purchasing in White Settlement or Lake Worth where home prices are well below the DFW median
  • Have an elementary-aged student interested in a fine arts-integrated curriculum (Fine Arts Academy at Tannahill)
  • Are already tied to the west Fort Worth area for employment at NAS Fort Worth JRB or west Tarrant employers
  • Value small-district community feel, where 6,900 students means a more personal experience than 20,000+ student districts
  • Are investors or buyers focused on affordability and understand the school district tradeoff

May not be the best fit if:

  • Academic performance ratings are the primary factor in your school district search
  • You have flexibility to look at homes in nearby EMS ISD or HEB ISD territory at similar price points
  • Your student has specialized academic needs that benefit from larger district program depth

TEA Accountability Rating

The Texas Education Agency assigns annual A-F ratings across three domains. White Settlement ISD scored 73/100 (C) in 2024, placing it in the lower range of rated districts in the DFW area.

Individual campus ratings within White Settlement ISD vary significantly. The Fine Arts Academy at Tannahill earns considerably higher marks than the district average, while other campuses score below the district's overall 73. A district rating of C does not mean all campuses perform equally, and your child's experience will depend substantially on their assigned or chosen campus.

Niche.com District Grade (Third-Party)

Niche rates White Settlement ISD a C overall, consistent with the TEA rating. Niche places it in the lower tier of Tarrant County school districts. The district's GreatSchools summary is rated below average. For families using academic performance ratings as a primary filter, these numbers are consistent across sources.

Academic Performance

White Settlement ISD STAAR proficiency data reflects a student population where approximately 62% of students are economically disadvantaged and roughly 48% are Hispanic. District-wide proficiency rates are below state averages across tested subjects. Individual campus performance, particularly at Tannahill, exceeds the district average.

Graduation & Completion

The 4-year graduation rate was 89.9% for the Class of 2023. This is in the acceptable range for Texas districts, though it trails higher-performing districts in Tarrant County. Brewer High School is the sole comprehensive high school serving grades 9-12 for the district's roughly 6,900 students.

A single comprehensive high school serving the entire district (about 2,100 high school students) means less campus choice than larger neighboring districts but can mean more predictable program availability. Every student ends up at Brewer HS unless they pursue an alternative program.

Campus Variability

The most important academic data point for White Settlement ISD: Tannahill Fine Arts Academy and the other elementary campuses do not perform identically. If the Fine Arts Academy is the draw for your family, understand that this program applies at the elementary level. Students who do not attend Tannahill are assigned to a zoned elementary school, and those campuses earn different ratings. Review individual campus accountability ratings at txschools.gov for your specific address before deciding.

Schools & Campuses

White Settlement ISD operates 8 campuses: 5 elementary schools (including 1 early childhood campus), 1 middle school, and 1 comprehensive high school. This is one of the smallest district footprints in Tarrant County. Every 9th-12th grade student in the district attends Brewer High School.

Programs & Special Offerings

Fine Arts Academy at Tannahill

The most distinctive program in White Settlement ISD. Tannahill integrates fine arts throughout the curriculum from Pre-K through grade 5, with dedicated instruction in music, visual arts, drama, and dance embedded in the school day rather than treated as an elective. The academy draws families specifically for this approach and earns campus-level ratings that outperform the district average. It is the clearest reason a family choosing between west Fort Worth area districts might select White Settlement ISD over alternatives.

Career & Technical Education (CTE)

Brewer High School offers CTE pathways in areas including health science, information technology, and business. The program scope is more limited than larger districts with dedicated CTE campuses, but core certification tracks are available for students pursuing vocational and technical career paths.

Special Education

White Settlement ISD provides special education services across campuses. Families transferring with an existing IEP should contact the district's special education department before enrollment. Texas law requires an ARD meeting within 30 days of transfer to review, adopt, or revise the existing plan. For families with significant or complex special education needs, contact the district directly to understand the specific resources available at your child's assigned campus.

Athletics

Brewer High School competes in UIL athletics at the 4A-5A level. With a smaller student body than neighboring districts, competitive athletics draw a proportionally large share of the student population, which can create a strong team culture for student-athletes in the program.

Approximately 62% of students qualify for free or reduced-price lunch. Roughly 48% of students are Hispanic and 28% are white, reflecting the demographic composition of White Settlement and adjacent western Fort Worth communities. The district's at-risk student percentage is among the higher figures in Tarrant County, which contributes to the district's lower TEA Closing the Gaps domain score.

Per-pupil spending of $10,375 is on the lower end for the area, which reflects both a smaller tax base and a district that does not have the same bond-funded infrastructure investment capacity as larger neighboring districts.

Enrollment Process

White Settlement ISD enrolls through the district website at wsisd.com. Required documents include two proofs of residency, birth certificate, Texas-compliant immunization records, most recent transcripts, and any IEP or 504 documentation. Contact the district registrar for mid-year enrollment. Call the district main office at (817) 367-1300 for assistance.

White Settlement ISD vs. EMS ISD

EMS ISD earns a B (87) from TEA and an A- from Niche, meaningfully stronger ratings than White Settlement's C. The districts are geographic neighbors, and some families considering west Tarrant County housing can access EMS ISD addresses at similar price points. EMS ISD's higher rating, larger program depth, and dedicated CTE campus (Hollenstein) make it the stronger academic option. The tradeoff is that EMS ISD's housing tends to sit in Saginaw and northwest Fort Worth areas, which may not match your specific employment location. White Settlement ISD's Tannahill Fine Arts Academy has no equivalent in EMS ISD's elementary lineup.

White Settlement ISD vs. Fort Worth ISD

Fort Worth ISD is the much larger district (145+ campuses, ~70,000 students) that borders White Settlement to the east. Both districts earn a C from TEA, though FWISD has a slightly higher Niche grade. Fort Worth ISD's magnet school system offers specialized programs that White Settlement ISD does not have at scale. For families specifically in the western Fort Worth area, confirming which district their address falls in first is the right starting point.

Living in the White Settlement Area

White Settlement is one of the more affordable housing markets in the DFW metro. The city offers single-family homes well below the regional median, close proximity to NAS Fort Worth Joint Reserve Base (significant for veterans and defense-industry workers), and reasonable access to Fort Worth's cultural and employment core via I-30 and Loop 820. Lake Worth, just to the north, adds lakefront character to the area.

Home Price Context

White Settlement homes typically range from the $180,000s for older stock to the mid-$300,000s for updated single-family properties. This is among the lower price ranges in Tarrant County, reflecting both the smaller district profile and the city's character as an established older suburb rather than a master-planned growth community. Families who need to maximize square footage per dollar and are close to west Fort Worth employment find the area attractive for that reason.

Veterans & Military Context

NAS Fort Worth JRB is located adjacent to White Settlement. The base's presence means the community has a long history with military and veteran families. Active-duty personnel and veterans using VA financing will find a supportive local culture. This is not a typical PCS relocation destination, but veterans separating from service who want to remain in the DFW area near the base community may find White Settlement worth considering.
