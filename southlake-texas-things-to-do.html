What Southlake Families Actually Do Here

Southlake families don't have to drive far for a great weekend, the parks, sports leagues, restaurants, and events are all within a few miles of home, anchored by a Town Square unlike anything else in DFW. See the full Southlake relocation guide →

Town Square at the Center

A 130-acre walkable mixed-use district where families shop, dine, catch live events, and run into their neighbors, all within walking distance of each other, in a setting DFW suburbs rarely match.

Curated Outdoor Spaces

Bob Jones Nature Center's more than 700 acres and 20+ miles of Cross Timbers trails sit five minutes from Town Square, Southlake's parks punch well above their weight for a city of 32,000.

Dragon Athletics Culture

Carroll ISD Dragon pride runs through everything, Dragon Youth Soccer, Dragon Youth Baseball, Dragon Lacrosse, and the nationally recognized Carroll Dragons football program define this city's family identity.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Southlake maintains over 1,100 acres of parkland and open space across roughly 20 parks and recreation areas, including one of North Texas' most distinctive natural preserves and a flagship community park that sets the standard for suburban amenity planning.

Bicentennial Park

Southlake's signature community park, home to the iconic dragon splash pad, Liberty Park playground, Southlake Tennis Center (19 courts, 17 outdoor + 2 indoor), baseball complex, synthetic turf fields, The Marq Champions Club, and the historic log cabin.

Splash Pad Baseball Complex Tennis Center Playground Trails

Bob Jones Nature Center & Preserve

More than 700 acres of rare Cross Timbers ecosystem on Southlake's northern edge, with more than 20 miles of hiking, biking, and running trails, a catch-and-release fishing pond, dog park, and environmental education programs.

20+ Mi Trails Fishing Dog Park Nature Programs

Lonesome Dove Park

An 8-acre neighborhood park with a stocked fishing pond, shaded playground, and a rentable pavilion, a low-key Saturday morning go-to for catch-and-release fishing and family picnics.

Fishing Playground Picnic Areas Pavilion

Chesapeake Park

A family-friendly neighborhood park with a fishing pier, mid-sized pavilion, and playground set around wide open grassy areas and a well-kept walking path, one of Southlake's calmer green spaces.

Fishing Pier Playground Walking Path Pavilion

Koalaty Park

Southlake's neighborhood ballfield hub, with four backstops heavily used by Dragon Youth Baseball teams on weekends. Open space and low-key, this is where the Saturday games live.

Ballfields Youth Sports Open Green Space

Bob Jones Park

Adjacent to the Nature Center, this park portion includes well-maintained soccer and baseball fields plus additional pond access, the practical sports and recreation complement to the preserve's trail system.

Soccer Fields Baseball Fields Fishing

Trail System Highlight

Bob Jones Nature Center Trail Network, More than 20 miles of maintained trails winding through more than 700 acres of one of North Texas' last remaining Cross Timbers habitats, with varied terrain suitable for trail running, mountain biking, and family hikes. Great for early morning runs before the Texas heat sets in and weekend trail rides with older kids.

Relocation Tip

Visit Bicentennial Park on a Saturday morning before you commit, you'll see Dragon youth baseball games, kids cooling off at the stone dragon splash pad, parents on the trails, and families on the tennis courts all at once. It captures the whole Southlake community energy in a single visit, and you'll understand why families stay for decades.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Carroll ISD Dragon athletics have defined Southlake's family culture for decades, with state championship teams across multiple sports and a youth pipeline that starts as early as age 3. The Dragon brand isn't just a school mascot; it's the city's identity. Compare DFW school districts →

Soccer

Dragon Youth Soccer (DYS): Pre-K through 3rd grade, ages 3+; Southlake/Westlake residency or CISD enrollment required; games at ULETE in Southlake; Fall & Spring seasons.
Grapevine–Southlake Soccer Association: Broader recreational soccer for ages 3–18, serving the wider area; Fall & Spring seasons.

Baseball & Softball

League: Dragon Youth Baseball Association
Ages: 3–18
Season: Spring & Fall; all home games played at Bicentennial Park's baseball complex; divisions vary by age and level of play

Lacrosse

Boys: Southlake Carroll Lacrosse Association, grades K–12.
Girls: Lady Dragon Lacrosse, grades K–12.
Youth spring registration typically covers K–8; high school registration handled separately. DFW Lacrosse member organizations; registration typically opens January.

Tennis

League: Southlake Tennis Center
Ages: 5+
Season: Year-round; clinics, lessons, and leagues at the 19-court facility at Bicentennial Park (17 outdoor hard courts + 2 indoor climate-controlled courts)

Swimming

League: Champions Club at The Marq
Ages: All ages
Season: Year-round; structured swim lessons from beginner through competitive; indoor pool open rain or shine

Basketball

League: Southlake Recreation Basketball
Ages: 5–15
Season: Winter; city-managed league through Experience Southlake; registration through cityofsouthlake.com

Cross Country & Track

League: Carroll ISD Feeder Programs
Ages: Elementary through high school
Season: Fall (cross country) & Spring (track); feeds into Carroll's nationally competitive athletics program

Golf

League: Junior Golf programs at area clubs
Ages: 6+
Season: Year-round; Timarron Country Club and nearby private clubs offer junior programs; Carroll ISD golf is consistently competitive at state level

Featured Sports Complex

Bicentennial Park Athletic Complex, Southlake's hub for Dragon youth sports, featuring the city's main baseball complex (Dragon Youth Baseball home), 44 athletic fields across the parks system, the 19-court Southlake Tennis Center (17 outdoor, 2 indoor climate-controlled), synthetic turf multipurpose fields, and The Marq Champions Club with indoor and outdoor aquatics. Most Saturday mornings, three or four Dragon leagues are running here simultaneously.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Southlake packs a remarkable variety of family entertainment options for a city of 32,000, from a state-of-the-art indoor recreation center to an outdoor Town Square that doubles as one of DFW's best year-round event venues.

Champions Club at The Marq

Community Recreation Center · Southlake's flagship rec facility: indoor pool with lazy river and water slides, zero-entry tot pool with children's slides, outdoor splash pad, indoor track, fitness center, basketball court, and supervised kids' play area. One of the most complete family rec centers in DFW, 2 min from Town Square.

Urban Air Adventure Park

Trampoline & Adventure Park · Wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pit, dodgeball courts, and climbing walls; birthday parties and group events welcome. The go-to high-energy destination for ages 2–18 and a reliable rainy-day option in Southlake.

Harkins Theatres Southlake 14

Movie Theater · 14-screen multiplex near Town Square with a Cine Capri large-format screen and Dolby Atmos sound. Reliably current on new releases, with a full concessions setup; the Cine Capri is worth upgrading for blockbusters.

EVO Entertainment Southlake

Family Entertainment Center · Bowling, laser tag, gaming, and arcades inside Town Square. One of the most convenient family entertainment options in the city, dinner at Town Square followed by a game of bowling is a well-worn Southlake family tradition.

Bob Jones Nature Center Programs

Environmental Education · Guided ranger walks, school field trip programs, and nature discovery activities for families, exploring the Cross Timbers ecosystem is unlike anything else near DFW. The visitor center opens the experience to kids of all ages.

Southlake Town Square Events Lawn

Outdoor Events Space · The Square's central lawn hosts Art in the Square, Oktoberfest, Stars & Stripes fireworks, holiday markets, and outdoor movies throughout the year. Southlake's de facto community living room, the best suburban gathering space in North Texas.

Day Trip from Southlake

SEA LIFE Grapevine Aquarium & LEGOLAND Discovery Center (15 min), Both are inside Grapevine Mills Mall, making it easy to hit both in one outing. SEA LIFE's 45,000 sq ft of sea life exhibits and LEGOLAND's hands-on build zones are perennial hits for ages 3–12, and the combined ticket saves money. Historic Downtown Grapevine is also worth a post-visit stroll, it's one of the most charming small-town main streets in DFW.

Family-Friendly Dining

Southlake's dining scene tilts upscale but stays family-welcoming, most of the best restaurants cluster in and around Town Square and Park Village, which means browsing boutiques and dinner naturally combine into one outing.

Truluck's Best celebration dinner
Southlake's premier seafood destination; Florida Stone Crab served trap-to-table October through May, plus responsibly sourced fish and USDA Prime steaks; great for celebration dinners when kids are old enough to appreciate it.
Mi Cocina Best with kids · picky-eater friendly
Classic Tex-Mex staple in Town Square with a bright, spacious dining room; solid kids' menu and the famous Mambo Taxi margarita that keeps parents coming back, 11am to 11pm daily.
Brio Italian Grille Best for everyone at the table
Tuscan-inspired Italian with handmade pasta, wood-fired flatbreads, and seafood; reliably family-friendly with a wide enough menu that everyone at the table finds something they love.
Moxies Best weekend brunch · best patio
Globally inspired menu with a gorgeous patio; popular for weekend brunch (10am–3pm) and half-price wine bottles every Wednesday; full of locals on a Friday evening.
Malai Kitchen Best for adventurous young eaters
Modern Thai and Vietnamese in Town Square; approachable flavors that get adventurous young eaters curious and keep parents genuinely excited about dinner.
Taverna Rossa Best quick dinner after practice · Park Village
Wood-fired artisan pizza at Park Village with a relaxed, lively atmosphere; Texas-accented Italian fare where the whole family almost always agrees on pizza and stays for dessert.
Shake Shack Best after Bicentennial Park · unanimous kids vote
Classic smashed burgers, chicken sandwiches, and thick-cut frozen custard right in Town Square; the fastest path to a happy-kids decision after a morning at Bicentennial Park.
Postino Wine Cafe Best for parents who want real food
Bruschetta boards, small plates, and a social patio atmosphere in Town Square; perfect for parents who want something more interesting than fast-casual after a full day with the kids.
North Italia Best weeknight family dinner · no reservation needed
Fresh, seasonal Italian in a modern setting; generous portions of handmade pasta and wood-roasted dishes that earn repeat visits, a go-to for family dinners that feel special without requiring a reservation weeks out.

Most of these are concentrated in and around Southlake Town Square and Park Village, dining and browsing are designed to go together here, which makes dinner-out a full evening rather than just a meal.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Southlake's retail landscape is anchored by one of the most successful open-air lifestyle centers in Texas, with neighborhood options that handle everyday needs without a trip to the big center.

Southlake Town Square

Lifestyle Center

130 acres of walkable open-air retail, dining, entertainment, and city services, over 120 shops including Apple, Anthropologie, lululemon, Tesla, Tiffany & Co., Market by Macy's, Sephora, and dozens of boutiques. For a full-day family outing: the Square does shopping, dinner, a movie, and dessert all without moving the car.
Best for: full Saturday family outing, shopping + dinner + entertainment, all walkable.

Shops of Southlake

Everyday

A neighborhood retail center anchored by Whole Foods and specialty tenants, the practical everyday shopping counterpart to Town Square. For weekly grocery runs and quick errands without navigating a 130-acre development, this is the neighborhood standard.
Best for: weekly groceries and quick errands, no Town Square parking battle required.

Park Village

Neighborhood Retail

Southlake's best neighborhood retail cluster with Taverna Rossa, Original Chop Shop, and a mix of specialty services. For the errand-plus-dinner combination: grab what you need, then stay for wood-fired pizza without battling Town Square traffic on a Saturday night.
Best for: dinner + errands on a weeknight, relaxed parking, local feel.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Southlake's community calendar runs nearly year-round, with most signature events centered on Southlake Town Square, the city's natural gathering place and the backdrop for everything from art festivals to fireworks.

April

Art in the Square

Three-day juried fine arts festival produced by the Southlake Women's Club, held annually since 2000. Over 200 artists, two entertainment stages, a bistro walk with local food vendors, and a dedicated kids' activity zone. One of DFW's signature outdoor art events; check artinthesquare.com for this year's lineup.

July

Southlake Stars & Stripes

The city's Independence Day celebration, held July 3rd in Southlake Town Square. Live entertainment, local food vendors, family activities, and a fireworks display over the Square. Check cityofsouthlake.com for this year's entertainment lineup and event schedule.

Sep–Nov

Carroll Dragons Football Season

Home games at Dragon Stadium draw community-wide crowds and create an atmosphere that defines Southlake family culture. Pep rallies, tailgating, and Friday-night stadium energy that parents and kids experience together, one of Texas high school football's most storied programs.

October

Oktoberfest Southlake

The Southlake Chamber of Commerce's annual weekend festival in Town Square: live music, German-inspired food, artisan marketplace, and the crowd-favorite wiener dog races. Family-friendly atmosphere with entertainment and food vendors spread across the Square.

December

Home for the Holidays

Southlake Town Square's annual Christmas tree lighting and holiday illumination. The Square transforms into one of DFW's most photographed seasonal destinations, with holiday entertainment and festive programming throughout the season. Check cityofsouthlake.com for event dates.

Year-Round

Dragon Sports Tournaments

Southlake regularly hosts regional and state-level youth sports tournaments at Bicentennial Park and area facilities, Dragon Youth Soccer, baseball, and lacrosse tournaments draw travel teams from across DFW throughout the year, adding energy to an already sports-active community.

Indoor Activities for Kids

When July temperatures push past 100°F, and they will, Southlake families have a well-practiced rotation of air-conditioned options that keep everyone entertained without driving to a big city.

Champions Club at The Marq

Type: Community Recreation Center · Ages: All ages
Indoor pool with lazy river and water slides, zero-entry tot pool with children's slides, indoor track, fitness, and supervised kids' play area, the most family-complete indoor facility in Southlake, open rain or shine year-round.

Urban Air Adventure Park

Type: Trampoline & Adventure Park · Ages: 2–18
Wall-to-wall trampolines, foam pit, dodgeball courts, and climbing walls. Birthday party packages available; the monthly membership pays for itself quickly if your kids want to go more than twice.

Harkins Theatres Southlake 14

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
14-screen multiplex with a Cine Capri large-format screen and Dolby Atmos sound, the upgrade is worth it for big releases. Always current on new releases; the standard for Southlake family movie nights.

EVO Entertainment Southlake

Type: Family Entertainment Center · Ages: All ages
Bowling, laser tag, gaming, and arcades inside Town Square, popular for birthday parties and family outings where the adults want a cocktail while the kids bowl. One-stop A/C entertainment in the heart of the city.

Southlake Public Library

Type: Library & Programs · Ages: All ages
Hosts 450+ programs per year for children, teens, and adults, including STEAM activities, summer reading programs, and story times. One of the most genuinely useful free resources in Southlake; the summer reading program keeps kids engaged between school years.

Bob Jones Nature Center Visitor Center

Type: Environmental Education · Ages: All ages
Exhibits, ranger programs, and guided nature walks exploring the Cross Timbers ecosystem, available year-round. A rare opportunity to experience one of DFW's most ecologically significant habitats, right inside city limits.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

Southlake families run a well-practiced three-stop rotation in July: early morning at the Bicentennial Park dragon splash pad before the real heat hits, midday at the Champions Club indoor pool (the lazy river and slides are genuinely worth the membership), and afternoon at the Southlake Public Library for a summer reading activity or free STEAM program. EVO Entertainment in Town Square is the backup plan when you need A/C, entertainment, and dinner all within walking distance.
