Why Allen Is a Great City for Families

Allen's family appeal is anchored by three things no other Collin County suburb can match: a 6,500-seat professional arena at the center of town, a preserved-farm mixed-use district that feels like a real neighborhood gathering place, and one of the highest-performing school districts in Texas. Add 40+ parks, a deep youth sports ecosystem, and the Allen USA Celebration, one of DFW's biggest Fourth of July events, and you have a city that earns its "best places to live" rankings year after year.

Credit Union of Texas Event Center

The CUTEC is Allen's anchor venue, a 6,500-seat arena that is home to the Allen Americans (NAHL professional hockey), hosts national touring concerts, Disney on Ice, family shows, and major sporting events year-round. It's one of the only suburb-based professional sports arenas in DFW, and its central location makes it genuinely walkable from much of the city.

Watters Creek at Montgomery Farm

Watters Creek is a 120-acre mixed-use development built on the footprint of the historic Montgomery Farm, one of the last working farms in Collin County. The result is a walkable outdoor town center with restaurants, boutiques, a creek-side trail, and outdoor green space that hosts farmers markets, live music, and community events throughout the year. It's the closest thing to a genuine town square that most DFW suburbs don't have.

Allen Premium Outlets

Allen Premium Outlets is one of the largest outlet destinations in DFW, 100+ brand-name stores (Coach, Nike, Michael Kors, and dozens more) in an open-air layout with food options and frequent family-friendly events. It's within 10 minutes of virtually every neighborhood in Allen and draws shoppers from across Collin County. For families relocating to Allen, this effectively replaces the need to drive to Dallas or Frisco for most major retail needs.

Parks & Outdoor Spaces

Allen maintains 40+ parks across the city, ranging from large multi-use recreation complexes to nature preserves with boardwalk trails. The city's parks department is particularly active, most major parks see consistent investment in playgrounds, athletic fields, and amenities. Allen's trail network links many neighborhoods directly to green space, making outdoor recreation genuinely accessible for most families.

Allen Central Park

Size: 68 acres · Best for: All ages
Allen's largest municipal park surrounds the Joe Farmer Recreation Center and Allen Natatorium complex. Features include multi-use athletic fields, playgrounds, picnic pavilions, and open lawn space. The park anchors the City Services Center corridor and is heavily used for organized youth sports leagues and community events. One of Allen's primary outdoor gathering hubs for families.

Celebration Park

Size: 120+ acres · Best for: Sports families
Allen's flagship multi-sport park with 14 lighted baseball/softball fields, 9 soccer fields, concession stands, and restroom facilities. Celebration Park is the primary venue for Allen Youth Baseball (AYBA) and AYSO Region 116 soccer, if your kids play organized sports in Allen, you will spend significant time here. The park also includes walking trails and open lawn areas for non-sports use.

Bolin Wilderness Park

Size: 190+ acres · Best for: Nature walks, older kids
One of Allen's most distinctive green spaces, a large preserved natural area with boardwalk trails, a creek corridor, native vegetation, and wildlife observation opportunities. The boardwalk section is particularly accessible for young children and strollers. Bolin is a genuine nature escape within the city limits and a popular choice for families who want something more substantive than a manicured park.

Bethany Lake Park

Size: 32 acres · Best for: Fishing, walking, picnics
A neighborhood-scale park centered on a small lake, with a fishing pier (catch-and-release stocked), walking trail around the lake, picnic tables, and a playground. Bethany Lake Park is a go-to for Allen families who want a quick outdoor outing, the paved trail loop makes it accessible for strollers and young cyclists. Evening walks around the lake are a favorite local activity.

Reed Park

Size: 45+ acres · Best for: Youth sports, playgrounds
A multi-use park in east Allen with baseball fields, playgrounds, and open green space. Reed Park is one of the more heavily used community parks in the city's eastern neighborhoods and serves as a secondary venue for youth baseball and recreational programs. Nearby to several Allen ISD school campuses, making after-school use common.

Molsen Farm Park & Natural Area

Size: 60+ acres · Best for: Nature exploration, older kids
A preserved natural area in northwest Allen featuring open meadow, upland forest, and seasonal creek habitat. Molsen Farm retains the character of historic North Texas farmland and is the kind of open green space that becomes rarer as suburban development continues. Trail access allows exploration without heavy foot traffic, a quieter alternative to Allen's more developed parks.

Watters Creek Linear Park & Trail

Size: Multi-mile trail · Best for: Walking, cycling, strollers
The greenway trail running through the Watters Creek development and connecting to adjacent neighborhoods is one of Allen's most-used recreational corridors. The creek-side sections offer natural shade and a pleasant walking environment even in summer. The trail links directly to the Watters Creek retail and dining area, making it practical for families who want to combine a walk with a restaurant stop.

Allen's Trail Network

Allen maintains 60+ miles of hike and bike trails that connect neighborhoods to parks, schools, and recreation areas across the city. The trail network is a genuine quality-of-life asset, many Allen families report that bike-commuting to school or trail access to the nearest park was a deciding factor in their neighborhood selection. For a full trail map, visit allenusa.org/trails.

Youth Sports & Recreation Leagues

Allen has a mature, well-organized youth sports ecosystem built around Celebration Park, the Joe Farmer Recreation Center, and a network of school-affiliated programs through Allen ISD. Leagues span ages 4 through 18 across all major sports, with both recreational and competitive divisions. The presence of the Allen Americans also gives Allen kids an unusual advantage: watching professional-level hockey year-round is a genuine recruitment pipeline for youth hockey programs.

Soccer, AYSO Region 116

Ages: 4–18 · Seasons: Fall & Spring
AYSO (American Youth Soccer Organization) Region 116 is Allen's primary recreational soccer league, serving thousands of kids each season at Celebration Park. Recreational divisions run Fall and Spring; EXTRA (travel/competitive) and VIP (all abilities) programs expand options beyond recreational play. Registration opens in summer for fall season and in fall for spring season.

Baseball & Softball, AYBA

Ages: 4–18 · Seasons: Spring & Fall
The Allen Youth Baseball Association (AYBA) operates at Celebration Park with divisions from T-ball through high school. Spring is the primary season; fall offers a shorter developmental season for younger players. Allen ISD fields competitive varsity and JV programs at Allen High School. Celebration Park's 14 lighted fields give Allen one of the best youth baseball infrastructures in Collin County.

Football, Allen Youth Football

Ages: 5–14 · Season: Fall
Youth football in Allen runs through the Allen Youth Football Association with flag and tackle divisions. Allen High School's football program is one of the most decorated in Texas, the Eagles are a consistent state contender, which creates strong community investment in youth development pipelines. Allen ISD also runs Pee Wee and freshman programs that feed into the high school varsity.

Basketball, Joe Farmer Rec Center

Ages: 5–18 · Season: Winter
The City of Allen Parks & Recreation Department runs youth basketball leagues through the Joe Farmer Recreation Center in Winter, with recreational and skills clinics available year-round. Allen Youth Basketball Association also operates independent leagues and tournaments. The Joe Farmer Rec Center is Allen's primary indoor recreation hub, court space, gym programming, and fitness facilities are all consolidated here.

Hockey, Allen Americans & Youth Programs

Ages: 4–18 (youth) · Season: Oct–Apr
The Allen Americans (NAHL) give Allen one of the only professional hockey franchises in a DFW suburb, and proximity to the team is a direct driver of youth hockey participation. Youth hockey in the Allen area operates through NTHL (North Texas Hockey League) and programs at local ice rinks. The CUTEC experience of watching live professional hockey makes the sport tangible for kids who might not otherwise consider it.

Swimming, Allen Natatorium

Ages: All ages · Season: Year-round
The Allen Natatorium is the city's dedicated aquatics facility, a competition-grade indoor pool with learn-to-swim programs, recreational swim, and year-round competitive swim club programs. The Allen Swim Club and Allen ISD's varsity swim programs both use the natatorium. Lessons are available for children from infant through teen; competitive swimmers in the area use this as their primary training venue.

Tennis, Allen Community Tennis

Ages: 4–18 · Season: Year-round
Allen maintains public tennis courts at multiple parks (Celebration Park and others) with USTA junior development programs and private lesson options. Allen ISD fields strong varsity tennis programs at the high school level. The combination of accessible public courts and structured junior programs makes tennis one of the more accessible year-round sports for Allen families.

Lacrosse, Allen Lacrosse Club

Ages: 6–18 · Season: Spring
Youth lacrosse in Allen operates through the Allen Lacrosse Club and DFW-area travel organizations, with recreational and competitive options for boys and girls. Allen ISD added lacrosse at the high school level, reflecting the sport's growth across Collin County. Spring season is the primary competitive window; skills clinics and fall development programs supplement year-round.

Joe Farmer Recreation Center

Allen's primary indoor recreation hub at Allen Central Park, the Joe Farmer Recreation Center houses gymnasium courts, a fitness center, multipurpose rooms, and a full schedule of youth programming. It's the city's main venue for basketball leagues, youth enrichment programs, and indoor recreational activities when summer heat makes outdoor options impractical. Memberships and drop-in options are available, visit allenusa.org/recreation for current programming schedules.

Entertainment & Family Attractions

Allen offers something genuinely uncommon in DFW's northern suburbs: a real arena at the center of town that delivers professional sports and major touring entertainment without requiring a drive to Dallas. Combined with Main Event Entertainment, a historic village, and the Watters Creek experience, Allen families have more keep-the-kids-busy options than the city's size would suggest.

Credit Union of Texas Event Center (CUTEC)

Type: Arena / Event Venue · Ages: All ages
CUTEC is the anchor of Allen's entertainment scene, a 6,500-seat multipurpose arena that hosts Allen Americans NAHL hockey games (Oct–Apr), national touring concerts, Disney on Ice, family shows, and sporting events year-round. NAHL playoffs can draw national-level attention. The arena's location at the center of Allen (US-75 corridor) makes it accessible without the downtown parking challenges of larger Dallas venues. An Allen Americans game is one of the best introductions to hockey a DFW family can get, loud, affordable, and genuinely exciting.

Main Event Entertainment

Type: Entertainment Center · Ages: All ages
Main Event is Allen's primary all-in-one family entertainment center, featuring bowling, laser tag, billiards, a large arcade, go-karts, and a full restaurant with bar. It's the go-to for birthday parties and group outings for school-age children through adults. Weekday afternoons are significantly less busy than weekend evenings; bowling lane reservations are recommended on Fridays and Saturdays.

Allen Heritage Village

Type: Historic Site · Ages: All ages (school programs especially)
The Allen Heritage Village preserves original 19th-century Allen structures, a depot, farmhouse, one-room schoolhouse, and other historic buildings, on a dedicated site near downtown Allen. It hosts school field trips, seasonal events, and self-guided tours. It's a low-key, genuinely educational outing for families who want something beyond standard entertainment options, and it gives kids a concrete sense of Allen's history before it became a suburb.

Cinemark Allen 16 & XD

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
Allen's primary movie theater, a 16-screen Cinemark with XD format for premium presentations. The Allen location is well-maintained and typically less crowded than comparable theaters in Frisco or Plano. Weekend matinees are the most family-friendly option; XD screenings of major releases (Marvel, Pixar, etc.) are a popular family-outing choice. Advance online tickets are recommended for opening weekends.

Watters Creek at Montgomery Farm

Type: Mixed-use Outdoor District · Ages: All ages
Beyond its shopping and dining, Watters Creek functions as a genuine community gathering place, with outdoor concerts, a farmers market, live music in the courtyard, and seasonal events throughout the year. The creek-side trail and green lawn areas make it genuinely pleasant for families even without a specific activity in mind. Weekend evenings in spring and fall bring a walkable, small-town-square energy that's hard to find elsewhere in DFW's northern suburbs.

Day Trip: Perot Museum & Legacy West (25 min)

Distance: ~25 min via US-75 South · Ages: All ages
The Perot Museum of Nature and Science in Dallas is one of the best science museums in Texas for school-age children, interactive exhibits, dinosaur fossils, an energy hall, and a dedicated children's museum floor. Allen's position on US-75 makes it a 25-minute drive without major highway changes. Legacy West in Plano (15 min) is also easily accessible for upscale dining and shopping. Both are natural day-trip complements to Allen's local options.

Family-Friendly Restaurants

Allen has one of the stronger dining scenes in Collin County, anchored by the Watters Creek restaurant corridor, the Exchange Pkwy strip, and Allen Premium Outlets food options. The range spans local stalwarts to national concepts with strong family reputations. For pre-game dinners (Americans games), Watters Creek and the US-75 corridor are the most convenient.

Rock Creek Seafood & Steaks

Best Celebration Dinner

Allen's long-running upscale seafood and steakhouse, the go-to for celebrations, anniversaries, and any dinner where you want something genuinely elevated without driving to Dallas. Family-friendly in portion sizing and atmosphere; private dining available for larger groups. Worth the splurge for birthdays or closing-day dinners.

BJ's Restaurant & Brewhouse

Best Family Group Dinner

The Watters Creek BJ's is one of Allen's most consistently reliable family restaurants, a large menu that genuinely pleases all ages, good Pizookie desserts the kids always want, and a space that handles groups without stress. Strong choice for after-sports dinners when you have a mix of tired parents and hungry kids. Reservations recommended on weekend evenings.

Thirsty Lion Gastropub & Grill

Best After an Americans Game

Thirsty Lion is the natural post-game destination, close to CUTEC, with a full bar, hearty pub-food menu, and a lively atmosphere that fits the energy of a post-hockey crowd. The burger selection is well above average for a sports-bar concept. Busy on game nights; arriving right after puck drop keeps the wait manageable.

The Mellow Mushroom

Best Family Pizza Night

Watters Creek's Mellow Mushroom is one of Allen's most kid-friendly sit-down options, the combination of creative pizza, a comfortable atmosphere, and a space that handles families without pressure makes it a reliable weekly rotation choice. The variety of specialty pies gives adults something interesting while kids get their reliable classic options. Good weekend brunch menu as well.

Torchy's Tacos

Best Quick Family Dinner

Allen's Torchy's is the go-to for a quick, high-quality family dinner, tacos that adults genuinely enjoy alongside kid-appropriate options, fast service, and an easy atmosphere for families with young children. A good weeknight default when no one has the energy for a sit-down experience. The Trailer Park taco is a crowd-pleaser for any age.

Cedar Creek Brewhouse

Best Local Saturday Lunch

Allen's local craft brewery and kitchen, a relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere with outdoor seating, craft beer for adults, good food, and a vibe that allows children without tension. A solid Saturday lunch option when the weather permits outdoor dining. One of Allen's few locally-owned spots with genuine craft character as opposed to a chain concept.

Genghis Grill

Best for Picky Eaters

The Allen Genghis Grill is a reliable choice for families with picky eaters, the build-your-own-bowl format lets every family member choose exactly what goes in their dish, which eliminates the usual dinner-table negotiation. Protein and vegetable variety is excellent; the stir-fry execution is fast and consistent. A useful rotation option when kids are in a particularly difficult-to-please phase.

Fuzzy's Taco Shop

Best Unanimous Family Vote

Allen's Fuzzy's consistently wins on the "where does everyone agree" vote, baja-style tacos that kids love, margaritas adults appreciate, counter service that keeps it fast, and a no-stress atmosphere for all ages. A strong choice for post-Celebration Park dinner when everyone is tired and just wants tacos. The breakfast tacos on weekend mornings are also worth the trip.

Perry's Steakhouse & Grille

Best Special Occasion

Allen's Perry's, one of the area's premier upscale steakhouse experiences, is the choice for significant occasions: school milestones, first days in a new home, or when out-of-town family visits. The Friday pork chop special is legendary in DFW. Dress code is relaxed but the quality is consistently excellent. Reservations are strongly recommended, especially on Fridays and special occasions.

Allen's dining corridor is primarily along Exchange Pkwy, US-75 / Central Expwy, and Watters Creek. For the most current restaurant options and hours, visit the City of Allen's dining directory at allenusa.org.

Shopping for Families

Allen's retail landscape is anchored by one of DFW's major outlet destinations and one of the region's best mixed-use town centers. For families relocating to Collin County, Allen's position between Frisco's major retail corridors and Plano's Legacy West makes it a genuinely convenient base for every shopping scenario.

Allen Premium Outlets

Parent Use Case: Major outlet shopping, great for back-to-school, sports gear, and brand-name apparel at outlet prices.
100+ brand-name outlet stores in an open-air design, Coach, Nike, Under Armour, Gap, Brooks Brothers, and many more. One of the most complete outlet shopping experiences in DFW, and the open-air format makes it workable for families even with young children. The back-to-school season (late July–August) brings significant deals on children's clothing and footwear. A food court and several restaurant options make it a half-day family outing when combined with nearby activities.

Watters Creek at Montgomery Farm

Parent Use Case: Weekend browsing and boutique shopping in a walkable outdoor environment, the Allen alternative to a mall trip.
A curated mix of boutique retailers, specialty shops, and national concepts in a walkable outdoor setting on the Montgomery Farm footprint. The atmosphere is significantly more pleasant than a traditional mall, open air, creek views, and a layout that encourages wandering rather than destination shopping. A farmers market runs on weekends seasonally. Spring and fall weekend evenings are particularly well-attended by Allen families combining shopping with dinner at the adjacent restaurants.

Exchange Pkwy & US-75 Retail Corridor

Parent Use Case: Everyday errands, Target, Costco, Home Depot, major grocery chains, and everything else in a single corridor.
Allen's primary everyday retail corridor runs along Exchange Pkwy and the US-75 feeder road, concentrating Target, Costco, Home Depot, major grocery chains (H-E-B, Kroger, Tom Thumb), and a full complement of service businesses. This is the backbone of daily life for most Allen families. Its density means most routine errands can be completed without leaving the US-75 corridor, a meaningful practical advantage for busy family schedules.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Allen runs a notably active community events calendar, anchored by the Allen USA Celebration (one of the largest Fourth of July events in DFW) and the Allen Americans hockey season, which turns the CUTEC into a community hub from October through April. The city's events team consistently delivers well-organized, family-first programming throughout the year.

Allen USA Celebration

When: July 4th · Where: Allen USA Celebration grounds
One of the largest Fourth of July events in North Texas, a full day of live music, food, family activities, and a major professional fireworks show that draws tens of thousands of attendees from across Collin County and beyond. The Allen USA Celebration is regularly cited as one of the best Fourth of July experiences in the DFW metro. Allen families treat it as one of the non-negotiable annual traditions from the first year in the city.

Allen Americans Hockey Season

When: October–April · Where: Credit Union of Texas Event Center
The Allen Americans (NAHL) regular season runs from October through April, with home games at CUTEC most weekend evenings and occasional weeknight games. Ticket prices are significantly lower than NHL or AHL options, making it an affordable weekly family-entertainment option. Playoff games (spring) bring the highest energy, the CUTEC atmosphere during a playoff run is one of Allen's best family experiences. Check allenamericans.com for the current season schedule.

Celtic Festival of Allen

When: Fall (September/October) · Where: Allen Central Park
One of North Texas's largest Celtic heritage festivals, bagpipes, Celtic music stages, Highland games demonstrations, food, craft vendors, and family activities. The Celtic Festival consistently draws families from across DFW for a full weekend and has become one of Allen's signature fall community events. Allen High School's significant Irish and Scottish heritage community makes this event feel particularly authentic compared to other regional festivals.

Watters Creek Seasonal Concerts & Farmers Market

When: Spring–Fall (ongoing) · Where: Watters Creek at Montgomery Farm
Watters Creek hosts a recurring lineup of outdoor concerts, weekend farmers market sessions, and seasonal events throughout spring and fall. The creek-side green space provides a natural outdoor-concert venue that draws families for casual Friday and Saturday evening outings. The farmers market runs seasonally on Saturday mornings with local produce, baked goods, and artisan vendors. Check watterscreekatmontgomeryfarm.com for the current calendar.

Allen Heritage Village Heritage Day

When: Fall (annual) · Where: Allen Heritage Village
The Allen Heritage Village's annual Heritage Day is the primary open-house event for Allen's historic preservation site, featuring guided tours of the restored 19th-century buildings, living history demonstrations, period crafts, and family-friendly programming. A lower-key but genuinely educational event, particularly valued by families with school-age children studying Texas history.

Allen Christmas Tree Lighting & Holiday Events

When: December · Where: Various Allen venues
Allen runs a consistent holiday events program each December, a city tree lighting ceremony, holiday concerts at CUTEC, and seasonal programming at Watters Creek. The Christmas season at Watters Creek is particularly atmospheric, with lights and outdoor decorations that make evening strolls through the outdoor town center a family tradition for many Allen residents. Allen ISD school concerts and performance events also anchor December for most families with school-age children.

For the most current events calendar, visit allenusa.org/calendar. Allen Americans game schedule: allenamericans.com.

Indoor Activities for Kids

Allen summers consistently hit 100°F+ from mid-July through August. The combination of Main Event Entertainment, the Allen Natatorium, Joe Farmer Recreation Center, and the Credit Union of Texas Event Center gives Allen families a solid indoor rotation, with enough variety that the summer months don't require driving to Frisco or Plano for options.

Main Event Entertainment

Type: Entertainment Center · Ages: All ages
Allen's primary all-weather family entertainment venue, bowling, laser tag, billiards, go-karts, an extensive arcade, and a full restaurant under one roof. Birthday party packages are available and heavily used; weekday afternoons are significantly less crowded than weekend evenings. The combination of activities makes it a genuine half-day destination for families with school-age children.

Allen Natatorium

Type: Indoor Aquatics Facility · Ages: All ages
Allen's dedicated indoor competition pool, available for recreational swim during open-swim sessions, with year-round learn-to-swim programs for children from infant through teen. The natatorium is Allen's primary answer to the summer heat for water-oriented families. Open swim availability varies by day and season; check allenusa.org/recreation for current schedules before planning a visit.

Joe Farmer Recreation Center

Type: Recreation Center · Ages: All ages
Allen's primary indoor recreation hub, gymnasium courts, fitness equipment, multipurpose rooms, and a year-round schedule of youth programming, classes, and drop-in recreation. The Joe Farmer Rec Center is particularly active in summer, when its climate-controlled facilities host structured day programs and drop-in activities for school-age children. Daily passes are available; memberships provide the best value for families using it regularly.

Allen Public Library, Children's Programs

Type: Public Library · Ages: All ages (infant through teen)
Allen's public library system runs story times, STEM workshops, summer reading programs, and school-break activity sessions year-round. The summer reading program is one of the most-used free family resources in the city, providing daily structure for June and July when school is out. All programs are free with a library card; the children's department is well-resourced and actively programmed throughout the year.

Cinemark Allen 16 & XD

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
Allen's primary movie theater with 16 screens and XD format. A reliable rainy-day option and particularly popular for summer blockbuster season. Matinee pricing on weekdays significantly reduces cost for families; the XD format is worth the premium for animated features and action releases. Monday through Thursday afternoon shows are the least crowded windows for a stress-free family movie experience.

Round1 Bowling & Amusement

Type: Bowling & Arcade · Ages: All ages
Round1 at Allen Premium Outlets offers bowling, billiards, karaoke, ping-pong, and a large Japanese-style arcade with prize redemption. The arcade component is more extensive than typical American bowling alleys, imported Japanese rhythm games, claw machines, and redemption games make it particularly popular with tweens and teens. A useful option when Main Event is crowded; the outlet mall location makes it easy to combine with shopping.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

Allen summers peak from mid-July through August with temperatures regularly exceeding 100°F. The local indoor rotation most Allen families rely on: Main Event for high-energy entertainment days, the Allen Natatorium for water time, Joe Farmer Rec Center for active programming, and the Allen Public Library for structured learning during the summer reading weeks. An Allen Americans game, even a preseason exhibition, is also a surprisingly effective rainy or hot afternoon option during the hockey-adjacent months.

Allen ISD produces some of the highest academic outcomes in the state, with Allen High School regularly earning national recognition. The city maintains 40+ parks, 60+ miles of trails, and a professional sports arena at the center of town. The combination of excellent schools, abundant recreation, and a genuinely active community events calendar makes Allen one of the most complete family cities in Collin County.
  • Allen has a mature and well-organized youth sports ecosystem. Soccer is organized through AYSO Region 116 (ages 4–18) at Celebration Park with Fall and Spring seasons. Baseball and softball run through Allen Youth Baseball (AYBA) at Celebration Park, with Spring as the primary season. Football is organized through Allen Youth Football Association with flag and tackle divisions. The City of Allen runs basketball leagues through the Joe Farmer Recreation Center in Winter. Swimming is available year-round at the Allen Natatorium, and the Allen Swim Club offers competitive programming. Tennis, lacrosse, and youth hockey programs round out the options.
  • Plenty of indoor options. Main Event Entertainment is the go-to, bowling, laser tag, arcade, and go-karts under one roof. The Allen Natatorium is open for recreational swim on weekends. Allen ISD and McKinney ISD are both highly regarded; the primary practical difference is that Allen families tend to have more structured entertainment options (arena events, outlet shopping) while McKinney families lean more toward outdoor recreation and the Historic Downtown experience. Both are excellent choices for families relocating to Collin County.
  • Allen is best known for three things: Allen High School football (the Eagles' stadium is one of the most prominent in Texas), the Credit Union of Texas Event Center and the Allen Americans hockey franchise, and Allen ISD's consistently high academic performance. For families moving to the area, this translates into a city with exceptional schools, professional-level local sports entertainment, and a well-developed park and recreation infrastructure that makes daily family life convenient and engaging. The Allen USA Celebration on July 4th is also widely considered one of the best fireworks and festival events in the DFW region.
