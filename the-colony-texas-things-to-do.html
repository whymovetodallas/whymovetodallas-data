The Colony lives up to "City by the Lake": Stewart Creek Park puts families on the shore of Lewisville Lake, Grandscape delivers one of DFW's most ambitious entertainment destinations, and a city events calendar with exact dates confirms this community shows up for its residents.

What The Colony Families Actually Do Here

The Colony earns its "City by the Lake" tagline. Stewart Creek Park and Hidden Cove Park both sit on Lake Lewisville's south shore, giving families direct water access for swimming, boating, fishing, and camping without a road trip. Grandscape, the $1.5 billion mixed-use development anchored by Andretti's Indoor Karting and the world's largest furniture store, adds a density of entertainment options rarely found in a suburb of this size. Families also come here for Lewisville ISD's schools, a free splash park with official city hours, and a community events calendar with six signature events that make the calendar worth planning around. See the full Colony relocation guide.

Lake Lewisville Access

Two city parks sit directly on Lake Lewisville's south shore: Stewart Creek Park (65 acres with a swim beach, boat ramp, camping, and sand volleyball) and Hidden Cove Park and Marina. For families who want water access in their suburb rather than a two-hour drive to a lake, this is one of the strongest arguments for The Colony over surrounding Lewisville ISD cities.

Grandscape

A $1.5 billion, 400-acre mixed-use development that includes Andretti's Indoor Karting and Games (go-karts, bowling, laser tag), Galaxy Theatres cinema, 30+ restaurants, Nebraska Furniture Mart (the world's largest furniture store), and a landscaped outdoor environment with a pavilion for family events. Nothing comparable exists in most DFW suburbs at this scale.

Six Signature Events With Exact Dates

The Colony runs a tightly produced community events calendar: the Spring Eggstravaganza (April 2), Festival of Flight (May 2), Liberty By The Lake fireworks (July 4), American Heroes Festival honoring veterans and first responders (November 14), and Holiday in the Park with the Parade of Lights (December 5). All are in 2026 and most are free.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

The Colony's park system includes neighborhood parks, community parks with athletic facilities, and two lake parks on Lewisville Lake. The city describes itself as "City by the Lake" and the park system earns that label: direct lakefront access for swimming, camping, boating, and fishing is the defining outdoor advantage here over most other Lewisville ISD communities.

Stewart Creek Park

The Colony's 65-acre flagship park on the eastern shore of Lake Lewisville. A swim beach is the centerpiece: one of the few places in the Lewisville ISD area where families can actually swim in the lake with a real sandy shoreline rather than concrete. Boat ramp and courtesy dock for powerboats and jet skis. Tent and RV camping. Fishing from shore and the dock. Sand volleyball court, basketball, playground, and a covered pavilion available for events. Nature trail through the wooded park edge. Restrooms and parking are solid. This park is the strongest argument for The Colony over neighboring Lewisville.

Swim Beach Boat Ramp Camping Fishing Sand Volleyball Nature Trail

Kids' Colony Park & Splash Park

Located at 5151 North Colony Boulevard, this community park is home to The Colony's free public splash park. Open daily from Memorial Day through September 30, 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. (closed Thursdays until noon for maintenance). Children 6 and under must be supervised by an adult 18 or older. No standing water in the spray features. Playground and picnic areas adjacent to the splash pad. Free to the public. This is the primary summer water play destination for TC families with younger children.

Splash Pad Playground Free Public

Hidden Cove Park & Marina

A Lake Lewisville park and marina serving The Colony's boating and fishing community with full marina services, camping facilities, and lake access. Managed through a public-private partnership. Good for families with boats or kayaks who want a full-day lake outing beyond what Stewart Creek Park's launch provides. Check hiddencovepark.net for current fees and services.

Marina Lake Access Camping Boating

Wheel Zone Bike & Skate Park

A dedicated outdoor bike and skate facility in The Colony's park system. Concrete ramps and features for skateboarders, BMX riders, and scooter riders. Good for families with older kids and teens who want a supervised outdoor activity space. Free to use; check the city's Parks and Recreation calendar for any programmed events at the facility.

Skateboarding BMX Outdoor Free

Pawsome Playground Dog Park

The Colony's off-leash dog park with fenced areas for both large and small dogs. Well-maintained and a consistent weekend gathering spot for TC families. The name alone tells you this is a city that takes its resident experience seriously: a dog park with a personality is a neighborhood indicator as much as a facility.

Off-Leash Area Fenced Dog Park

The Tribute Nature Trail

A scenic trail through the grounds of The Tribute Golf Links on the western shore of Lake Lewisville. The trail winds through native Texas landscaping with views of the lake through the tree cover. One of the more pleasant walking routes in The Colony for families who want lake scenery without the crowds of Stewart Creek Park. Connected to the broader lakeside trail network in the area.

TOCA Soccer Center / Eastvale Park

A 43,000 sq ft indoor soccer complex with two indoor fields plus three full-size outdoor lighted fields. The TOCA Center hosts local youth leagues, adult recreational leagues, youth camps, clinics, flag football programs, and regional, state, and national tournaments. One of the more complete soccer-specific facilities in North Texas for families with competitive soccer players.

Turner Soccer Complex & Baseball/Softball Complex

The city's dedicated outdoor athletic complexes for league play across soccer and baseball/softball. Multiple fields, lighting for evening games, and restroom facilities. These complexes host TCYSA soccer leagues and LBSA baseball and softball through the regular competitive seasons spring through fall.

Trail System Highlight

Stewart Creek Park Nature Trail loops through the wooded sections of the park along the Lake Lewisville shoreline. Combined with The Tribute Nature Trail on the western lake shore, The Colony offers lakeside trail access that most inland Denton County suburbs can't match. Both routes are best in early morning before summer heat peaks; the tree cover along Stewart Creek makes the humidity manageable longer than exposed trails elsewhere in the city.

Relocation Tip

Spend a Saturday morning at Stewart Creek Park, then drive to Grandscape for lunch. The contrast tells you exactly what The Colony offers that adjacent suburbs don't: a genuine swim beach on a major lake, followed by a go-kart track and 30+ restaurant options within a 10-minute drive. Families from Little Elm often look at The Colony too, since both cities border Lewisville Lake and share Lewisville ISD. The Colony's Grandscape tilts the balance toward entertainment variety; Little Elm has more open lakefront space.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

The Colony's entertainment profile is split between the lake (Stewart Creek Park and Hidden Cove for outdoor summer fun) and Grandscape (indoor and evening entertainment anchored by Andretti's and Galaxy Theatres). Hawaiian Waters Water Park, the city's own waterpark partnership, fills the gap between the two.

Andretti's Indoor Karting & Games

Family entertainment center at Grandscape · Go-kart racing, boutique bowling lanes, laser tag, and an extensive arcade game floor with no admission fee (pay as you go). The go-kart track is the main draw for families with kids 8 and up; the multi-level track layout makes it genuinely fun for parents too. One of the best birthday party destinations in Denton County for the 8-to-16 age range.

Hawaiian Waters Water Park

Waterpark · Open May through September with the Breaker Bay wave pool, Kona Cooler lazy river, and The Torpedo high-speed slide as the headline attractions. One of the few dedicated water parks in the northern DFW suburbs accessible without a long drive. Check hawaiianwaters.com for current seasonal hours and ticket pricing before visiting with kids.

Galaxy Theatres at Grandscape

Cinema · Multi-screen movie theater at Grandscape. The cinema within Grandscape means families can combine a movie with dinner at one of 30+ adjacent restaurants or a pre-show session at Andretti's without moving the car. Grandscape's outdoor landscaping between venues makes the between-venue walk actually pleasant.

Stewart Creek Park Lake Activities

Lake recreation · Swimming, boating, jet skiing, fishing, and camping at 65-acre Stewart Creek Park on Lake Lewisville. The swim beach is the most accessible family activity here: sandy shoreline, manageable depth for younger swimmers, and a park setting with shade, volleyball, and picnic areas supporting a full-day visit. Boat and jet ski rentals are available near the park; bring your own gear or rent locally.

TOCA Soccer Center Events

Sports facility and events · Beyond league play, the TOCA Soccer Center hosts soccer tournaments that bring families from across Texas into The Colony for weekend-long competitions. For TC families already connected to the soccer community, this means a built-in social scene of other soccer families at the highest level of youth recreational and competitive play in the area.

Golf at The Tribute & Old American

Golf venues · The Tribute Golf Links and Old American Golf Club are both award-winning courses on or near Lake Lewisville within The Colony. The Tribute is ranked among the top public courses in Texas and plays along the lake's natural terrain. Junior golf programs are available at both; for golf families these courses are one of the strongest draws TC offers over comparable Lewisville ISD suburbs.

Day Trip from The Colony

Grandscape itself is the day trip for most families who discover it after moving here: the Nebraska Furniture Mart (even if you're not buying furniture, the 1.9 million sq ft is a sensory experience), Andretti's for the kids, dinner at Portillo's or P.F. Chang's, and a movie at Galaxy Theatres is a full Saturday for most families without leaving the development. For something farther, Grapevine Mills with SEA LIFE Aquarium and Legoland Discovery Center is about 20 minutes east on Hwy 121.

Family-Friendly Dining

Grandscape dramatically expanded The Colony's dining options with 30+ restaurants in a single development. The range covers fast-casual to fine dining, with enough family-friendly formats that most palates in a mixed-age group can be satisfied in one stop.

Portillo's
Best for a family lunch
The first Texas location of Chicago's beloved Portillo's, at Grandscape. Chicago-style Italian beef sandwiches, hot dogs, and cheese fries that fans of the original have driven hours for. Quick-service format with good kid-friendly menu options.
P.F. Chang's
Best for a Chinese dinner out
Chinese-American restaurant at Grandscape with signature dishes like Chang's Chicken Lettuce Wraps, Mongolian Beef, and hand-rolled sushi. Consistent format and portions that work well for families with picky eaters who can reliably find something they like.
Saltgrass Steak House
Best for a special family steak night
Texas steakhouse known for Angus beef cuts and warm service. A solid choice for the kind of family dinner that needs to feel celebratory: after the first day of school, a birthday weekend, or when extended family is visiting from out of town.
Thirsty Lion Gastropub
Best weekend lunch spot
Gastropub format with a menu broad enough for mixed family groups: burgers, salads, sandwiches, shareables, and a draft beer selection that makes the adult end of the table comfortable while kids are occupied with games after the meal.
JÄKÖ Cocina y Agave
Best Tex-Mex & cocktails
Mexican restaurant and agave bar with a vibrant atmosphere. The food-forward cocktail program makes this work for adults while the classic Mexican food format gives kids familiar options. One of the more Instagram-friendly dining environments in The Colony.
Manee Thai
Best Thai takeout night
Thai restaurant that expanded to The Colony from Frisco at the start of 2025. A phone book-length menu that accommodates families with varying heat tolerances, from mild curry for kids to spicier dishes for parents who want the real thing.
Windmills Craftworks
Best neighborhood gastropub
Craft beer and kitchen concept in The Colony. A relaxed format that works for casual family dinners in the same way sports bars do: enough screen coverage that older kids stay engaged while younger ones finish quickly and want to leave.
Akira Back
Best for a special occasion dinner
Michelin-starred chef Akira Back's namesake Japanese restaurant at Grandscape. Korean-Japanese fusion with omakase and a la carte options. The occasion-dining option in The Colony that does not require driving to Uptown Dallas; best for families with teenagers and adults who appreciate elevated cuisine.
Portillo's (repeat for takeout nights)
Best after-practice takeout
See above. The Italian beef and cheese fries travel well; TC families with after-school and after-practice schedules have discovered that a Portillo's drive-through stop on the way home is one of the better weeknight dinner decisions available in the city.
Grandscape Outdoor Dining Patios
Best for spring and fall evenings
Grandscape's landscaped outdoor environment with patio seating areas connecting multiple restaurants makes outdoor dining in spring and fall genuinely pleasant. Families who like to eat outside will find the greenway areas and open pavilion between venues one of the better outdoor dining experiences in Denton County.

The majority of the Colony's dining scene is at Grandscape near Hwy 121 and Destination Drive. The Stewart Peninsula area near the lake adds a smaller cluster of casual dining closer to the waterfront.

Shopping & Retail Districts

The Colony has one of the most distinctive retail anchors in all of DFW: the Nebraska Furniture Mart Texas store at Grandscape, which at 1.9 million square feet is literally the largest furniture store in the world. Even families who don't need furniture often visit once just for the scale of it.

Grandscape

Mixed-Use Destination

A $1.5 billion, 400-acre development with Nebraska Furniture Mart, 30+ restaurants, Andretti's Indoor Karting, Galaxy Theatres, specialty retailers, and a landscaped outdoor public space with a covered pavilion. The scale makes it genuinely different from any other shopping and entertainment destination in Denton County.

Best for: a full-day family outing combining entertainment, dining, and shopping in one walkable destination.

Nebraska Furniture Mart Texas

World's Largest Furniture Store

The Texas store at Grandscape spans nearly 1.9 million square feet across 100 acres, covering furniture, appliances, electronics, flooring, and interior design in a single building. One of only four Nebraska Furniture Mart locations in the country and the only one outside the Midwest. New Colony residents furnishing a home don't need to drive to Dallas.

Best for: new residents setting up a home who want everything (furniture, appliances, electronics, flooring) in one trip rather than multiple big-box runs.

Hwy 121 Retail Corridor

Everyday Retail

The State Hwy 121 corridor near Grandscape and extending toward Lewisville anchors The Colony's everyday retail: grocery stores, pharmacies, auto services, and chain retail. For families who want Grandscape for major shopping and the 121 corridor for weekly errands, The Colony is well-served by this combination without requiring a freeway drive to a mall.

Best for: weekly groceries, pharmacy runs, and everyday household errands within The Colony.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

The Colony's Parks and Recreation department runs six signature events per year, and unlike many suburb event calendars, the city publishes confirmed dates in advance. All six 2026 dates below were published by the city before summer 2026.

April 2, 2026

Spring Eggstravaganza

The city's Easter egg hunt and spring family event. Kids gather eggs filled with candy and prizes in an outdoor park setting. One of the highest-attended annual events on the TC calendar; a reliable first-of-spring family gathering that new residents quickly put on repeat.

April 11, 2026

Coach Cox's Kids Race by the Lake

A youth running race on the lakefront: children ages 9 and under run a half-mile course; ages 10-12 run a full mile. The lakeside setting at Stewart Creek Park makes this a genuinely scenic community event. Well-organized and a good way for new families to meet other active TC families in a low-pressure outdoor setting.

May 2, 2026

Up, Up & Away! Festival of Flight

FREE outdoor festival with kite flying, kite-making demonstrations, birding demonstrations, paper airplane contests, and live music. The outdoor nature of the event and the variety of hands-on activities make this one of the more genuinely kid-engaging free events on the TC calendar. Check the city's events page for location details each year.

July 4, 2026

Liberty By The Lake

The Colony's Fourth of July celebration: Liberty 5K and 1M Fun Run in the morning, Independence Day Parade, then evening live music, food trucks, carnival rides, and fireworks over the lake. The lakefront fireworks setting is one of the better Independence Day experiences in Denton County. With live entertainment (check the city's events calendar for this year's lineup).

November 14, 2026

American Heroes Festival

FREE event honoring veterans and first responders with carnival rides, games, a concert series, food trucks, and a drone show. The veterans and first responders focus reflects TC's community values; the free admission and diverse family-friendly activities make it one of the city's most broadly attended events. Veteran families specifically cite this event as a reason they feel welcome in The Colony.

December 5, 2026

Holiday in the Park & Parade of Lights

The Colony's signature holiday event: themed parade floats, festive activities, games and rides, and the Christmas Spectacular with a synchronized light show. The December calendar anchor for TC families. Arrive early for the best parade viewing spots; the event consistently draws large crowds from across Denton County.

Full calendar: Visit The Colony events page

Indoor Activities for Kids

Grandscape gives The Colony better indoor entertainment options than most Denton County suburbs its size. Between Andretti's, the cinema, and the TOCA Soccer Center's climate-controlled fields, TC families have more in-city indoor options than the outdoor-first city character might suggest.

Andretti's Indoor Karting & Games

Type: Karting / Entertainment · Ages: 8 and up for go-karts; all ages for games
Go-kart racing, boutique bowling, laser tag, and arcade games in a climate-controlled facility at Grandscape. Pay-as-you-go pricing, no admission fee. The go-kart track works best for families with kids 8 and up; smaller kids can do the arcade and bowling while older siblings race. Strong birthday party destination.

Galaxy Theatres at Grandscape

Type: Cinema · Ages: All ages
Multi-screen cinema at Grandscape. The co-location with Andretti's and 30+ restaurants makes this more than just a movie theater; it becomes a multi-hour family entertainment destination when combined with the surrounding amenities. Matinee pricing and weekday screenings are reliably less crowded than weekend evenings.

TOCA Soccer Center (Indoor Fields)

Type: Indoor Sports · Ages: Youth through adult
The 43,000 sq ft climate-controlled facility with two indoor soccer fields provides year-round soccer options when DFW summer heat makes outdoor play unworkable. Indoor league play, camps, and clinics run through July and August when other youth sports pause. A meaningful differentiator for TC families with competitive soccer players.

The Colony Recreation Center

Type: Recreation Center · Ages: All ages
The city's recreation center, located near the community and senior center complex, offers fitness programming, classes, and community activities. Check thecolonytx.gov/1021/Facilities for current class schedules and programming offered through the center for youth and families.

The Colony Public Library

Type: Public Library · Ages: All ages; children's programming
The Colony's public library serves the community with children's storytimes, summer reading programs, and family events. Part of the Denton County library cooperative, giving residents access to the broader county system's resources and interlibrary services.

Nebraska Furniture Mart (Rainy Day Adventure)

Type: Retail Experience · Ages: All ages (seriously)
At 1.9 million square feet, walking through Nebraska Furniture Mart is genuinely time-consuming in a way that can fill a rainy afternoon. The appliance department alone is the size of a small building. Families who have moved to The Colony sometimes take visiting family members on a "tour of the world's biggest store" as a novelty outing. It works better than it sounds.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

The Colony's summer rotation is built around the lake (Kids' Colony Park splash pad before 11 a.m., Stewart Creek Park swim beach for longer mornings with older kids) and Grandscape's air conditioning (Andretti's from noon, Galaxy Theatres for the hottest part of the afternoon, dinner at Portillo's on the way out). On splash pad maintenance days (Thursdays, closed until noon), the TOCA Soccer Center's indoor fields and the Grandscape circuit absorb the gap. Hawaiian Waters Water Park is the all-day weekend option from May through September for families who want a proper waterpark rather than a splash pad.
