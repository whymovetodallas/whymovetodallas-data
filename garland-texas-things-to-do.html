What Garland Families Actually Do Here

Garland sits on the eastern edge of Dallas County with Lake Ray Hubbard as its defining asset: a 22,745-acre reservoir that gives families fishing, boating, and lakeside dining without driving an hour. Inside the city, Surf and Swim at Audubon Park is an outdoor wave pool that most DFW newcomers haven't heard of yet. Four free city spray parks, a vibrant cultural events calendar at Downtown Garland Square, and Firewheel Town Center rounding out weekend options make Garland a city that reliably delivers more than families expect. See the full Garland relocation guide.

Lake Ray Hubbard

A 22,745-acre reservoir on Garland's eastern edge with fishing, boating, lakeside dining, and Bass Pro Shops as an activity destination in itself. The lake gives Garland a natural recreation anchor that most comparable-priced DFW suburbs simply do not have.

Surf and Swim at Audubon Park

An outdoor wave pool water park inside Audubon Park, with waves, fountains, and splash zones that rival the area's paid water parks. One of the better-kept family secrets in northeast DFW and a recurring summer staple for Garland families.

Cultural Events Year-Round

Downtown Garland Square hosts free monthly concerts, Juneteenth celebrations, Asian American Heritage festivals, Dia de los Muertos events, and a beloved Christmas on the Square, making it one of the most active free-event corridors in suburban Dallas.

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Garland's parks system covers more than 2,800 acres of parks and open spaces, supported by six recreation centers and four free city spray parks. The city's spray parks open May 2 each year and run daily through October 5 (10 a.m. to 8 p.m.) at Embree Park, Wynne Park, Watson Park, and Downtown Square, per official city announcements.

Audubon Park

Home to Garland's Surf and Swim outdoor water park, with waves and splash fountains that families return to every summer. A large neighborhood park with open green space and access to Surf and Swim ticket pricing through Garland Parks and Recreation. Best for ages 3 and up when the water park is open.

Surf and Swim Water Park Green Space Picnic Areas

Wynne Park

A 16.9-acre neighborhood park on West Centerville Road with a free city spray pad, playground, and standard park amenities. Touch the gray bollard pole to activate the spray features. Open daily May through early October (10 a.m. to 8 p.m. per city schedule). Restrooms on-site. Good for ages 2 through 9 at the spray pad.

Free Spray Pad Playground Picnic Areas

Woodland Basin Nature Area

A 49-acre natural area with an unpaved nature trail through wooded terrain, offering a quieter alternative to Garland's more developed parks. Best for families with kids ages 5 and older who enjoy nature walks and birdwatching. Minimal shade amenities; bring water.

Nature Trail Birdwatching Dog Friendly

Rivercrest Branch Park

A 23.4-acre park with a 0.3-mile concrete walking trail and additional unpaved hiking and biking trails through natural areas. A local neighborhood park used regularly by Garland families for daily walks and off-leash dog areas in certain sections.

Walking Trail Hiking Dog Friendly

Lake Ray Hubbard Greenbelt

A city-designated greenbelt park along the Lake Ray Hubbard shoreline with trail access, fishing areas, and scenic lake views. The Bass Pro Shops complex sits adjacent to this area, making it easy to combine a morning walk with a stop at one of DFW's most family-friendly retail destinations.

Lake Access Fishing Trails Scenic Views

Ablon Park

A 10.7-acre neighborhood park with 0.9 miles of paved walking and jogging trail, a playground, and a recreational green space popular for weekend family outings. The paved trail loop is flat and stroller-accessible. Restrooms available. One of the better-maintained smaller parks in the system.

Paved Trail Loop Playground Stroller-Friendly

Embree Park

A city park with one of Garland's four free spray pads, activated by touching the orange bollard pole. Open daily May 2 through October 5 (10 a.m. to 8 p.m. per city schedule), free admission. A quick and easy neighborhood cool-down spot for families with younger kids. Check garlandtx.gov/3210 for any schedule changes.

Free Spray Pad Playground

Trail and Nature Access

Garland's greenbelt and nature areas range from paved neighborhood trails at Ablon Park to unpaved woodland paths at Woodland Basin Nature Area and lakefront access at the Lake Ray Hubbard Greenbelt. The city's parks include fishing piers, a disc golf course, and miles of combined trail across its 80+ park locations. Great for family bike rides, morning jogs, and nature walks.

Relocation Tip

Start with Audubon Park on your first summer weekend. Surf and Swim gives you a feel for what Garland residents actually use on hot days, and it's one of the most genuinely kid-focused public amenities in northeast DFW. Pair it with an evening visit to the Lake Ray Hubbard Greenbelt near Bass Pro Shops for a full picture of what living here actually looks like.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Garland Parks and Recreation contracts with non-profit athletic associations to provide youth sports programs across the city, covering baseball, basketball, football, soccer, softball, cheer, cricket, and special needs sports. The city also hosts faith-based programs like Upward Sports and national franchises like i9 Sports.

Recreation Center Network

Six Garland recreation centers serve as the home-court venues for City recreation leagues and after-school programming. The city's most recent major aquatics investment, the Holford Aquatic Center, opened in 2025 with two water slides, a current channel, lap lanes, and both a tot pool and an activity pool, giving Garland families an updated year-round swimming facility to complement the seasonal outdoor water options.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Garland's entertainment anchors are two outdoor destinations that families return to repeatedly: Lake Ray Hubbard and Firewheel Town Center. Between them and Surf and Swim, most DFW families discover quickly that Garland gives a lot of return for a mid-tier housing budget.

Bass Pro Shops Outdoor World at Lake Ray Hubbard

Outdoor Retail and Attraction · Over 120,000 sq ft of fishing, hunting, camping, and boating gear on the scenic banks of Lake Ray Hubbard. Features an onsite arcade, restaurants, a bar, and a private saltwater beach. Kids love exploring the taxidermy displays and saltwater aquariums inside. A destination outing in itself.

Surf and Swim at Audubon Park

Outdoor Water Park · Waves, fountains, and splash zones in a colorful outdoor water park inside Audubon Park. Affordable seasonal admission through Garland Parks and Recreation. One of the best public water play options in northeast DFW for families who want real water fun without Six Flags pricing.

Holford Aquatic Center

Public Aquatics · Garland's newest aquatic facility, opened in 2025, with two water slides, a current channel, lap lanes, a tot pool, and an activity pool. Check garlandtx.gov for current hours, admission pricing, and seasonal schedule.

Firewheel Town Center

Outdoor Lifestyle Center · The premier shopping and entertainment destination for northeast Dallas families, with over 100 stores, an AMC theater, and dozens of dining options in an open-air format. A full-afternoon destination that combines retail, movies, and dining without driving to a distant mall.

Lake Ray Hubbard (Fishing and Boating)

Lake Recreation · A premier fishing and boating destination with multiple public access points, boat ramps, and fishing piers along the Garland shoreline. One of the most productive bass-fishing lakes in North Texas, with family-friendly kayaking and paddleboarding options for lower-key outings.

Downtown Garland Square

Historic Downtown · Garland's historic square is an active community gathering space hosting monthly free concerts, cultural festivals, a farmers market, and seasonal events throughout the year. A walkable, family-welcoming environment with local shops and dining around the square perimeter.

Day Trip from Garland

Rockwall Harbor (about 15 minutes east along Lake Ray Hubbard), with waterfront dining, live music venues, a Cinemark 14 movie theater, lakeside walkways, and an outdoor amphitheater. An easy lake-district day trip that extends naturally from an afternoon on the Garland side of the lake. Families visiting Rockwall Harbor often also look at what Rowlett has to offer families along the same Lake Ray Hubbard corridor.

Family-Friendly Dining

Garland's strongest dining concentration is at Firewheel Town Center, where 30+ restaurants cluster in a walkable open-air setting. The Lake Ray Hubbard shoreline adds a smaller but scenic waterfront dining option for families who want something different from a strip mall experience.

Texas Roadhouse
Best for: Family steakhouse tradition
Fresh-baked rolls the moment you sit, a reliable kids' menu, and portions that satisfy both adults and hungry athletes. The Firewheel-area location is a consistent family favorite for Thursday or Saturday dinner.
Red Robin
Best for: Burger night picky eaters
Gourmet burgers and bottomless steak fries at Firewheel Town Center, with a full kids' menu and a layout that handles large family groups without stress. Good for weekday dinners when everyone wants something different.
Cheddar's Scratch Kitchen
Best for: Value family dinner scratch-cooked
Scratch-cooked comfort food at a price point that makes family dining genuinely affordable. Large portions, good variety, and a warm atmosphere near the Firewheel corridor.
Olive Garden
Best for: Italian night unlimited kids love
Pasta, salad, and breadsticks in a reliably kid-welcoming format. The Firewheel location handles large tables well and the unlimited breadsticks and salad make it a genuine value for families with growing appetites.
Houlihan's Firewheel
Best for: Nicer sit-down without special-occasion price
American comfort food in a slightly more polished Firewheel Town Center setting, with good ambiance and reasonable noise levels. A solid option when you want a proper meal but not a price tag that requires advance planning.
Buffalo Wild Wings
Best for: Sports night with older kids
Wings, big screens, and a kids' menu near Firewheel Town Center. The kind of place where dinner can stretch into two hours while the game plays. A sports-family staple for Friday or Saturday nights.
Chuck E. Cheese Firewheel
Best for: All-in-one birthday or rainy day
Scratch-made pizza plus arcade games and the Adventure Zone indoor playground. The Firewheel location is a full-package destination for ages 2 through 10 when you need food and entertainment under one roof.
Harbor Bay Marina
Best for: Lake view casual lunch different
Casual dining with marina views along Lake Ray Hubbard, offering a waterfront atmosphere that feels genuinely different from Garland's inland dining options. A good weekend lunch stop before or after time at the lake.

The Firewheel Town Center area at President George Bush Turnpike and Firewheel Pkwy is Garland's main dining hub, with 30+ restaurants within walking distance. The Lake Ray Hubbard shoreline adds a scenic waterfront dining corridor about 5 minutes east.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Garland's retail options span from the open-air Firewheel Town Center lifestyle center to the historic Downtown Square and the unique destination retail of Bass Pro Shops at Lake Ray Hubbard. Each serves a different kind of family shopping trip.

Firewheel Town Center

Lifestyle Center

The premier outdoor shopping, dining, and entertainment destination for northeast Dallas, with 100+ stores, an AMC theater, and a full dining corridor in an open-air format. Anchor stores plus local and national retailers with good walkability between stops.

Best for: A full Saturday family outing: shop in the morning, catch a matinee, and pick from 30+ restaurants for dinner without moving the car.

Bass Pro Shops at Lake Ray Hubbard

Destination Retail

Over 120,000 sq ft of outdoor gear, fishing equipment, camping supplies, and boating accessories on the lakefront. An arcade, restaurants, and indoor aquariums make it a destination for kids even if nobody is buying anything. A genuine family outing in a retail format.

Best for: A Saturday morning exploration with kids who love animals, fishing, or camping; combine with time at the lake for a full day.

Downtown Garland Square

Historic Downtown

Garland's historic downtown square with local boutiques, antique shops, and dining alongside the community events calendar that keeps the square active year-round. A more personal, locally-owned alternative to the Firewheel lifestyle center experience.

Best for: Weekend browsing with older kids or teens, especially when a free Music Made Here concert is happening in the evening.

Annual Events & Community Calendar

Downtown Garland Square is one of the most consistently active free-event venues in suburban Dallas, with the city hosting monthly concerts, cultural celebrations, and seasonal festivals throughout the year. Most events are free and family-oriented.

Year-Round (First Fridays)

Music Made Here Concert Series

Free outdoor concerts on the first Friday of every month at Downtown Garland Square. Most concerts are free to attend, with food and drink vendors on-site. A consistent family event that gives Garland residents something to plan around every single month of the year.

Spring

Asian American Heritage Festival

Garland's annual celebration of Asian-Pacific American communities at Downtown Square, with cultural performances, the Colorful World Parade, lion dances, craft stalls, food vendors, and art displays. Reflects Garland's genuinely diverse community and is one of the strongest multicultural events in suburban Dallas.

June 20

Garland Juneteenth Celebration

An annual Juneteenth event held on June 20th with live entertainment, local food, a children's activity area, and a meaningful commemoration of emancipation. Held annually at Downtown Garland Square with free admission and programming suited for families with children of all ages.

July 3

Red, White and You!

Garland's Independence Day celebration on July 3rd with live music, food, games, and a fireworks show. Held annually the evening before the 4th, giving families a full fireworks experience without the July 4th traffic and crowds. One of the most attended annual events in the city.

September

Garland Fall Fest

An annual fall festival at Downtown Square featuring live music, a kids' zone, pumpkin patch photo-ops, pumpkin bowling, festive food, and autumn activities. A community favorite for families marking the start of fall season. Held annually in September at Downtown Garland Square.

October

Dia de los Muertos

A free celebration honoring Mexican cultural traditions at Downtown Garland Square, with live performances, altars, food trucks, an artisan market, and community participation. One of Garland's signature multicultural events, reflecting the city's strong Latino community.

December

Christmas on the Square

Garland's annual Christmas celebration with tubing, snowman building, train rides, food and craft vendors, live entertainment, and photos with Santa at Downtown Square. A full family holiday experience that draws Garland families back year after year.

Indoor Activities for Kids

When Garland's summers push past 100 degrees, families have solid indoor options from an adventure park and bowling venues to the Holford Aquatic Center's climate-controlled pool and the public library system's year-round programming.

Urban Air Adventure Park

Type: Adventure Park · Ages: All ages
Located at 3046 Lavon Dr in Garland, this is more than a trampoline park: Warrior Course obstacles, a Tubes Playground with slides, interconnected trampolines, dodgeball, and open jump. A solid half-day for kids ages 4 and older on hot summer days.

Chuck E. Cheese Firewheel

Type: Family Entertainment · Ages: Best for ages 2-10
The Adventure Zone indoor playground with trampolines, slides, and superhero-style obstacles for kids under 56", plus 50+ arcade games and scratch-made pizza. A reliable birthday party and rainy-day venue at Firewheel Town Center.

Holford Aquatic Center

Type: Indoor/Outdoor Aquatics · Ages: All ages
Opened in 2025 with two water slides, a current channel, lap lanes, a tot pool, and an activity pool. A significant upgrade to Garland's aquatics infrastructure and the best year-round water option for families. Check garlandtx.gov for hours and admission.

AMC Firewheel 18

Type: Movie Theater · Ages: All ages
An 18-screen AMC theater inside Firewheel Town Center, with recliner seating and a full concession setup. Good for family movie nights year-round, and convenient to combine with dinner at one of the adjacent Firewheel restaurants.

Bass Pro Shops Indoor Aquariums and Arcade

Type: Destination Retail / Indoor Activity · Ages: All ages
The indoor saltwater aquariums and freshwater tanks at Bass Pro Shops on Lake Ray Hubbard function as a free mini-aquarium experience for kids. Add the in-store arcade and onsite restaurant and it becomes a legitimate indoor outing for families.

Garland Public Library System

Type: Public Library · Ages: All ages
Multiple branch locations across Garland with children's story times, summer reading programs, and digital resources. Free and air-conditioned, with year-round programming calendars. A good first-stop resource for new families getting oriented in the community.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

When Garland temperatures peak in July and August, local families rotate between the Holford Aquatic Center in the morning before lines build, Urban Air Adventure Park for a 2-3 hour mid-day energy burn, and the free city spray parks (open through October 5 at Embree, Wynne, Watson, and Downtown Square) for quick afternoon cool-downs with toddlers. Bass Pro Shops functions as a free indoor outing when you need somewhere to go without spending money. The Garland Public Library has summer programs that give kids structured, air-conditioned time when parents need a break from entertainment-planning.
