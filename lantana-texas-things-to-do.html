What Lantana Families Actually Do Here

Lantana is a master-planned community in unincorporated Denton County, and family life revolves around what the neighborhood owns: five pools, two fitness centers, lighted tennis and pickleball courts, and miles of tree-shaded greenbelt trails that connect almost every street. When families want a bigger outing, Flower Mound shopping, Lake Lewisville parks, and Denton are all a short drive away. See the full Lantana relocation guide →

Parks, Trails & Green Space

Lantana doesn't have a public parks department. It's a master-planned community where the green space is the neighborhood itself: two amenity centers, five pools, playgrounds, and miles of tree-shaded greenbelt trails maintained by the HOA. For lake days and bigger outings, three Lake Lewisville parks sit within about 15 minutes.

North Amenity Center

The larger swim-lane pool (home of the Lantana Lightning swim team), an 18-foot kiddie pool, a full weight-and-cardio fitness center, and a playground. Shaded deck and restrooms on site. Best for swim-team families and lap swimmers.

Pool Fitness Center Playground

South Amenity Center

A water spray park with moveable features, a resort-style pool, and a cardio-focused fitness room. The zero-depth spray play makes it the go-to for under-5s. Restrooms on site, shaded seating nearby.

Splash Pad Pool Fitness

Lantana Greenbelt Trails

Miles of paved, tree-covered hike-and-bike trails thread through nearly every neighborhood and connect homes to the pools, schools, and parks. Shaded and stroller-friendly. Best for after-school bike rides and morning walks.

Trails Stroller-Friendly Shade

Lantana Sport Complex

Open fields used for the community's youth clinics and practices, plus playground space. Flat, easy parking. Best for younger kids' soccer, flag football, and tee-ball seasons.

Fields Sports Playground

Pilot Knoll Park (Highland Village)

About 12 minutes south on Lake Lewisville: a playground, boat ramp, kayak launch, nature and equestrian trails, sand volleyball, and a Little Free Library. Bring sun cover, the bluff is open. Best for lake days with older kids.

Lake Access Trails Playground

Copperas Branch Park (Highland Village)

A Lake Lewisville park with a swimming beach, picnic areas, and shade beneath the bridge, about 12 minutes away. Best for a low-key beach afternoon when you don't want the drive to a bigger lake.

Lake Access Swim Beach Picnic

LLELA Nature Preserve

The Lewisville Lake Environmental Learning Area, about 20 minutes away, is 2,600 acres of bottomland forest, prairie, and river trails with bison and bird-watching. Best for a half-day nature outing with school-age kids.

Nature Preserve Trails Wildlife

Trail System Highlight

The Lantana greenbelt trail network weaves tree-shaded paths through nearly every neighborhood, linking homes to the amenity centers, schools, and pools without crossing a major road. Great for stroller walks and after-school bike rides.

Relocation Tip

Start at whichever amenity center is closest to the home you're considering, then walk the greenbelt to the other one. The North and South centers have different pools and fitness setups, and the trail between them shows you how connected the neighborhood really is. For a lake day, Pilot Knoll Park in Highland Village is about 12 minutes south.

Youth Sports Leagues & Facilities

Because Lantana is unincorporated, youth sports run through community programs and nearby league hubs rather than a city rec department. Younger kids start right in the neighborhood with i9 and N Zone clinics, while the Lantana Lightning swim team is the community's signature program. Older athletes usually play in Flower Mound, Argyle, or Denton leagues a short drive away.

Swimming

League: Lantana Lightning Swim Team (Swim North Texas)
Ages: school-age, must swim 25 yards unassisted
Season: Summer, home meets at the North Amenity Center

Instructional Multi-Sport

League: i9 Sports (Argyle / Flower Mound / Lantana clinic at E.P. Rayzor Elementary)
Ages: 3 to 12
Season: Fall & spring, soccer, flag football, basketball, tee ball

Youth Soccer & Rec Sports

League: N Zone Sports (Lantana Sport Complex & Bert Winters Park)
Ages: 3 to 16
Season: Year-round seasons, soccer, flag football, basketball, tee ball

Baseball & Softball

League: Flower Mound Baseball Association & nearby Argyle leagues (about 15 minutes)
Ages: 4 to 14
Season: Spring & fall

Soccer (Older Kids)

League: Flower Mound Soccer Association (about 15 minutes)
Ages: 4 to 18
Season: Fall & spring

Tennis & Pickleball

League: Lantana Community Association courts (four lighted tennis courts, a stadium court, and pickleball courts)
Ages: All
Season: Year-round clinics and open play for residents

Where Games Are Played

The Lantana Sport Complex and the two amenity centers host most in-neighborhood practices and the Lantana Lightning swim program. For tournament-level fields and courts, families head to Flower Mound's Bakersfield and Gerault athletic complexes, about 15 minutes south.

Family Entertainment & Attractions

Lantana keeps daily fun close to home, but some of DFW's best family attractions are a quick drive. Here's what's nearby.

Marty B's

Live Music & Eats · Bartonville's sprawling patio with a stage, lawn games, and a kid-friendly weekend crowd, about 5 minutes from Lantana.

Lake Lewisville Recreation

Lake Recreation · Kayaking, paddleboarding, fishing, and beaches at Pilot Knoll and Copperas Branch parks, about 12 minutes from home.

SEA LIFE Grapevine

Aquarium · A walk-through ocean tunnel, touch pools, and a stingray bay at Grapevine Mills, about 25 minutes away.

LEGOLAND Discovery Center

Indoor Attraction · Build zones, rides, and a 4D cinema at Grapevine Mills, best for ages 3 to 10, about 25 minutes away.

Alamo Drafthouse Denton

Dine-In Cinema · First-run movies with food and drink delivered to your seat plus family matinees, about 15 minutes north.

Day Trip from Lantana

SEA LIFE Grapevine and LEGOLAND Discovery Center (about 25 minutes), two big indoor attractions under one roof at Grapevine Mills, are the perfect escape on a hot or rainy day, with the outlet mall's shops and theater steps away.

Family-Friendly Dining

Lantana's dining sits just outside the gates along the FM 407 corridor through Bartonville and Argyle, a surprisingly good cluster of patios, BBQ, and breakfast spots.

Shopping & Retail Districts

Everyday shopping is built into the community, and bigger retail outings are a short drive in any direction.

Lantana Town Center

Everyday

Anchored by a 120,000-square-foot Kroger Marketplace at FM 407 and Jeter Road, with Starbucks, a pharmacy, a fuel center, and everyday shops.
Best for: weekly groceries and quick errands without leaving the neighborhood.

The Shops at Highland Village

Lifestyle Center

An open-air center about 12 minutes away with shopping, restaurants, a cinema, and a fountain plaza that hosts family events.
Best for: a full Saturday of shopping plus dinner and a movie.

Grapevine Mills

Outlets & Indoor

A massive indoor outlet mall about 25 minutes away with SEA LIFE, LEGOLAND, a theater, and a food court.
Best for: a rainy-day, all-day outing that mixes shopping and attractions.

Historic Downtown Denton Square

Historic Main Street

Independent shops, record stores, and cafes around the courthouse square, about 15 minutes north.
Best for: an afternoon of browsing with older kids and teens.

Indoor Activities for Kids

When the Texas heat or a rainy afternoon sends everyone inside, Lantana families have a strong set of indoor options within a 15-to-25-minute drive.

Urban Air Adventure Park

Type: Trampoline & Adventure · Ages: 3+
Trampolines, an indoor coaster, ropes course, and climbing walls. The closest location is a short drive in the Flower Mound area.

Denton Public Library, South Branch

Type: Library · Ages: All
Story times, summer reading, and maker programs about 10 minutes away on Teasley Lane, the closest branch to Lantana.

SEA LIFE Grapevine

Type: Aquarium · Ages: All
An all-weather indoor aquarium with an ocean tunnel and touch pools at Grapevine Mills, about 25 minutes away.

LEGOLAND Discovery Center

Type: Indoor Play · Ages: 3 to 10
Build zones, gentle rides, and a 4D cinema at Grapevine Mills, ideal for younger kids on a hot day.

Lantana Amenity Center Fitness Rooms

Type: Fitness Centers · Ages: Teens & adults
Two indoor gyms with Cybex and Precor equipment, an easy in-neighborhood option when it's too hot to be outside.

Alamo Drafthouse Denton

Type: Dine-In Cinema · Ages: All
Family matinees and first-run films with food delivered to your seat, about 15 minutes north.

Summer Heat Pro Tip

From late May through September, Lantana families basically live at the amenity-center pools. The water spray park at the South center is the toddler favorite, while the North lap pool keeps older kids busy with the Lantana Lightning swim team. When it tops 100, swap the afternoon for SEA LIFE Grapevine or an air-conditioned story time at the Denton library.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from families considering Lantana.

  • Yes. Lantana is one of Denton County's premier master-planned communities, with low crime, top-rated Denton ISD schools, and family infrastructure built right into the neighborhood: five pools, two fitness centers, tennis and pickleball courts, miles of greenbelt trails, and a calendar full of community events. For most families the appeal is simple, the pools, trails, and friends are a short walk from the front door.
  • Most of Lantana is zoned to Denton ISD, with E.P. Rayzor and Adkins elementary schools, Harpool Middle School, and Guyer High School, which has been recognized on the U.S. News list of top high schools. A small section on the western edge of the community feeds Argyle ISD, so confirm the exact zoning for any specific home before you fall in love with it.
  • In-neighborhood options include the Lantana Lightning swim team, plus i9 Sports and N Zone clinics that run soccer, flag football, basketball, and tee ball for ages 3 to 16. Older athletes usually join Flower Mound or Argyle leagues, such as the Flower Mound Soccer Association and Flower Mound Baseball Association, all within about 15 minutes.
  • Plenty within a 15-to-25-minute drive: SEA LIFE Grapevine, LEGOLAND Discovery Center, Urban Air, the Denton Public Library, and the Alamo Drafthouse cinema. Closer to home, the two amenity-center fitness rooms stay open year-round for active teens and parents.
  • Lantana's South Amenity Center has a water spray park with moveable features, and the community's pool system includes two splash areas for younger kids. For open-water play, Pilot Knoll and Copperas Branch parks on Lake Lewisville are about 12 minutes away.
  • Lantana is smaller and amenity-focused: the pools, courts, and trails come with the neighborhood, and almost everything is walkable. Flower Mound and Highland Village offer larger public park systems and direct Lake Lewisville access, and both are only 10 to 15 minutes away, so many Lantana families end up using all three.
  • Lantana is known as one of Denton County's premier master-planned communities, recognized repeatedly as a Community of the Year. It's defined by resort-style amenities, five pools, two fitness centers, tennis and pickleball courts, and miles of greenbelt trails, a private golf club, strong Denton ISD schools, and a tight-knit, event-filled community feel.
